How to read the pool

These are design hypotheses, not rules. Each card is written as a standalone atomic action. A card labelled Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court uses that named Operation family; a card labelled Unbound resolves only its own text and invokes none of the four baselines. Unless the text says “may” or “up to,” every part must be possible for the card to be legal. “Acting party” means the open party being operated, as in Ruleset 21.

Balance warning: none of these effects has been costed or playtested. The pool deliberately ranges from conservative ideas to sharp, situational effects so it can be cut down further before testing. It does not change the current two-card-per-party component limit.

This audit removed 74 of the original 120 candidates. A candidate was culled when it produced the same board change as a current card or another candidate and differed only by a legality restriction, route phrasing, or renamed target. Current cards always took precedence. When only candidates collided, the version kept was the strongest fit for its party. Effects with materially different token ownership, Operation baseline, quantity, or final board state remain.

The 46 curated family candidates stay on the map or at Court. The 60 unbound experiments deliberately range into piles, private cards, Firm markers, Closure, turn order, points, and Election state. Their extra timing and ownership interactions are open questions if any survive curation.
