Index/Bonus card candidates

A curated pool of possible collectible Bonus cards for Bellweather. Each party’s two current Ruleset 21 cards appear first, followed by its remaining non-duplicate candidates for comparison.

Exploration

How to read the pool

These are design hypotheses, not rules. Each card is written as a standalone atomic action using its named Operation family. Unless the text says “may” or “up to,” every part must be possible for the card to be legal. “Acting party” means the open party being operated, as in Ruleset 21.

Balance warning: none of these effects has been costed or playtested. The pool deliberately ranges from conservative ideas to sharp, situational effects so it can be cut down further before testing. It does not change the current two-card-per-party component limit.

This audit removed 74 of the original 120 candidates. A candidate was culled when it produced the same board change as a current card or another candidate and differed only by a legality restriction, route phrasing, or renamed target. Current cards always took precedence. When only candidates collided, the version kept was the strongest fit for its party. Effects with materially different token ownership, Operation baseline, quantity, or final board state remain.

The remaining effects stay on the map or at Court; they do not change Collection, Closure, card ownership, Election scoring, or year timing.

Bees · coordinated movement, shared districts, and deliberately built alliances.

Resolve Organise. Then add another acting-party Support to a free spot in the destination district.

  3. Organise

  8. Court
