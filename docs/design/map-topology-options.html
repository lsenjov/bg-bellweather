Density held constant

Every option has eighteen connections, matching the live map. Changes in reach, redundancy, and chokepoints therefore come from placement rather than from adding more adjacency.

Asymmetry is intentional

The four alternatives after Capital Corridors deliberately concentrate shortcuts, high degree, or isolation on one side. They are stress tests, not attempts at geographical balance.

Scoring cards need revision

The live deck requires each card’s three districts to be pairwise non-neighboring. Every option marks the current cards that violate that rule under its new connections; selecting a map means replacing or re-registering those cards.
