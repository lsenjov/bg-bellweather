Three regions.
Three possible maps.

Each region holds 18 Support and elects up to 9 votes. Elections still resolve district by district. Each scoring card has three region–party objectives; the exact deck assignments remain open.

Concept studies, not adopted rules. Bellweather Centre shares a real border with every region in all three maps. Its current three Support spaces and separate scoring are retained here as a prototype assumption.

The same 15 regional districts appear in every study. Names and boundaries are provisional. Circles show available Support spaces, not starting pieces. Only shared border segments create adjacency; corner contact does not.

Connected regions, unequal land areas. The three-city region stays together. Its cities occupy compact territories, while low-capacity countryside spreads much further. Northgate, the mixed region’s city, is separated from that cluster by intervening districts. All districts and regions remain connected.

Compact hub

A compact three-city cluster occupies the northwest. The mixed region stretches east to a distant city, above broad southern countryside.

Play hypothesis. The mixed city, Northgate, is separated from the urban cities by suburbs and low-capacity districts. Players can approach along the northern region or through the southern countryside.

Watch in playtesting. The broad southern districts offer shortcuts across the map. Test whether these routes invite interference or make the northern contests easy to bypass.

Centre neighbors: Grand Market, Ironwood, Canal Ward, Westfield. Total shared district borders: 29.

Rural fringe

Three small northern cities sit beside the Centre. A connected mixed region follows the eastern edge to Northgate in the far southeast; countryside wraps around the west and south.

Play hypothesis. Northgate sits at the far end of the mixed region, beside rural Vale. Reaching it from the urban cluster requires crossing several districts, with a choice of eastern and rural approaches.

Watch in playtesting. Northgate has only two neighbors. Its remoteness could make it a defensible destination or leave it too quiet.

Centre neighbors: Grand Market, Ironwood, Canal Ward, Eastfield. Total shared district borders: 29.

City corridor

A small southwestern city cluster anchors a southern mixed corridor. Northgate lies at the opposite end, while scattered suburbs punctuate a much larger northern rural region.

Play hypothesis. The two outlying suburbs, Westfield and Eastfield, share no border. Moving between those denser footholds requires crossing lower-capacity territory.

Watch in playtesting. Northgate has only one neighboring district, Meadow. That gateway could become a useful contest or shelter the city too effectively.

Centre neighbors: Grand Market, Canal Ward, Heath. Total shared district borders: 30.

What to compare

Compare three approaches to the distant mixed city: several neighboring approaches in the compact hub, two entry districts in the rural fringe, and a single gateway in the city corridor. The question is whether intervening districts create contested journeys or simply shelter a remote scoring opportunity.

All three studies have more shared borders than the current map's 18. That is an exploratory topology choice: extra routes might encourage interference, but could also make it easier to bypass opponents.
