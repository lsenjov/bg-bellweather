Concept studies, not adopted rules. Bellweather port allows only urban districts to touch the Centre. Bellweather port

The city spreads in a 270-degree fan around an open harbour. Bellweather sits at the inner waterfront, with three compact urban districts around it. Mixed districts follow the right shoreline; outlying districts wrap around the left side and inland edge.

Play hypothesis. Urban districts provide the only access to Capital scoring. Travel between the opposite shores goes around the port, with a choice of urban routes and the outer districts. Mixed and outlying share land borders, so their interaction does not require passing through Bellweather.

Watch in playtesting. Canal Ward has seven neighbors and Grand Market has six. Their central positions may concentrate influence despite the alternative routes. Northgate remains distant from the urban cluster, with access through Crown Road and Meadow.

Centre neighbors: Harbormouth, Grand Market, Ironwood. All three regions are connected and share land borders pairwise. The 33 district connections have no dead ends, sole gateway districts, or sole gateway connections. Each region retains 18 Support / up to 9 votes, elected district by district; the Centre retains 3 separate Support spaces.

Water rule for the prototypes: districts separated by water are not adjacent for any effect, including movement, Smear, and bonuses that require adjacency. Physical proximity across the harbour creates no connection.

Outer country: river and lake

Outlying districts wrap around the northern, western, and southern edges. Compact cities sit inland; Bellweather Centre borders only the urban and mixed regions.

Play hypothesis. The river blocks Canal Ward–Orchard. From Canal Ward, reach Orchard by heading south through Crown Road and its crossing, or north through Westfield, the northern crossing to Heath, and then Orchard. The river then turns east along Coast, blocking Orchard–Coast. Orchard can reach Coast via Meadow’s bridge or via Crown Road’s open land border. Market Lake also blocks Eastfield–Meadow. Northgate keeps approaches from Orchard and Meadow.

Watch in playtesting. The river removes two district connections and the lake removes one. The northern route from Canal Ward to Orchard takes three moves; the southern route takes two. Test whether existing Support and rival positions make the longer rural approach worth using. Bridge position adds no movement inside a district.

Proposed movement for this study: water cannot be crossed except at marked crossings. Crossings make their two districts adjacent; they add no separate spaces, costs, or control mechanism. Other exposed shared land borders remain passable. This proposal has not been adopted into the game rules.

Outlying coverage: 48 of 56 equal perimeter units (85.7%). Centre neighbors: Grand Market, Ironwood, Crown Road. There are 28 passable district connections after water blocks three of the 31 underlying land borders. Each district has at least two neighbors and lies on a loop; no single district or connection is the sole route joining parts of the map. Each region retains 18 Support and up to 9 votes, elected district by district.

Compact hub

A compact three-city cluster occupies the northwest. The mixed region stretches east to a distant city, above broad southern countryside.

Play hypothesis. The mixed city, Northgate, is separated from the urban cities by suburbs and low-capacity districts. Players can approach along the northern region or through the southern countryside.

Watch in playtesting. The broad southern districts offer shortcuts across the map. Test whether these routes invite interference or make the northern contests easy to bypass.

Centre neighbors: Grand Market, Ironwood, Canal Ward, Westfield. Total shared district borders: 29.

Rural fringe

Three small northern cities sit beside the Centre. A connected mixed region follows the eastern edge to Northgate in the far southeast; countryside wraps around the west and south.

Play hypothesis. Northgate sits at the far end of the mixed region, beside rural Vale. Reaching it from the urban cluster requires crossing several districts, with a choice of eastern and rural approaches.

Watch in playtesting. Northgate has only two neighbors. Its remoteness could make it a defensible destination or leave it too quiet.

Centre neighbors: Grand Market, Ironwood, Canal Ward, Eastfield. Total shared district borders: 29.

City corridor

A small southwestern city cluster anchors a southern mixed corridor. Northgate lies at the opposite end, while scattered suburbs punctuate a much larger northern rural region.

Play hypothesis. The two outlying suburbs, Westfield and Eastfield, share no border. Moving between those denser footholds requires crossing lower-capacity territory.

Watch in playtesting. Northgate has only one neighboring district, Meadow. That gateway could become a useful contest or shelter the city too effectively.

Centre neighbors: Grand Market, Canal Ward, Heath. Total shared district borders: 30.

What to compare

Bellweather port tests urban-only access to the Centre and travel around an open harbour. Compare whether the inland mixed–outlying borders create useful alternatives to the urban routes.

Outer country tests movement through other regions, with a river and lake shaping the approaches. Its edge districts can be small on the board while implying a much larger rural landscape beyond it.

Compare three approaches to the distant mixed city: several neighboring approaches in the compact hub, two entry districts in the rural fringe, and a single gateway in the city corridor. The question is whether intervening districts create contested journeys or simply shelter a remote scoring opportunity.

All five studies have more district connections than the current map's 18. That is an exploratory topology choice: extra routes might encourage interference, but could also make it easier to bypass opponents.
