Archived initial queue / draft 00
Priority Question Why it matters Smallest useful test
01 What does the ladder represent? Position could be progress, turn order, price, status, or eligibility; each creates a different game. Sketch one loop for each interpretation using the same six-space track.
02 What are players bidding to gain or change? The prize determines whether bids express valuation, timing, denial, or prediction. Run five contests with one recurring prize and no scoring layer.
03 What does a bid cost? Reusable, spent, and future-punishing bids produce very different incentives. Compare finite coins with a bid that moves a player backward.
04 How are bids made and revealed? Open, sealed, simultaneous, and once-around bidding create different information games. Play the same contest three times using open, sealed, and once-around bids.
05 Why does a player want to climb? The ladder needs an intrinsic incentive or a clear relationship to winning. Test immediate privileges against end-game position scoring.
06 Can players fall, block, pass, or share a rung? Movement constraints define the game’s sharpest interaction. Prototype exclusive rungs first, then allow sharing and compare.
07 What happens to a losing bidder? Repeated non-results can create runaway leaders or disengagement. Compare keeping the bid, gaining priority, and moving on another track.
08 What ends the game? The end trigger determines whether climbing is a race, a timer, or a positional contest. Test reaching the top against a fixed number of auctions.
09 What theme makes these incentives intuitive? Theme should explain the loop after the loop proves interesting. Retheme one proven prototype, not the whole possibility space.

Current mechanic questions

Status Question Why it matters Smallest useful test
Playtest watch Do the twelve party bonuses remain distinct and worth first refusal without overshadowing their baselines? Every operation has three party expressions, but global movement, conversion, double effects, persistent protection, and delayed actions may differ sharply in practical value. Record every claimed, declined, and unusable bonus, the bid rank that received it, and whether the baseline alone would have justified the operation token.
Playtest watch Do hidden district–party objectives and partial Support draws reward deliberate map play? Each card can still score at most six base points, but a coalition broadens which parties’ Support matches while shared random draws may either create useful election uncertainty or conceal the effect of twelve Campaign Rounds of Support movement. At each Election Day, record every objective’s own-party and coalition-partner Support before the draw, points actually drawn, and whether its holder knowingly influenced that district, party, or coalition.
Playtest watch Are twelve Campaign Rounds per Election Day and three Election Days per game the right clock? The fixed 12/3 structure currently produces thirty-six complete Campaign Rounds regardless of player count, contest count, or insolvency. Record elapsed time and meaningful board changes in each twelve-round period, then ask whether each Election Day arrived too early, too late, or at a useful turning point.
Open rule How are ties for the highest cumulative score broken? The first scoring prototype defines a winner but can produce equal final totals, including negative totals. Record all final ties before testing a tie-breaker; prefer a visible board state over retained Clout or operation tokens.
Playtest watch Does reciprocal coalition scoring make Court actions and overtures strategically valuable without overpowering district control? Each drawn Support belonging to either coalition partner matches objectives for both, so a coalition can broaden scoring immediately before Election Day. Record every coalition at each Election Day, the points each partner’s Support contributed to the other, and which Court actions formed or broke those coalitions.
Open component Do the sleeved scoring cards need printed backs? The 40 × 61 mm fronts fit the selected sleeves, but clear sleeves and thin paper may expose hidden agendas without a common back or opaque insert. Test the front-only cards in the intended 41 × 63 mm sleeves under ordinary table lighting before designing backs.
Playtest watch Does the sixteen-district map make both outer-ring distance and Crownwater causeway movement matter? The three large outer districts should attract Support while the low-capacity causeways provide alternate routes between sectors; either structure could dominate and flatten spatial choices. Track every Organise origin and destination, whether its shortest route uses a causeway, and the free spots remaining at each capacity after every contest.
Playtest watch Do the long party boards keep bids, bonuses, and overtures readable without consuming too much table space? The boards combine the contest target with persistent state, but their opening-bid areas must fit the 50 mm opening markers and six boards occupy more space than one central track. Print all three sheets at actual size, use the intended covers and markers, and run one contest at every party on an ordinary table.
Open component How much Support begins on the map, where does it begin, and how much remains in each party reserve? The map has fifty-seven spots, but starting density and distribution determine whether Rally has room, Smear creates valuable vacancies, and supportless recovery occurs. Choose an equal Support supply for all six parties, seed the map rotationally, and record free spots by 6-, 4-, 3-, and 2-capacity districts after every contest.
Playtest watch Does the 4 Organise / 4 Rally / 4 Smear / 2 Court supply create the right operation economy? Court remains half as common as each spatial family, making overture changes scarcer than spatial actions, while every token can concentrate in another player’s reserve through transfers. Record each token’s bids and transfers, every round in which a family is unavailable to a player, and all reserves containing three or more tokens of one family.
Playtest watch Does having no Support create a useful recovery state or a desirable global-Smear strategy? A supportless party may Smear anywhere until it re-enters through Organise or Rally, so absence may provide offensive flexibility rather than merely prevent elimination. Record every voluntary or forced loss of final Support, all global Smears, and how soon the party re-enters.
Playtest watch Do limited district spots create tactical vacancies or frequent failed actions? Organise and Rally require free spots while Smear creates them, but a crowded map may make operation legality too brittle. Record each failed spatial operation, each contested vacancy, and the number of free spots after every contest.
Playtest watch Do legal last-second counterbid moves improve bluffing or merely prevent informed reactions? The timed phase deliberately permits sniping, but it may reward speed, vigilance, or reach more than judgment. Record every move made in the final five seconds, when each player stops revising, and whether opponents felt they had a meaningful response.
Playtest watch Do uncapped operation token bundles create worthwhile risk or overwhelming action bursts? Operation token supply is the only limit, so one bid may produce a long resolution before transferring a highly concentrated asset. Record the largest bundle, its resolution time, and whether another player would willingly make the same wager.
Playtest watch Does placing an additional bid on one’s own influence contest create varied tactics or compulsory insurance? An opener can occupy several ranks and may be able to route valuable opening resources back to themselves. Track how often players self-bid, whether the choice protects value, and what they gave up elsewhere to do it.
Playtest watch Do resource trades, binding immediate deals, and nonbinding future promises deepen negotiation without overwhelming play? Behind-screen trades can redistribute the closed economy outside the Revolving Door, while component eligibility and the distinction between immediate and future terms may create disputes, social pressure, or kingmaking. Record every trade and promise, whether all traded components were behind screens, whether its terms were immediate or future, any rules dispute or broken promise, and whether quieter players could participate meaningfully.
Playtest watch Does the Revolving Door make circular transfer easier to predict without implying that a party receives the bid? The theme should clarify that lobbying firms exchange complete influence packages after every contest, even though the party was the target. Ask players to predict each transfer before moving any Clout or operation tokens and record every mistaken destination.
