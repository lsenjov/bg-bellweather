Open questions

Questions for testing the replacement yearly loop.

Index/Open questions

Ruleset 30 is the current core ruleset. These questions identify balance and pacing choices requiring tabletop evidence; they do not introduce extra rules. See the rulebook for any explicitly unresolved interactions. The app update is deferred. The printable component prototype is ready for physical fit and readability tests.

Current queue / Ruleset 30
PriorityQuestionWhy it mattersSmallest useful test
AgentsDoes positioning create useful choices rather than repeated recovery work?Common Operations now require agent presence, while party cards bypass location requirements.Record turns spent only repositioning, inaccessible Operations, and any state with no legal action. Do not add a Pass exception during testing.
FiddlerIs the opening favour burst too strong?Up to six favours resolve before regular Lobby turns; the initial burst uses three.Track Operations versus movement from favours, early vote swings, and whether opponents can respond effectively.
DoppelgängersDoes copying the first pile-top Operation per party reward positioning without excessive output?Both agents need legal operations for the same acting party; additional mirrors require different parties in the same turn.Record usable and missed copies, mirrors per turn, copied Operation types, and whether Organise dominates. Test low and high player counts.
HecateDoes the prophecy economy pay at a useful rate?Reward Operations avoid shared party piles, while prophecies require the third card slot.Track predictions placed, fulfilled, withdrawn and failed; rewards lost to an empty supply; movement rewards and chains.
WitchDo familiar steps and teleport cards provide enough access?Each familiar enables a different Operation; the witch connects their positions.Record unavailable Operation types, teleport use, and useful Organise transport of the group.
PuppeteerIs mandatory relocation a planning puzzle or repetitive overhead?Every common Operation replaces its authorising marionette at the puppeteer’s departure district.Track productive trails, stranded marionettes, and use of personal movement cards.
GalleryDoes portrait adjacency invite use without excessive toll income?Three awake portraits connect districts for all adjacency effects; opponents pay common or party cards.Track paid connections, avoided connections, toll card types, and changes caused by the sleep/wake cycle.
Shared economyDoes asymmetric play preserve collection tension?Personal abilities can alter card circulation and action efficiency.Record pile sizes, common/party cards earned, collection counters unused, and distribution of final hand-rank points.
Year timingDo Elections before New Year release resolve clearly?Closure prophecies resolve before Elections; Election prophecies return for the next midterm year.Walk through automatic Closures in firm order, an Election with Hecate predictions, and final scoring after card release.
Low player countsDo fixed personal kits work with doubled common hands and openings?Each human has one identity and its usual agents, firm cards and reward supply.Compare movement availability, turns per year, and firm strengths at two, three and higher player counts.
PoliciesDo Party laws combine clearly with agents and repeated Operations?Agent checks apply to baseline Operations; law extras need no extra presence and trigger once per card.Exercise doubled Operations, portrait routes, party cards, and the Fiddler’s favour actions under laws.
Party cardsAre the existing eighteen party effects balanced with remote access?Party cards ignore agent locations but the Gallery must select an awake portrait and cycle it afterwards.Track remote plays, repeat collection, and effects that dominate local common Operations.
PacingDo six years still provide enough development and interaction?Agent movement, prophecies and favours add decisions to the existing yearly loop.Time complete games and note unused personal cards, unreachable plans and long reactive sequences.
TitleShould the working title change?Ladder Bidding no longer describes the game’s mechanics.Use playtest descriptions of the game’s strongest moments before selecting a title.
