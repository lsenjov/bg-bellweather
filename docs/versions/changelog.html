Unversioned draft

  • 2026-07-28: Set the game in the fictional Republic of Bellwether, with rival lobbying houses influencing animal political parties. Added six party targets—Honeycomb Cooperative, Old Shell Union, Foxglove League, Riverworks Party, Many Wings Coalition, and Night Parliament—covering every unique pair of Campaign, Expansion, Platform, and Coalition operations. Themed reusable currency as Clout, command cards as operatives, the Priority Order as the media Pecking Order at the Press Perch, and circular transfer as the Revolving Door. Party effects, operative quantities, constituencies, Election Day, government formation, scoring, and the end trigger remain open.
  • 2026-07-28: Added the first auction core and selected four players with 20 currency each, represented in denominations of 1 and 5, identical category-based starting command sets, a fixed starting Priority Order, and a 90-second counterbid timer for the first prototype. At two or three players, each player opens up to two funded auctions and receives four additional bids; at four or more, each opens up to one funded auction and receives two. The core uses a fully closed economy with no automatic between-round resource changes or direct reserve scoring, concealed currency and command reserves, physical bid escrow, mandatory public opening bids when affordable, a full recovery-bid allowance during partial insolvency, timed counterbids whose hidden contents and public locations remain revisable until expiry, withdrawal of held or ambiguous bids at the buzzer, unrestricted nonbinding negotiation, auction-local tie cancellation, simultaneous reveal, uncapped command bundles with flexible parameters at execution, circular bid transfers, a persistent player-contestable Priority Order area changed by adjacent swaps, and locked resolution when commands or targets become invalid. Updated the card and board component specifications and archived their superseded ladder-era prompts. Flagged opening-target collisions, last-second sniping, negotiation pressure, self-bid insurance, and large command bursts for further design work.
  • 2026-07-28: Created the Ladder Bidding design archive, rules shell, component prompts, and playtest workflow. No game mechanics committed.

Version naming

Begin with v0.1 when a complete loop can be set up, taught, played, and ended. Use short labels such as v0.2 for coherent playtest builds; reserve v1.0 for a stable ruleset.
