Minimal and empty counterbids can capture disproportionately large Revolving Door packages, concentrating resources and enabling repeated oversized bids. Four-agent API game: a one-Leverage bid lifted one reserve to 25 Leverage; self-insurance repeatedly returned large openings and captured neighboring packages. Medium Investigate; this affected strategy throughout one complete game. Repeat with players explicitly valuing transfer position, and compare package sizes and reserve concentration after each round.
Uncapped, non-depleting operation bundles can create deterministic district takeovers without reducing the winner's future agency. Four-agent API game: one protected ten-card Foxglove opening converted a six-capacity district and denied an adjacent space, then returned through an empty self-bid. Medium Investigate burst size and recovery cost before changing limits. Deliberately construct one large bundle per player and measure counterplay, resolution time, and subsequent reserve strength.
Relative-seat modifiers can outweigh excellent map play. The twenty-game learning league and five-player extension found that the winner had or shared the highest cumulative modifier in 18 of 25 games. In the extension only one winner had or shared the highest direct base; five distinct seats also made all four directional references different rivals. High for agent-play outcome impact; medium for human balance Investigate the modifier's magnitude and player legibility before changing it. Direct construction remained the most controllable scoring foundation. Replay matched map states with alternate seat orders, then compare with a human table that can negotiate reference denial explicitly.
Whole-pile Collection enables agency, but collection volume alone does not convert into points. The prior four-player game ended with one player holding all forty cards. Across the learning league, active denial capped the worst year-start share at 60%, while final score had effectively no linear association with cards collected. The five-player extension had a lower 56% worst snapshot and 42.8% mean game peak. Medium Keep whole-pile Collection under observation. The runaway is credible but not automatic when every player values exposed piles and Closure timing. Run human tables with visible hand counts and explicit collector-threat guidance, then compare peak share, denied piles, and scoring conversion.
Flexible Operate length produces mostly short actions, while raw card throughput is a poor proxy for effective play. The learning league and five-player extension recorded 3,523 Operate actions resolving 6,274 cards, an average of 1.78. The 1,667 / 961 / 895 one-, two-, and three-card distribution and repeated high-volume losses both favor selective conversion over throughput. Medium for agent play Keep the one-to-three-card choice. Evaluate whether human decision time or pile exposure makes the same marginal-card discipline visible. Measure Operate length, decision time, and Election points per card at two human tables.
Persistent Capital support creates meaningful spikes but often becomes a blocker or accidental gift when agendas change. Across the learning league and five-player extension, 53 of 315 player-Election results scored Capital, including nine exact triples, for 71 points. A fifth hidden pattern increased accidental-beneficiary risk: Cache's self-improving Centre move completed Orbit's winning triple in Game 21. Medium Keep the Centre exception while testing whether exact-set construction is sufficiently legible and reachable. Give players a deliberate two-Election Centre transition problem and record route length, blocker removal, and accidental opponent matches.
Players can misread directional Coalition Targets as active coalitions, causing costly strategic errors. Four-agent API game: one agent overinvested in High Pastures after Foxglove redirected, because only Old Shell still targeted Foxglove. Medium Improve the web/API derived-state presentation before changing coalition rules. Display explicit active reciprocal pairs and test whether players still make the same mistake.
Protected openings and equal counterbid cancellation are strategically important but easy to apply asymmetrically by mistake. Four-agent API game: multiple agents expected an equal counterbid to cancel an opening; the counter cancelled while the opening survived. High Clarify the revealed-bid ledger and player aid. Ask new players to predict three equal-bid reveal outcomes before play.
Whole-map Election thinning now executes consistently while scoring remains limited to revealed objectives. Ruleset-12 API game: each Election recorded all fifteen non-Centre districts, never recorded Bellweather Centre, and thinned Ironwood from six Support to three at Election 1. High for implementation; low for balance Keep the rule. Run a targeted strategic test before judging how strongly it reopens the map. Populate all three district capacities, route Support through the Centre, and compare pre- and post-Election mobility.
Losing an agent seat's bearer token or controlling process can force a complete playtest restart. The first aborted API test and the discarded setup before the ruleset-12 follow-up both stalled after a seat became unrecoverable. High Require verified credential and process persistence before start; investigate a development-only reconnect flow. Kill and restore one agent process during a disposable lobby using the proposed recovery workflow.
Facilitator pacing constraints can invalidate the resource economy and final result even when the engine completes normally. The constrained ruleset-14 game curtailed counterbid-based Revolving Door recovery, especially in rounds 9–12. Its unrestricted replacement retained active final-period counterbidding and mixed final reserves. High Never change legal player behavior to accelerate a timerless test. Wait, repair orchestration, or mark the session incomplete. Apply the same facilitation rule to future agent and human sessions.
Bellweather Centre can be both a useful route and a persistent occupied space because Elections do not thin it. Unrestricted ruleset-14 game: Foxglove moved Canal Ward → Centre → Old Quarter and scored there at Election 2; the Centre later finished full with three Many Wings Support that survived Election 3. Medium Keep the exception. Test whether deliberate Centre occupation creates interesting denial or frustrating lockout. Fill the Centre before round 9, then give two opponents agendas requiring different inner-ring routes.
Large Revolving Door packages remain sustainable and can produce lengthy late-game resolution bundles. Unrestricted ruleset-14 game: players kept counterbidding through round 12, while one final opening reached seventeen cards and was resolved in full. Medium Investigate resolution burden and counterplay without imposing facilitator caps. Measure decision count and elapsed resolution time by package size across three unrestricted games.

Interpretation rules

  • “Three players disliked it” is feedback; “three players avoided it after the first round” is behavior.
  • Do not average away strong differences between new and returning players.
  • Record delight with the same care as friction.
  • Lower confidence when sessions share the same facilitator, group, or rules explanation.
