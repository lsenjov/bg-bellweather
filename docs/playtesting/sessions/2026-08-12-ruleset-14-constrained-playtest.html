The engine completed all twelve rounds and three Elections correctly. These scores describe what happened under the imposed constraints; they are not evidence for unrestricted balance or final-period strategy.

Invalidating intervention

  1. The strategic agents needed repeated prompting while they considered counterbids and operation choices.
  2. The facilitator incorrectly prioritized wall-clock completion over preserving player agency.
  3. Counterbids were first limited to one purposeful bid, then forbidden for rounds 9–12. Opening operation bundles were also capped for pacing.
  4. Those instructions reduced denial, exact-value cancellation, Revolving Door transfers, and opportunities to recover operation families.
  5. Ember's last Organise left in a round-5 Foxglove opening transferred to Cobalt. Because later counterbidding was curtailed, Ember never recovered Organise and could not pursue the Canal Ward objective on the known final agenda.

The final-period resource distribution and final result therefore cannot be separated from facilitator interference. The correct response to a slow timerless playtest is to wait, improve autonomous orchestration between sessions, or stop and label the session incomplete—not to change legal player behavior during the game.

Valid observations retained

  • Fixed agenda slots supported deliberate multi-round routes before the pacing intervention.
  • Foxglove's Spin repeatedly combined denial, conversion, and movement; it merits unrestricted comparison.
  • A Foxglove–Many Wings reciprocal coalition formed in round 1, persisted across Elections, and contributed to scoring.
  • Night Shift correctly created a delayed post-Revolving-Door Rally decision. The API projection required the client to infer the least-occupied legal district.
  • The live projection advertised unfavoured legal operations under availableBonusOperations; the engine then rejects a bonus claim for such an operation. This is misleading API data and is independent of the pacing intervention.
  • All three Elections recorded fifteen non-Centre district draws and no Bellweather Centre draw.

Facilitation rule for the replacement test

No facilitator instruction may limit bids, operation cards, legal choices, deliberation, negotiation, or duration. Subagents may parallelize simultaneous work, but speed is an orchestration benefit only; it is never a reason to alter the game. If a player stalls, restore its ability to act or mark the session incomplete.

The unrestricted replacement game followed this rule through all twelve rounds.
