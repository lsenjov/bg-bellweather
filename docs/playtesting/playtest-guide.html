Playtest guide

Test the new loop before tuning its edges.

Index/Playtest guide

Ruleset 22 makes the strict Closure majority the sole year ending, requires every personal Firm marker to return before Pass, and lets Collect target an empty open pile. The next useful evidence is whether these constraints sharpen the Operate–Collect–Close timing without making the ending routine.

Before the test

  1. Use the complete current setup, including doubled components at two or three players.
  2. Keep each hand and New Year area covered while leaving each area’s combined ordinary-and-Bonus card count visible.
  3. Explain that Firm markers establish who receives Closure, return when their parties close, and are not bids.
  4. Explain that a player may Pass only after all of their Firm markers return, and that Pass never ends the year.
  5. Remind players that ABBA or ABCCBA applies only to Party Openings.
  6. Confirm that each standard hand contains three Organise cards, doubled to six at two or three players.
  7. Trace the three city–spoke–Centre routes and note that Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood begin full.
  8. Explain that Election 3 ranks each human player’s combined final hand; home Bonus cards and separate Firm identities do not count.
  9. Choose one primary question from the current queue and copy the session template.
Suggested first test

Collectible Bonus-card circulation

Play Years 1–2 and Election 1 without adding a midterm effect. Put all twelve Bonus cards in their party homes and use the ordinary Support setup and assigned scoring slots. Stop after the Election only if the group cannot continue; a complete six-year run is valuable once the loop is comprehensible.

Primary observation: For every party pile, note who opened it, who added cards, every Collect, who received it at Closure, and its size each time it changed hands.

During each year

  • Record the opening order and which parties are left unopened.
  • Record every Operate size, ordinary or Bonus card played, total resolution time, pauses between cards, and voluntary finish after one or two.
  • Record each Collection counter spent, including empty-pile Collections, deliberate no-card Collections, and any Collect a player wanted but could not afford.
  • Record every voluntary Closure, automatic Closure, returned Firm marker, and player whose action ended the year.
  • Mark every Pass after the player’s final Firm returns and whether they could have Operated or Collected instead.
  • Count the Lobby turns and note who loses an expected turn to a mid-orbit ending.
  • Record every optional Bonus-card award at Collect, voluntary Close, and automatic Closure, including declines and unavailable cards.
  • At Cleanup, verify that automatic Closure choices finish, remaining Firms return, all New Year cards enter private hands, Collection counters return, and Early Bird moves before any Election.

During an Election

  • Reveal only the assigned slot and identify the fixed Capital card before scoring.
  • Record each player’s base district score, relative-seat modifier when used, and Capital score separately.
  • Record which of each Capital card’s three parties are actually present in Bellweather Centre; coalition partners do not substitute.
  • Record the whole-map 3/2/1 draw and thinning, leaving the Centre untouched.
  • Clear Court Support after scoring without moving Coalition Targets.
  • At Election 3, record each human player’s ordinary cards, held Bonus cards, total final card count, and rank bonus separately.

Questions after the test

  1. When did you most want to add to a pile, take it, or close it—and why?
  2. Did a Firm marker feel like useful access, guaranteed income, or ownership?
  3. Were Collection counters scarce at interesting moments?
  4. Did resolving up to three cards one at a time make turns sharper or harder to follow?
  5. Did holding, playing, and potentially recollecting Bonus cards create meaningful timing choices?
  6. Could you predict when the year would end?
  7. Was becoming next Early Bird enough reason to end a year?
  8. Could everyone track cards waiting in New Year without extra handling?
  9. Did Capital scoring affect the Centre at the right intensity?
  10. Did Quiet Hours feel thematic, distinctive, and constrained enough by its empty-district requirement?
  11. Did the city spokes make Bellweather Centre easier to contest, easier to block, or genuinely useful as a route?
  12. Did the third Organise card create useful routes, idle excess, or pressure to Collect Organise-heavy piles?
  13. Did two years provide enough actions and card circulation to pursue each agenda before its Election?
  14. Did the final ranking reward deliberate conservation without making late Operations or Bonus-card plays feel wrong?
  15. Which part of the annual procedure felt redundant?

After the table clears

  1. Separate observed behavior from player interpretation.
  2. Update the feedback summary only when a pattern appears.
  3. Change one connected rule group at a time.
  4. Update rules, components, the decision log, and the changelog together.
