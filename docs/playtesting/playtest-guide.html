Playtest guide

Test the new loop before tuning its edges.

Index/Playtest guide

Ruleset 24 gives every party a third, Unbound Bonus card and checks the strict majority of currently returned Firm markers at the end of every Lobby turn. The next useful evidence is whether the six novel effects justify their added choice load and whether Shell Firm and Midnight Session make the ending more tense or merely harder to predict.

Before the test

  1. Use the complete current setup, including doubled components at two or three players.
  2. Keep each hand and New Year area covered while leaving each area’s combined ordinary-and-Bonus card count visible.
  3. Explain that Firm markers establish who receives Closure, return when their parties close, and are not bids.
  4. Explain that every party begins with two Bonus cards tied to preferred Operations and one Unbound card that resolves only its printed effect.
  5. Explain that Shell Firm may move any active Firm marker while preserving its owner, and Midnight Session may reuse only the current player’s returned Firm marker.
  6. Explain that a player may Pass only after all of their Firm markers return, and that Pass never ends the year.
  7. Explain that the year-ending majority is checked at the end of every Lobby turn, after the complete action and any Firm movement.
  8. Remind players that ABBA or ABCCBA applies only to Party Openings.
  9. Confirm that each standard hand contains three Organise cards, doubled to six at two or three players.
  10. Trace the shared borders and nine bridges, including routes through Bellweather Centre and note that Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood begin full.
  11. Explain that Election 3 ranks each human player’s combined final hand; home Bonus cards and separate Firm identities do not count.
  12. Choose one primary question from the current queue and copy the session template.
Suggested first test

Unbound Bonus-card circulation

Play Years 1–2 and Election 1 without adding a midterm effect. Put all eighteen Bonus cards in their party homes and use the ordinary Support setup and assigned scoring slots. Stop after the Election only if the group cannot continue; a complete six-year run is valuable once the loop is comprehensible.

Primary observation: For each Unbound card, note when it is available, collected, held, played, or ignored; record the state it changes and whether its extra handling earns a decision that the preferred-Operation cards could not provide.

During each year

  • Record the opening order and which parties are left unopened.
  • Record every Operate size, ordinary or Bonus card played, total resolution time, pauses between cards, and voluntary finish after one or two.
  • Record each Collection counter spent, including empty-pile Collections, deliberate no-card Collections, and any Collect a player wanted but could not afford.
  • Record every voluntary Closure, automatic Closure, returned or reused Firm marker, and player whose turn ended the year.
  • Mark every Pass after the player’s final Firm returns and whether they could have Operated or Collected instead.
  • Count the Lobby turns and note who loses an expected turn to a mid-orbit ending.
  • Record every optional Bonus-card award at Collect, voluntary Close, and automatic Closure, including declines and unavailable cards.
  • For Institutional Memory, record how many objectives were completed; for Shell Firm and Midnight Session, record every repeated Closure or Bonus-card award they enabled.
  • At Cleanup, verify that automatic Closure choices finish, remaining Firms return, all New Year cards enter private hands, Collection counters return, and Early Bird moves before any Election.

During an Election

  • Reveal only the assigned slot and identify the fixed Capital card before scoring.
  • Record each player’s base regional score, relative-seat modifier when used, and Capital score separately.
  • Record which of each Capital card’s three parties are actually present in Bellweather Centre; coalition partners do not substitute.
  • Record the whole-map 3/2/1 draw and thinning, leaving the Centre untouched.
  • Clear Court Support after scoring without moving Coalition Targets.
  • At Election 3, record each human player’s ordinary cards, held Bonus cards, total final card count, and rank bonus separately.

Questions after the test

  1. When did you most want to add to a pile, take it, or close it—and why?
  2. Did a Firm marker feel like useful access, guaranteed income, or ownership?
  3. Were Collection counters scarce at interesting moments?
  4. Did resolving up to three cards one at a time make turns sharper or harder to follow?
  5. Did holding, playing, and potentially recollecting Bonus cards create meaningful timing choices?
  6. Did each Unbound card create a distinctive decision often enough to justify being the party’s third card?
  7. Could players resolve Institutional Memory, Mass Transit, and Empty Every Nest without rereading or lengthy board searches?
  8. Did Shell Firm or Midnight Session create satisfying reversals, excessive repeated Closure rewards, or confusing Firm ownership?
  9. Could you predict when the year would end?
  10. Was becoming next Early Bird enough reason to end a year?
  11. Could everyone track cards waiting in New Year without extra handling?
  12. Did Capital scoring affect the Centre at the right intensity?
  13. Did Quiet Hours feel thematic, distinctive, and constrained enough by its empty-district requirement?
  14. Did the bridges make Bellweather Centre worth contesting, and did the outer routes offer useful alternatives?
  15. Did the third Organise card create useful routes, idle excess, or pressure to Collect Organise-heavy piles?
  16. Did two years provide enough actions and card circulation to pursue each agenda before its Election?
  17. Did the final ranking reward deliberate conservation without making late Operations or Bonus-card plays feel wrong?
  18. Which part of the annual procedure felt redundant?

After the table clears

  1. Separate observed behavior from player interpretation.
  2. Update the feedback summary only when a pattern appears.
  3. Change one connected rule group at a time.
  4. Update rules, components, the decision log, and the changelog together.
