Voting reminder markers

Print this one A4 portrait page at actual size on card and cut out the 24 squares: four 16 × 16 mm yes markers per human player. Each row supplies one player; the markers themselves are identical.

Place them in the four blank 18 × 18 mm spaces on your private sheet however you wish. Keep unused markers behind the shield. These are entirely private reminders of desired outcomes, with no effect on votes, actions or scoring. Their use is optional; an empty space has no fixed meaning. Supply remains four per human at 2–3 players.
