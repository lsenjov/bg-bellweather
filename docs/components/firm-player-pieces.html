Ruleset 15 Firm and player pieces

Print at actual size on three A4 landscape sheets. Each Firm kit contains one 40 × 61 mm annual opening marker, two 20 mm Collection counters, and one 25 mm score marker.

At four to six players, take one complete kit. At two or three players, combine two kits for two openings and four counters, but keep only one folio and use one score marker.
