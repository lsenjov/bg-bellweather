Component specification

Policy deck

Thirty 63 × 88 mm cards on four pairs of sheets: proposal fronts followed by matching enacted backs. Print double-sided, flip on the long edge. Check alignment with the first sheet pair. Both faces identify the policy; shuffle and draw with both faces concealed. Print at 100% on A4 portrait paper.
