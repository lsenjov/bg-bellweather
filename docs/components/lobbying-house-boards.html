Lobbying house boards

Print at actual size on three A4 portrait sheets. Each sheet holds two 200 × 140 mm pastel stationery boards whose charcoal numbers, emblems, and patterns identify their houses without resembling saturated party state. At two or three players, take two house boards but share one reserve, agenda, and score.

01Open

Choose targets. Commit public Clout and operation-token count.

02Cover

Place and revise counterbids during the timer.

03Reveal

Lift together. Cancel equal counterbids per contest.

04Resolve

Pecking: low to high. Parties: 1 to 6.

05Revolve

Each full bid moves one rank down; lowest to highest.

06Advance

Collect received bids. Election Day after round 12.
