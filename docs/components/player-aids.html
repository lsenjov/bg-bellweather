Player aids

References for the annual cycle and direct Operations.

Index/Player aids

The complete helper printed inside each Firm shield keeps the Year, Lobby actions, Operation keywords, shared terms, and Election scoring visible without becoming a teaching document.

Required aid

Year sequence

  1. Party Openings: from Early Bird, use snake order at two or three players and once clockwise at four to six.
  2. Lobby: clockwise turns; Operate, Collect, Close, or Pass.
  3. Cleanup: close remaining parties to their Firm owners, resolve their required Bonus-card awards, release New Year cards, refill Collection counters, and give Early Bird to the player whose turn ended the year.
  4. Election: after Years 2, 4, and 6, always after Cleanup.
Required aid

Lobby action reference

  • Operate: resolve one to three cards, one at a time, with one acting party.
  • Collect and Close: move the complete pile and one available Bonus card into New Year.
  • Collect: spend a counter at any open party, including one with an empty pile; leave it open.
  • Close: after your first Lobby turn, choose one open party containing your Firm marker and return it.
  • Pass: only after all your Firm markers have returned; do nothing.
  • At the end of every Lobby turn, more than half the Firm markers currently returned ends the Lobby.
Required aid

Operations, keywords, and Election

Give Organise, Rally, and Smear one short meaning each; their cards retain complete legality and absent-party exceptions. Define only Open party, acting party, New Year, policy, law, and Bonus. Distinguish a bound card’s baseline-plus-effect from an Unbound card’s printed effect, but do not list named Bonus cards or edge cases.

The Election checklist covers random half-capacity retention outside Centre, one vote per remaining Support on each region’s policy, majority passage with ties failing, and activation after all four votes. Award Party laws to the largest surviving For contributor in their region, breaking ties leftmost in the For row. Keep them beside that party; only its Operations benefit. Global scoring laws affect every player. Deal new policies after Elections 1 and 2. After Election 3, reveal each human’s single priority card, score all enacted policies, and add final hand rank. Negative totals are allowed. See the policy reference for law composition.

Decided

Physical aid format

Print the complete helper inside each Firm shield, replacing the separate reference sheet. Arrange Year sequence on the left wing, Lobby and Election on the rectangular centre, and Operations and Keywords on the right wing. Retain the preceding helper’s rules reference, omitting the redundant one-scoring-card-per-player sentence and the separate Party/Global law reminder paragraph. Use 8 pt body text on pale paper, with all text clear of the two folds and sloping cut edges.

The shield interior is the per-player rules reference. The separate public and private sheets contain physical areas and Firm identity; the private reminder grid has no rules effect. The Firm shield hides Private Priorities and the reminders. The shared piece sheet supplies Firm cards, Early Bird and the Year marker.
