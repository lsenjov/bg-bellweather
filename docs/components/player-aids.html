Player aids

References for the annual cycle and direct Operations.

Index/Player aids

The aid should keep the four Lobby choices, annual timing, and Operation legality visible without duplicating the full rulebook.

Required aid

Year sequence

  1. Party Openings: ABBA at two players, ABCCBA at three, otherwise once clockwise.
  2. Lobby: clockwise turns; Operate, Collect, Close, or Pass.
  3. Cleanup: close remaining parties, resolve their optional Bonus-card awards, release New Year cards, return Collection counters, move Early Bird.
  4. Election: after Years 2, 4, and 6, always after Cleanup. Years 1, 3, and 5 are thematic midterms with no extra procedure.
Required aid

Lobby action reference

  • Operate: resolve one ordinary or held Bonus card immediately on one open party; finish or resolve another there, up to three total. The third ends the action.
  • Collect: spend a counter and take one complete non-empty party pile plus optionally one Bonus card there into New Year.
  • Close: only your opened party and not on your first Lobby turn; take its pile, optionally one Bonus card there, and return its Firm marker.
  • Pass: every player passing consecutively closes all parties to their opening Firms.
  • A strict majority of opening markers returned ends the year immediately.
  • The player whose action ends the year becomes Early Bird.
Required aid

Operation and Capital reference

Include baseline legality for Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court; the twelve collectible Bonus cards; and reminders that a Bonus card is standalone, atomic, playable at its home or reciprocal partner, and returns home after success.

Capital reminder: use the three parties on the Capital card; 0/1 present scores 0, 2 present scores 1, and all 3 score 3. Coalition partners and duplicate Support do not add matches.

Election 3 reminder: after Year 6 Cleanup, rank each human player’s combined ordinary and held Bonus cards from 0 through one less than the player count. Ties use the highest occupied rank; Bonus cards at party homes do not count.

Decided

Physical aid format

The shared state sheet carries the public annual sequence. Each player folio carries the per-player Lobby action reference.
