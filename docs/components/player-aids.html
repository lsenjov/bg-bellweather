Player aids

References for the annual cycle and direct Operations.

Index/Player aids

One compact helper per player keeps the Year, Lobby actions, Operation keywords, shared terms, and Election scoring visible without becoming a teaching document.

Required aid

Year sequence

  1. Party Openings: from Early Bird, use snake order at two or three players and once clockwise at four to six.
  2. Lobby: clockwise turns; Operate, Collect, Close, or Pass.
  3. Cleanup: close remaining parties to their openers, resolve their optional Bonus-card awards, release New Year cards, refill Collection counters, and give Early Bird to the majority closer.
  4. Election: after Years 2, 4, and 6, always after Cleanup.
Required aid

Lobby action reference

  • Operate: resolve one to three cards, one at a time, with one acting party.
  • Collect and Close: move the complete pile and optionally one Bonus card into New Year.
  • Collect: spend a counter at any open party, including one with an empty pile; leave it open.
  • Close: after your first Lobby turn, choose one party you opened and return its Firm marker.
  • Pass: only after all your Firm markers have returned; do nothing.
  • More than half the Firm markers returned ends the Lobby immediately.
Required aid

Operations, keywords, and Election

Give Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court one short meaning each; their cards retain complete legality and absent-party exceptions. Define only Open party, acting party, New Year, Target, coalition, and Bonus. Do not list named Bonus cards, fixed Bonus quantities, setup, or edge cases.

The Election checklist covers agenda reveal, random whole-map 1/2/3 retention outside the Centre, ordinary objective and coalition scoring, Gain/Lose base scores, 0/0/1/3 Capital scoring, final hand rank at Election 3, and clearing Court Support without moving Targets. It also keeps the low-player exceptions and final victory condition visible.

Decided

Physical aid format

The helper is one-sided at 210 × 99 mm, with three identical horizontal copies filling one A4 portrait sheet. Use a neutral Bellweather register treatment that remains legible in grayscale, keep body copy at 7 pt where possible and never below 6.5 pt, and include two horizontal cut guides.

The printable player helper becomes the sole per-player rules reference. The reduced player folios retain only physical areas and Firm identity; the unchanged shared state sheet may retain its public annual register.
