Duplex reminder markers

Print these two A4 landscape pages at actual size, duplex with short-edge binding, on opaque card. This produces one physical sheet: 24 reversible ✓/✕ vote reminders.

Print a plain-paper test first to check front/back alignment. The back-side block is mirrored across the sheet for registration; the text and symbols read normally. Cut along the front grid lines. For a one-sided printer, mount pages 1 and 2 back-to-back with their top edges aligned.

Give each human four vote markers, including at 2–3 players. Each marker is 16 × 16 mm. Use these with your own generic counters in the four blank reminder spaces on the private sheet. They are entirely private reminders with no effect on votes, actions or scoring and no prescribed game meaning.
