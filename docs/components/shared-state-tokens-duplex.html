Duplex reminder markers

Print these two A4 landscape pages at actual size, duplex with short-edge binding, on opaque card. This produces one physical sheet: 24 reversible ✓/✕ vote reminders and 24 reversible 1–2/3+ range reminders.

Print a plain-paper test first to check front/back alignment. Back-side block positions are swapped for registration; the text and symbols read normally. Cut along the front grid lines. For a one-sided printer, mount pages 1 and 2 back-to-back with their top edges aligned.

Give each human four markers of each type: four pairs in total, including at 2–3 players. Each marker is 16 × 16 mm. Use and flip either type freely in the four blank pair spaces on the private sheet; they are entirely private reminders with no effect on votes, actions or scoring. No face or number range has a prescribed game meaning.
