Ruleset 21 Bonus cards

Print this front-only A4 portrait sheet at actual size. Each of the twelve unique 40 × 61 mm cards begins over its matching home on a party workspace, may enter a player’s private hand through Collect or Closure, and returns home immediately after successful play.
