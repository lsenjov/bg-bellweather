Public & private player sheets

Print at actual size on two A4 portrait sheets. Cut each firm’s pair into two separate 100 × 90 mm sheets along the centre border. The public sheet’s firm header faces the other players. Keep New Year and Collection public; put Private Priorities and the blank reminder grid behind the shield.

The grid has no labels or printed vote icons. Use the four yes markers freely as private reminders; they have no gameplay effect. At 2–3 players, use one pair of sheets and four markers per human.
