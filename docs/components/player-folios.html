Ruleset 20 player folios

Print at actual size on three A4 portrait sheets. Each 200 × 120 mm folio holds three ordered Election agendas, one covered New Year pile, and four Collection counters while keeping the available ordinary and Bonus hand behind a screen. Keep both New Year and hand totals visible, and returned opening markers face up beside the folio.

At four to six players, use the first two Collection wells. At two or three players, use all four wells and stack each registered agenda pair with its odd-numbered Capital card on top.
