Bid cards

Print at actual size, duplex on the long edge. Every card is exactly 40 × 61 mm for a 41 × 63 mm opaque sleeve. The eighteen A4 portrait pages alternate fronts and shared backs for sixty one-Leverage, twenty-four Bluff, twelve Organise, twenty-four Rally, twelve Smear, and six Coalition cards. The unique L, B, O, R, S, and C corner indices remain visible in a fanned hand.
