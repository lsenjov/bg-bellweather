Ruleset 15 state and player pieces

Print at actual size on one A4 landscape sheet. It contains the complete six-Firm supply: six exact 40 × 61 mm annual opening markers, twelve 14 mm Collection counters, six 25 mm score markers, Early Bird and Year markers, six Closed markers, six Coalition Targets, and twelve bonus-used markers.

At four to six players, take one opening, two Collection counters, and one score marker from one Firm. At two or three players, combine two Firm sets for two openings and four counters, but use only one score marker.
