Ruleset 20 party workspaces

Print at actual size on three A4 landscape sheets and cut each sheet along the shared middle border. Every party workspace holds one annual Firm opening marker, one public Operation pile, two exact Bonus-card homes, five Court spaces, and its persistent Coalition Target.

A Firm marker in the opening well means the party is open. Return it to its player when the party closes. Put each collectible Bonus card over its matching home and return it there immediately after play. Stack each party’s Support on the five Court spaces, move its Target only after a unique Court leader emerges, and clear Court Support after every Election.
