Party tracking boards

Print at actual size on three A4 landscape sheets. Cut each sheet along the shared middle border. The boards match the prototype Support colours: Honeycomb yellow, Old Shell green, Foxglove pink, Riverworks blue, Many Wings orange, and Night Parliament black. Stack acting-party Support on the five Court spaces, use the source-coloured Coalition Target token supplied on the Pecking Order sheet to mark the persistent target and move it when a unique leader emerges, and cover a bonus’s Ready circle after it is used.
