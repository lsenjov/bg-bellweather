Policy prototype party workspaces

Print at actual size on three A4 landscape sheets and cut each sheet along the shared middle border. Every party workspace holds a party identity panel with its motto and unnumbered priorities, one Firm marker well, one public Operation pile, and three Bonus-card spaces to the right. Priorities run from most important at the top to least important at the bottom.

A Firm marker in the opening well means the party is open. Return it to its owner when the party closes; a Bonus card may move it to another party or reuse it later. Put each party’s three collectible Bonus cards over its three blank card spaces and return played cards to a blank space at their home party. A party votes For when the policy’s + issue ranks above its − issue. Rankings never change.

Open print-ready PDF →

Keep awarded Party policies face up beside their recipient’s board, separate from Bonus cards and Operation piles. Only that acting party’s Operations benefit, whoever plays them. Policies stay here through Closure and never enter a player’s hand.
