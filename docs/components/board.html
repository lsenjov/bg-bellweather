Board

District Support and yearly party access.

Index/Board

The main board has a fixed Support map and six party areas. There is no shared auction or persistent party-order track.

Decided

Party areas

Each party area must show its identity, two favored bonuses, five Court spaces, Coalition Target, annual Firm opening position, and public Operation pile. A Firm marker on a party identifies who receives that party’s pile when it closes; it is not a bid. Its presence also means the party is open.

  • The pile must hold and expose the families of played Operation cards.
  • Two annual bonus states reset during Cleanup and do not reset when the pile is Collected.
  • Returning a Firm marker closes its party. No separate Closed state or marker is required.
  • Quiet Hours needs no delayed marker; its chosen empty district is resolved immediately after Rally.

The printable party workspaces place an exact card-sized Firm opening and public Operation pile beside the bonus panels, with Coalition Target markers supplied on the shared state sheet.

Decided

District map

The map contains sixteen districts, fifty-seven Support spaces, and eighteen shared borders. Shared printed border segments define adjacency; districts touching only at a point are not adjacent. The outer twelve districts form a continuous ring, while Crown Road, Canal Ward, and Old Quarter connect Bellweather Centre directly to Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood respectively.

Superseded

Retired Pecking Order

The mandatory Pecking Order auction, six movable party-order tokens, adjacent swaps, and special low-to-high Operation resolution are retired. Their former role is summarized in the component archive; the current Early Bird, Year, bonus-used, and Coalition Target markers are on the shared state sheet.

Decided

Print layout

The party workspaces sit beside the district map so piles never obscure Support. Each opening and pile uses an exact card-sized well. A marker in the opening well means open; returning it closes the party. The two Ready circles take distinct bonus-used markers.
