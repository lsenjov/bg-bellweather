Board

Animal parties, political state, and shared media order.

Index/Board

A connected district map holds limited Support spots. Six animal political parties are the ordinary influence targets, while the Pecking Order board holds their persistent resolution sequence and its mandatory auction.

Decided

Current board role

  • Give every party a unique, readable identity and two favored-operation bonus icons.
  • Show connected political districts, their limited Support spots, and every party’s public Support.
  • Show each party’s directional overture and any reciprocal coalition.
  • Provide one public location for each opening influence contest and its ranked bids.
  • List every party in a persistent Pecking Order that determines party-contest resolution.
  • Provide one automatic Pecking Order auction every Campaign Round, with no opener or opening bid.
  • Resolve Pecking Order operation tokens from the lowest active bid to the highest, swapping one adjacent pair per token.
  • Show that the Pecking Order Revolving Door retains the normal high-to-low bid ranking.
  • Use the same fixed starting order in every first-prototype session.

Prototype component decided / setup open

Party state

Each party has public Support on the district map, a public Support reserve, and one directional overture that may point toward another party. Reciprocal overtures form a coalition; on Election Day, drawn Support belonging to either partner scores for objectives naming either party. Old Shell’s Court bonus may reinforce its overture with a Shell marker. Night Parliament may hold delayed Rally and Court bonuses until ordinary operation token resolution ends. Exact Support quantities, starting positions, and starting overtures—including whether any begin unpointed—remain unresolved.

Each party has one long horizontal tracking board. Three A4 landscape sheets hold two boards each. A board shows the party identity and favored operations, one opening-bid area, two full-text bonus panels with coverable Ready circles, and an overture track containing the five other parties plus an explicitly setup-only unpointed state. Reciprocal overtures show a coalition without a separate coalition marker. Old Shell’s board holds the Shell marker when its overture is reinforced; Night Parliament reminds players to mark each bid that claimed a delayed bonus. Party Support reserves remain off these boards.

Prototype component decided / starting order open

Pecking Order

The Pecking Order is one A4 landscape board with six numbered positions and six movable 44 mm party tokens. The positions run from the first party contest to the last. Each token repeats its party’s prototype colour, emblem, and short name so the order remains readable without relying on colour. Party numbers do not appear on the tokens because numbers identify resolution positions on this component.

The board also identifies the automatic auction and its exceptional operative sequence. There is no opening-bid position. Covered counterbids sit beside the board’s long yellow edge because a fixed set of 70 mm spaces could not hold the maximum counterbid supply. Bids rank normally from most Clout to least, operatives execute from the lowest active bid to the highest, and the Revolving Door then uses the normal high-to-low ranking. Every operative swaps one adjacent pair of party tokens.

The first prototype uses the same starting order in every session, but the exact order remains an open setup choice.

Prototype map decided / setup open

District map

The first prototype uses sixteen named districts in a ring-and-cross layout. Twelve districts form the outer land ring. Bellwether Centre connects through Crown Road, Canal Ward, and Old Quarter to Northreach, Reedwater, and Westgate. Districts are neighboring when they share a black border segment; meeting only at a point or across Crownwater does not make them neighbors.

A3 print export

The print-ready PDF is one 420 × 297 mm A3 landscape page. It keeps the 396 × 297 mm artwork at 100% scale and centers it with 12 mm white margins on the left and right. Print at “Actual size” or 100% with “Fit to page” disabled.

Run ./scripts/export-print-assets.sh from the repository root to regenerate this PDF and every other print-ready component. The command verifies the fonts and the 396 × 297 mm source size before converting map text to paths, so the printed result does not depend on fonts installed at the printer. The repository README lists all tool and font prerequisites.

The grey districts—Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood—hold six Support each. Their six ring neighbors hold four each. Bellwether Centre holds three. Northreach, Reedwater, Westgate, and the three causeway districts hold two each. The complete map has fifty-seven Support spots and eighteen shared borders.

Superseded hypothesis

Initial ladder sketch

Draw six ordered spaces on one sheet of paper. Number them from bottom to top, leave room for every player marker on every space, and add no printed effects.

Reason: six spaces are enough to create relative position without making track length the main balance decision.

Specification checklist

  • Every party has a unique name, animal identity, and pair of favored-operation bonus icons.
  • Every district has a unique name, readable shared borders, and a fixed number of Support spots.
  • Party Support, free spots, overtures, coalitions, Old Shell’s reinforcement, and claimed Night Parliament delays remain visible from every seat.
  • Opening bids and counterbid locations remain readable around every party.
  • The Pecking Order and direction of resolution read from every seat.
  • The Pecking Order board is visibly distinct from the six parties while its movable tokens repeat party identities.
  • Any triggered effect is printed where it occurs.
  • The board shows state, not rules players must memorize.
