Board

District Support and yearly party access.

Index/Board

The main board has a fixed Support map and six party areas. There is no shared auction or persistent party-order track.

Decided

Party areas

Each party area must show its identity, two favored bonuses, five Court spaces, Coalition Target, annual Firm opening position, public Operation pile, and open/closed state. A Firm marker on a party identifies who receives that party’s pile when it closes; it is not a bid.

  • The pile must hold and expose the families of played Operation cards.
  • Two annual bonus states reset during Cleanup and do not reset when the pile is Collected.
  • A closed state must prevent further Operations while preserving Firm ownership through Cleanup.
  • Night Shift needs no delayed marker; it is an immediate second Rally in the same district.

The existing Ruleset 14 printable party boards remain a historical layout reference until the opening and pile areas are redrawn.

Decided

Superseded

Pecking Order

The mandatory Pecking Order auction, six movable party-order tokens, adjacent swaps, and special low-to-high Operation resolution are retired. The printable sheet remains a historical artifact and the former role is summarized in the component archive. The Early Bird marker and Coalition Target markers should move to the new shared token supply.
