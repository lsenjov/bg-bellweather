Animal parties, political state, and shared media order.

Index/Board

A connected district map holds limited Support spots. Six animal political parties are the ordinary influence targets, while the Pecking Order board holds their persistent resolution sequence and its mandatory auction.

Decided

Current board role

  • Give every party a unique, readable identity and two favored-operation bonus icons.
  • Show connected political districts, their limited Support spots, and every party’s public Support.
  • Show each party’s directional coalition target and any reciprocal coalition.
  • Provide one public location for each opening influence contest and its ranked bids.
  • List every party in a persistent Pecking Order that determines party-contest resolution.
  • Provide one automatic Pecking Order auction every Campaign Round, with no opener or opening bid.
  • Resolve Pecking Order operation cards from the lowest active bid to the highest, swapping one adjacent pair per card.
  • Show that the Pecking Order Revolving Door retains the normal high-to-low bid ranking.
  • Randomize the six party tokens to form the starting order.

Prototype component and starting state decided

Party state

Each party has an unlimited Support supply, public Support on the district map, and one directional coalition target that may point toward another party. Every party begins with one Support in Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood, and all six coalition targets begin unpointed. Reciprocal coalition targets form a coalition; on Election Day, drawn Support belonging to either partner scores for objectives naming either party. Old Shell’s Coalition bonus may reinforce its coalition target with a Shell marker. Night Parliament may hold delayed Rally and Coalition bonuses until ordinary operation-card resolution ends.

Each party has one long horizontal tracking board and one source-coloured coalition target token supplied on the shared Pecking Order sheet. Three A4 landscape sheets hold two boards each. A board shows the party identity and favored operations, one opening-bid area, two full-text bonus panels with coverable Ready circles, and a coalition target track containing a neutral setup position plus five target positions. Every target position uses the target party’s colour, short code, and name, making the current target legible at a glance without relying on colour alone. Reciprocal targets show a coalition without a separate coalition marker. Old Shell’s board holds the Shell marker when its coalition target is reinforced; Night Parliament reminds players to mark each bid that claimed a delayed bonus. Unlimited Support does not require a tracked reserve on these boards.

Prototype component and random setup decided

Pecking Order

The Pecking Order is one A4 landscape sheet. Its upper board has six numbered positions, while its cut-away lower panel supplies six movable 44 mm party tokens, six source-coloured coalition target tokens, one Early Bird marker, and one round marker. The positions run from the first party contest to the last. Each party token repeats its party’s prototype colour, emblem, and short name so the order remains readable without relying on colour. Party numbers do not appear on the tokens because numbers identify resolution positions on this component.

The board identifies the automatic auction and its exceptional operation-card sequence without retaining the former three-panel procedure block. There is no opening-bid position. Counterbid stacks sit beside the board’s long yellow edge. Bids rank normally from most Leverage to least, operation cards execute from the lowest active bid to the highest, and the Revolving Door then uses the normal high-to-low ranking. Every operation card swaps one adjacent pair of party tokens; Bluff cards do nothing.

Shuffle all six party tokens during setup to create a random starting order.

Prototype map decided / setup open

District map

The first prototype uses sixteen named districts in a ring-and-cross layout. Twelve districts form the outer land ring. Bellweather Centre connects through Crown Road, Canal Ward, and Old Quarter to Northreach, Reedwater, and Westgate. Districts are neighboring when they share a black border segment; meeting only at a point or across Crownwater does not make them neighbors. The A3 map is dedicated to district play; campaign and score state use a separate A4 tracker sheet.

A3 print export

The print-ready PDF is one 420 × 297 mm A3 landscape page. It keeps the 396 × 297 mm artwork at 100% scale and centers it with 12 mm white margins on the left and right. Print at “Actual size” or 100% with “Fit to page” disabled.

Run ./scripts/export-print-assets.sh from the repository root to regenerate this PDF and every other print-ready component. The command verifies the fonts and the 396 × 297 mm source size before converting map text to paths, so the printed result does not depend on fonts installed at the printer. The repository README lists all tool and font prerequisites.

The grey districts—Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood—hold six Support each. Their six ring neighbors hold four each. Bellweather Centre holds three. Northreach, Reedwater, Westgate, and the three causeway districts hold two each. The complete map has fifty-seven Support spots and eighteen shared borders. Capacity remains visible through the number of printed Support circles; redundant “2 spots,” “4 spots,” and “6 spots” labels are omitted.

Shared tracker sheet

The separate A4 landscape sheet places scores 0–40 in a 7 × 6 alternating-row snake and retains the three-column campaign sequence: R1–R4/E1, R5–R8/E2, and R9–R12/E3. Every track space is 27 mm square, fitting the existing 25 mm firm score markers and 22 mm round marker. Record a total beside the score track when it falls below 0 or rises above 40.

Specification checklist

  • Every party has a unique name, animal identity, and pair of favored-operation bonus icons.
  • Every district has a unique name, readable shared borders, and a fixed number of Support spots.
  • Party Support, free spots, coalition targets, coalitions, Old Shell’s reinforcement, and claimed Night Parliament delays remain visible from every seat.
  • Opening bids and counterbid locations remain readable around every party.
  • The Pecking Order and direction of resolution read from every seat.
  • The Pecking Order board is visibly distinct from the six parties while its movable tokens repeat party identities.
  • Any triggered effect is printed where it occurs.
  • The board shows state, not rules players must memorize.
