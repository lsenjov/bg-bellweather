Board

District Support, coalition visibility, and yearly party access.

Index/Board

The physical table has a fixed Support map, a compact Coalition summary, and six party areas. There is no shared auction or persistent party-order track.

Decided

Party areas

Each party area must show its identity, three blank card-sized Bonus spaces, five Court spaces, Coalition Target, annual Firm opening position, and public Operation pile. The current two Bonus cards occupy any two spaces; the third remains empty. A Firm marker on a party identifies who receives that party’s pile when it closes; it is not a bid. Its presence also means the party is open.

  • The pile must hold and expose the families of played Operation cards.
  • A Bonus card’s presence in any blank space visibly shows that it is available at its home party.
  • Returning a Firm marker closes its party. No separate Closed state or marker is required.
  • Quiet Hours needs no delayed marker; its chosen empty district is resolved immediately after Rally.

The printable party workspaces place an exact card-sized Firm opening and public Operation pile beside three blank, card-sized Bonus-card spaces, with Coalition Target markers supplied on the shared state sheet.

Decided

District map

The map contains sixteen districts, fifty-seven Support spaces, and eighteen shared borders. Shared printed border segments define adjacency; districts touching only at a point are not adjacent. The outer twelve districts form a continuous ring, while Crown Road, Canal Ward, and Old Quarter connect Bellweather Centre directly to Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood respectively.

Decided

Coalition summary

The map’s unused right margin holds a roughly 49 × 98 mm summary without moving or resizing the district diagram. Three interchangeable, unnumbered rows each join two 14 mm circular wells. A boxed reserve beneath them holds six unpaired tokens in a 3 × 2 arrangement, with No coalition printed inside the box.

Each party has one 14 mm Coalition summary token showing only its established color and emblem. All six begin in the reserve. After each card finishes resolving, the acting player places every reciprocal pair on any empty joined row and returns all other tokens to the reserve. Coalition Targets remain authoritative; the summary is only a table-readable copy of their reciprocal state.

Superseded

Retired Pecking Order

The mandatory Pecking Order auction, six movable party-order tokens, adjacent swaps, and special low-to-high Operation resolution are retired. Their former role is summarized in the component archive; the current Early Bird, Year, Coalition Target, and Coalition summary tokens are on the shared state sheet.

Decided

Print layout

The party workspaces sit beside the district map so piles never obscure Support. Each opening, pile, and blank Bonus-card space uses an exact card-sized well. A marker in the opening well means open; returning it closes the party. The three Bonus spaces and two workspace wells fit across the existing 287 mm board without reducing their 40 × 61 mm card geometry. The Coalition summary occupies existing map margin, and its six tokens occupy previously unused positions on the state-token sheet, so neither print component grows. The map’s upper-right register shows the current revision, R21; its replaced capacity header, the two-space Bonus layout, and the earlier Ruleset 19 bonus panels remain archived.
