District Support, coalition visibility, and yearly party access.

Index/Board

The physical table has a fixed Support map, compact Coalition and Year trackers, and six party areas. There is no shared auction or persistent party-order track.

Decided

Party areas

Each party area must show its identity, three blank card-sized Bonus spaces, five Court spaces, Coalition Target, annual Firm opening position, and public Operation pile. The party’s three Bonus cards occupy all three spaces. A Firm marker on a party identifies who receives that party’s pile when it closes; it is not a bid. Its presence also means the party is open.

  • The pile must hold and expose the families of played Operation cards.
  • A Bonus card’s presence in any blank space visibly shows that it is available at its home party.
  • Returning a Firm marker closes its party. No separate Closed state or marker is required.
  • Quiet Hours needs no delayed marker; its chosen empty district is resolved immediately after Rally.

The printable party workspaces place an exact card-sized Firm opening and public Operation pile beside three blank, card-sized Bonus-card spaces, with Coalition Target markers supplied on the shared state sheet.

Decided

District map

The default inland map has sixteen districts, 57 Support spaces and 23 adjacencies: fourteen shared borders and nine bridges. Urban, Mixed and Outlying each hold 18 Support; Bellweather Centre holds 3 separately. Water severs adjacency for every effect, including Smear, except at a marked bridge. Point contacts do not count.

Decided

Coalition summary

The Coalition summary fits beside the round tracker at the bottom of the A4 map. Three joined vertical pairs and a separate six-token reserve have 14 mm wells matching the shared-state tokens.

Each party has one 14 mm Coalition summary token showing only its established color and emblem. All six begin in the reserve. After each card finishes resolving, the acting player places every reciprocal pair on any empty joined pair and returns all other tokens to the reserve. Coalition Targets remain authoritative; the summary is only a table-readable copy of their reciprocal state.

Decided

Year register

The landscape A4 map has a six-space round/year tracker along its bottom edge. Each 23 × 23 mm space fits the existing 22 mm Year marker. Advance numerically from 1 through 6; Elections are marked after Years 2, 4 and 6.

Midterms are thematic boundaries with no additional procedure. After Years 2, 4, and 6, complete Cleanup and then resolve the corresponding Election. The separate A4 public ledger now contains only the 0–40 Firm score track.

Superseded

Retired Pecking Order

The mandatory Pecking Order auction, six movable party-order tokens, adjacent swaps, and special low-to-high Operation resolution are retired. Their former role is summarized in the component archive; the current Early Bird, Year, Coalition Target, and Coalition summary tokens are on the shared state sheet.

Decided

Print layout

Print the default map SVG or A4 map PDF at 100% on landscape A4 (297 × 210 mm). The map uses enlarged inland territories and a separate central Bellweather island. Support circles are 4.5 mm across; final physical Support-token size remains an open fit question. Keep party boards beside the map. The replaced rules and components remain archived.
