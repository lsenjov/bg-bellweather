The physical table has a fixed Support map, a shared Global-laws display and Year tracker, and six party areas. There is no shared auction or persistent party-order track.

Decided

Party areas

Each party area must show its identity, three blank card-sized Bonus spaces, a fixed public issue ranking, annual Firm opening position, and public Operation pile. The party’s three Bonus cards occupy all three spaces. A Firm marker on a party identifies who receives that party’s pile when it closes; it is not a bid. Its presence also means the party is open.

  • The pile must hold and expose the families of played Operation cards.
  • A Bonus card’s presence in any blank space visibly shows that it is available at its home party.
  • Returning a Firm marker closes its party. No separate Closed state or marker is required.
  • Quiet Hours needs no delayed marker; its chosen empty district is resolved immediately after Rally.

The printable party workspaces place unnumbered priorities and a motto in the left party panel, then a Party Closed well, public Operation pile and three blank, card-sized Bonus-card spaces; its fixed issue ranking determines its vote on every policy.

Decided

District map

The physical continuous-land map has fifteen districts, 51 Support spaces and 28 adjacencies, all across shared borders. Urban holds 18 Support (9 votes), Industrial Belt 14 (7 votes), and Outlying 16 (8 votes); Bellwether Centre keeps its 3 Support and votes without thinning. Shared borders define adjacency for every effect, including Smear. There are no rivers, lakes or bridges. Point contacts do not count.

Decided

Policy display

The A3 board includes four 63 × 88 mm policy wells: Urban and Outlying on the left, Industrial Belt and Centre on the right. Place one face-up policy in each well. Separate regional policy labels are retired; use the integrated wells. Use a shared display for Global laws, with a draw deck and failed-policy discard. Keep Party laws beside their recipient’s party board, separate from its Bonus cards and Operation pile. Award each to the largest surviving For contributor in its region, ties leftmost in the printed For row. Laws remain with that party even when it closes or loses all Support. Enacted cards need no region-of-origin marker. Keep all law text visible; up to eight laws affect ongoing play.

Decided

Year register

The landscape A3 map has a six-space round/year tracker along its bottom edge. Each 24 × 24 mm space fits the existing 22 mm Year marker. Advance numerically from 1 through 6; Elections are marked after Years 2, 4 and 6.

Midterms are thematic boundaries with no additional procedure. After Years 2, 4, and 6, complete Cleanup and then resolve the corresponding Election. The separate A4 public ledger now contains only the final score ledger, with space for negative totals.

Superseded

Retired Pecking Order

The mandatory Pecking Order auction, six movable party-order tokens, adjacent swaps, and special low-to-high Operation resolution are retired. Their former role is summarized in the component archive; the current Early Bird, Year and Firm pieces are on the shared state sheet.

Decided

Print layout

Previous print specification · Previous map with smaller slots.

Print the default map SVG or A3 map PDF at 100% on landscape A3 (420 × 297 mm). The continuous-land map occupies 264 × 210 mm in the middle of the sheet, with two full-size policy cards on either side. Its area is about 31% smaller than the previous land map; all district capacities and adjacencies are unchanged. Half the Support slots in each outer district have a check mark and a thick green outline; all three Centre slots are marked. These indicate the retained count only: survivors are chosen randomly, regardless of their slot. District labels omit vote counts; regional totals appear inside the policy wells. Support cubes are 8 mm across (about 11.3 mm diagonally, allowing roughly 12 mm). Slots are 13 mm in diameter on 15 mm centres. Retained slots have a 1 mm green stroke, leaving a 12 mm clear interior and a 14 mm outer diameter so the rim remains visible around a centred cube. Slot centres are at least 7.5 mm from district borders. Four-space districts use two rows of two slots; six-space districts use two rows of three. Keep party boards beside the map. The replaced rules and components remain archived.
