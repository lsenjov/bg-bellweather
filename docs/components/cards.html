Index/Cards and operation tokens

Reusable operation tokens drive influence contests, while a separate deck of hidden scoring cards gives each lobbying firm its private Election Day agenda.

Decided

Operation tokens

Each operation token belongs to the Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court family and performs its family’s baseline effect at any party. Each party favors two families and offers one bonus for each. Bids receive first refusal on still-available bonuses from highest rank to lowest, and each bonus may be applied with one matching operation token during the contest. The Pecking Order ignores printed families: every operation token there swaps one adjacent pair of party tokens instead of using its party baseline. Pecking Order bids execute from lowest Clout to highest Clout, and legal swap pairs are chosen when each token executes.

At four to six players, every human contributes two Organise, two Rally, two Smear, and one Court token to the closed economy. At two or three players, each human contributes double that supply. The neutral tokens have no lobbying-firm identity because complete bids pass between players through the Revolving Door and tokens may move through player gifts.

Decided

Scoring cards

The prototype uses a fixed 24-card scoring deck. Each card pairs three different parties with pairwise non-neighboring districts: one 6-capacity, one 4-capacity, and one 2-capacity district. It also names one relative-seat score to gain and one to lose in games with four or more players.

Each player holds one scoring card secretly during a campaign period, reveals it on Election Day, then discards it before drawing a new card for the next period. If the deck cannot deal every player a new card, shuffle the discards to form a new deck. Scoring cards never enter bids or transfer through the Revolving Door.

Superseded anatomy requirement

The initial card checklist required an operation-family icon to remain readable in a fanned hand. Operation tokens replaced that format before production. The superseded reusable-card specification remains in the component archive.

Scoring-card anatomy

  • Finished size is 40 × 61 mm for a 41 × 63 mm sleeve.
  • Objectives remain ordered by 6-, 4-, then 2-capacity district.
  • Party names and emblems identify each objective without relying on color.
  • Gain and Lose references remain visually distinct.
  • Every card keeps its SC-01 through SC-24 identifier.

Inventory

The scoring deck contains twenty-four individually identified cards. The largest required operation supply contains twelve Organise, twelve Rally, twelve Smear, and six Court tokens: the same forty-two tokens support six standard player supplies or three doubled supplies.
