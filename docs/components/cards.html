Cards

Political operations, compatibility, and prototype counts.

Index/Cards

Reusable operative cards are part of the current influence core. Their identities and quantities are concealed in lobbying-house reserves, committed physically to bids, revealed together, executed, and transferred intact through the Revolving Door.

Decided

Operative cards

Each operative belongs to the Campaign, Expansion, Platform, or Coalition family. A party accepts two families and defines the concrete effect of a matching operative. The Press Perch accepts every family, and each operative executed there swaps one adjacent pair in the Pecking Order. The card family is committed during bidding; legal parameters are chosen when it executes.

The total operative supply is fixed after setup. Every lobbying house begins the first prototype with an identical set, although the quantity from each family remains open.

Superseded anatomy requirement

The initial card checklist required a primary value to be readable in a fanned hand. Operative cards currently have no printed value—the Clout physically escrowed beside them ranks the bid—so the live checklist retains only the family icon and timing requirements.

Card anatomy

  • Name states the card’s role, not its flavor alone.
  • Operation-family icon is readable while held in a fanned hand.
  • Timing appears in the same location across a card family.
  • Rules text uses terms from the glossary.
  • Prototype cards use IDs so feedback identifies the exact card.

Inventory

Campaign, Expansion, Platform, and Coalition operative cards are required, but their effects and identical per-player starting counts remain open.
