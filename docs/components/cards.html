Index/Cards

Ruleset 20 uses ordinary Operation cards, twelve unique collectible Bonus cards, and scoring cards. Leverage and Bluff remain retired.

Decided

Operation cards

Each 40 × 61 mm card belongs to Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court. It has no Firm identity because any player may play it on any open party and another player may later Collect it. Played cards remain face up in that party’s pile until Collection or Closure.

Decided

Bonus cards

Each party has two unique 40 × 61 mm Bonus cards with exact homes on its workspace. A recipient may take at most one available card from a party when collecting its pile or receiving it at Closure. Held cards stay with that player until played.

Each Bonus card prints a baseline family and its complete additional effect. It is a standalone atomic action, may be played at its home party or current reciprocal coalition partner, never enters an Operation pile, and returns home immediately after a successful play.

Scoring cards

The 24-card scoring deck retains its exact objective and seat-reference data. Each card names three different parties and three districts, one each of capacity 6, 4, and 2.

At four to six players, each of a player’s three Election slots contains one card; that card supplies both its ordinary objectives and its three Capital parties. At two or three players, each slot contains one registered adjacent odd–even pair. Both cards score their ordinary objectives, but only the first card in the registered pair supplies its three Capital parties. Print or layout should make the first/Capital position unmistakable.

Capital reminder: 0 points for zero or one named party present in Bellweather Centre, 1 point for two, and 3 points for all three.
