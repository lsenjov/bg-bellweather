Cards

Political operations, hidden agendas, and prototype counts.

Index/Cards

Reusable operative cards drive influence contests, while a separate deck of hidden scoring cards gives each lobbying house its private Election Day agenda.

Decided

Operative cards

Each operative belongs to the Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court family and performs its family’s baseline effect at any party. Each party favors two families and offers one bonus for each. Bids receive first refusal on still-available bonuses from highest rank to lowest, and each bonus may be applied with one matching operative during the contest. The Press Perch accepts every family, and each operative executed there swaps one adjacent pair in the Pecking Order instead of using its party baseline. The card family is committed during bidding; legal parameters are chosen when it executes.

The total operative supply is fixed after setup. Every human player begins the first prototype with an identical set, although the quantity from each family remains open.

Decided

Scoring cards

The prototype uses a fixed 24-card scoring deck. Each card pairs three different parties with pairwise non-neighboring districts: one 6-capacity, one 4-capacity, and one 2-capacity district. It also names one relative-seat score to gain and one to lose in games with four or more players.

Each player holds one scoring card secretly during a campaign period, reveals it on Election Day, then discards it before drawing a new card for the next period. If the deck cannot deal every player a new card, shuffle the discards to form a new deck. Scoring cards never enter bids or transfer through the Revolving Door.

Superseded anatomy requirement

The initial card checklist required a primary value to be readable in a fanned hand. Operative cards currently have no printed value—the Clout physically escrowed beside them ranks the bid—so the live checklist retains only the family icon and timing requirements.

Card anatomy

  • Name states the card’s role, not its flavor alone.
  • Operation-family icon is readable while held in a fanned hand.
  • Timing appears in the same location across a card family.
  • Rules text uses terms from the glossary.
  • Prototype cards use IDs so feedback identifies the exact card.

Inventory

The scoring deck contains twenty-four individually identified cards. Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court operative cards are also required, but their identical per-player starting counts remain open.
