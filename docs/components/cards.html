Card categories and backs

All common cards share one back. Party cards share a back per home party. Firm cards share a back per firm, including every Hecate prophecy and movement card. Hecate reward cards have their own shared back. These fourteen back designs identify category or affiliation, never the exact card. Policy and private-priority backs are separate from these categories.

Fronts show name, symbol, effect and destination reminder. Election prophecies have a large top-edge +5, +1–4, 0, −1–4 or −5 label. Their full text explains the inclusive ranges. Place these cards partly beneath or beside the region’s policy with the label visible; no target tokens are needed for them.

Print at 100%, with scaling disabled. Follow each sheet’s duplex binding instruction. Print one front/back pair first and check registration before printing the full supply. Card fronts and mirrored back positions share cut coordinates; printer registration still needs a physical check.

Use and return

Common cards enter the acting party’s pile. Party cards return home. Firm cards normally enter New Year; Hecate prophecies remain placed until resolved or withdrawn. Hecate reward cards return to her face-up supply beside the table area. The supply needs no board or dedicated wells. Only common and party cards count for final hand rank, after final Election effects and New Year release.

One standard common hand is 3 Organise, 4 Rally, 2 Smear. At two or three players use 6, 8 and 4 instead. Personal kits never double. Each player uses one identity and one hand at every player count.
