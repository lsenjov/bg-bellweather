Ruleset 16 Operation cards

This four-sheet supply contains every Operation card needed for six standard hands or three doubled low-player hands. The retired Leverage, Bluff, and bid-stack components remain in the Ruleset 14 archive.

Print the four front-only A4 portrait pages at actual size. Every card is exactly 40 × 61 mm for a 41 × 63 mm opaque sleeve. Cards share cut lines in a zero-gutter 4 × 4 grid. The full sixty-card supply contains twelve Organise, twenty-four Rally, twelve Smear, and twelve Court cards. The O, R, S, and C corner indices remain visible in a fanned hand.
