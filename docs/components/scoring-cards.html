LB Design archive / 03.5

Scoring cards

Twenty-four hidden agendas for Election Day.

Index/Scoring cards

Each human player receives three fixed hidden Election slots: one card per slot at four to six players, or two compatible cards per slot at two or three. Every card names a different party for Urban, Mixed and Outlying. Its base score is the middle regional total, including coalition Support. The same three named parties supply possible Capital points.

Decided

Card anatomy

  1. The first line pairs Urban with one party.
  2. The second line pairs Mixed with a different party.
  3. The third line pairs Outlying with a third party.
  4. Each region has 18 Support capacity; district elections produce up to 9 votes per region. Score only the middle regional total (4 / 6 / 7 scores 6).
  5. In games with four or more players, gain the positive reference’s base regional score and lose the negative reference’s base regional score.
  6. Every physical card front prints its card ID and R24 so it cannot be silently mixed with a replaced deck.

Relative-seat references are Left, Right, Second Left, and Second Right. The two references on one card always indicate different players in a four-player game, so Second Left and Second Right never share a card. At two or three players, ignore both references.

At four to six players, shuffle all twenty-four individual cards once, then deal three ordered Election slots with one card per slot. That card supplies both the player’s ordinary objectives and Capital parties for its Election.

At two or three players, deal two cards per Election slot from the shuffled individual deck. Keep the first card fixed as the Capital card. If the second repeats any region–party pair from the first, set it aside and take the next card; keep passed-over cards in order for later slots. Compatibility is checked only within that Election’s pair. If the remaining cards cannot complete the deal, re-pair earlier slots without revealing any cards. Each card scores its own middle regional total; add the two results. Ignore Gain/Lose references.

Keep the first card physically first or leftmost. Only its parties count for Capital; players cannot choose a different Capital card at Election time. Repeated region–party pairs in different Election slots are allowed.

When an objective’s party is in a coalition on Election Day, drawn Support belonging to either coalition partner scores for that objective. Coalition state changes how the printed party is matched; it does not change the card inventory or draw size.

Decided

Capital scoring

After middle-region scoring and the Gain/Lose modifier, inspect Bellweather Centre without drawing or removing Support. Count how many of the Capital card’s three parties are present: 0 points for zero or one, 1 for two, and 3 for all three.

Multiple Support from the same named party count only once. A coalition partner does not stand in for a named party. Bellweather Centre never participates in ordinary Election draws, thinning, or regional-objective scoring.

Election 3’s final card rank bonus is applied after this ordinary scoring. It is a game-end hand rule, not a scoring-card objective, and does not alter the deck.

Decided

Deck distribution

  • All three regions appear on every card.
  • Each region–party pair appears four times.
  • Each party appears four times in each line position and twelve times across the deck.
  • Each relative-seat direction appears six times as a positive reference and six times as a negative reference.
  • All twenty-four ordered party triples are different.

Physical format

Each scoring card is 40 × 61 mm for a 41 × 63 mm sleeve. Two A4 portrait sheets hold twelve fronts each. Region names, party names and emblems, the middle-score reminder, and Gain/Lose references are printed. Card backs remain open. Preserve the first/Capital card position in low-player pairs.
