Lobbying firm boards

Print at actual size on three A4 portrait sheets. Each sheet holds two 200 × 140 mm pastel stationery boards whose charcoal numbers, emblems, and patterns identify their firms without resembling saturated party state. At two or three players, take two firm boards but share one reserve, compatible two-card agenda, and score.
