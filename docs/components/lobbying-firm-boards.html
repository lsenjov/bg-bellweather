Archived Ruleset 14 lobbying firm boards

Historical component: these boards refer to private bid economies that are not used in Ruleset 15. They remain available as design history.

Print at actual size on three A4 portrait sheets. Each sheet holds two compact 200 × 120 mm pastel stationery boards whose charcoal numbers, emblems, and patterns identify their firms without resembling saturated party state. The three ordered agenda wells each hold one card or a stacked compatible pair; at two or three players, take two firm boards but use one shared three-slot agenda rack and score.
