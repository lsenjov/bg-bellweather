Bellweather

Player Helper

R22
1 · Party OpeningsEarly Bird first · snake order 2–3p · once clockwise 4–6p
2 · LobbyClockwise · one action per turn
3 · CleanupClose remainder to openers (+ optional Bonus) · New Year → hand · refill Collection · majority closer → Early Bird
4 · ElectionAfter Years 2, 4 & 6

Lobby

Operate
Resolve 1–3 cards, one at a time, with one acting party.
Collect & Close
Pile + optional 1 Bonus → New Year.
Collect
Spend counter; any open party; stays open.
Close
After first turn; your open party; return Firm.
Pass
Only when all your Firms are returned; do nothing.

Lobby ends: more than half the Firms returned.

Operations

Organise
Move to a free neighbouring district.
Rally
Add Support where present.
Smear
Remove rival here / next door.
Court
Add Court Support for acting party.

Keywords

Open party
Has a Firm marker.
Acting party
Open party chosen for the entire Operate.
New Year
Gained cards wait; move to hand at Cleanup.
Target
Points to unique Court leader; tied lead = no change.
Coalition
Mutual Targets; partner Support matches objectives, never Capital.
Bonus
Acting party is card’s party or its coalition partner; all-or-nothing; return to card’s party.

Election

  1. Reveal this Election’s agenda(s).
  2. Not Centre: randomly remove Support down to 1 / 2 / 3.
  3. Objectives: named party + coalition partner = 1 per kept Support.
  4. Add Gain base; subtract Lose.
  5. Capital: 0–1 / 2 / 3 named parties present → 0 / 1 / 3; no coalition.
  6. Election 3: final hand rank scores 0 to players−1; ties take the higher rank.
  7. Clear Court Support. Targets stay.

2–3p: score both agendas; ignore Gain/Lose; first card supplies Capital parties.

After Election 3: highest total wins; ties share.
