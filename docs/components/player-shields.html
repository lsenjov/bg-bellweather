The firm’s front office

Six standing shields, each illustrated as its firm’s offices. The matching interior uses pale stationery stripes and a small emblem. One shield per human player, including at 2–3 players; choose one of your controlled firms.

Print-ready shields PDF → · Public & private sheets → · Yes reminder markers →

Printing and assembly
  1. Print at actual size on opaque cardstock. The PDF has twelve A4 landscape pages, alternating exterior and matching interior. Duplex with short-edge binding produces six shields. Test the first pair on plain paper to check orientation and alignment. For a one-sided printer, print each pair separately and mount back-to-back, with both top edges aligned.
  2. Before cutting, score two vertical folds at 80 mm and 200 mm from the artwork’s left edge, joining the guide ticks above and below it. Then cut the 280 × 110 mm outer rectangle using the exterior outline; this trims away the guide ticks and assembly text.
  3. Fold both 80 mm wings back towards the player, roughly at right angles or slightly splayed outward. The 120 mm front and side wings stand on the table; the top and player’s side remain open. Fold flat for storage.
  4. Place the 100 × 90 mm private sheet behind the front panel, with its top edge nearest the shield. Keep Private Priorities, the blank reminder grid and unused yes markers inside. Put the public New Year/Collection sheet beside the shield, fully visible. New Year card faces remain covered and its total count stays public; returned Firm markers also remain public.

Open prototype check: confirm that the 110 mm height and 80 mm wings hide the card and reminders from all seated opponents, and that the chosen cardstock stands reliably. These dimensions are a starting print prototype.
