Lobbying firm kits

Player identity, annual access, and private card areas.

Index/Lobbying firm kits

At four to six players, each human controls one Firm kit. At two or three, each controls two Firm kits but keeps one hand, one player folio, and one score.

Decided

Firm identities

  1. One Fell Swoop Public Affairs
  2. Pairliament Partners
  3. TriumviRAT Advisory
  4. IVy League Public Affairs
  5. Vested Interests
  6. VI.P. Access Group
Required kit

Per Firm

  • One annual opening marker, returned when its party closes and reusable next year.
  • Two Collection counters.
  • One score marker; a low-player player uses one of their two and leaves the other inactive.

The former three interchangeable identity cards are unnecessary. A Firm never owns a hand or point balance separately from its human player.

Player supply

Per human player

  • One private hand for ordinary and held Bonus cards, plus one covered New Year area. Only each area’s total card count is public.
  • Two Collection counters from the controlled Firm kit, or four from two kits at two or three players.
  • Standard hand: 3 Organise, 4 Rally, 2 Smear, 2 Court. Low-player hand: 6, 8, 4, and 4.
  • Three ordered Election slots. Standard slots hold one card; low-player slots hold one registered pair with the first card physically locked as the Capital card.
  • One cumulative public point balance, starting at 5 or at 10 in a low-player game.
  • At Election 3, one final card count combining every ordinary and held Bonus card after Year 6 Cleanup. A low-player player ranks this human hand once, not each Firm separately.
Decided

Physical layout

The 200 × 120 mm printable player folio identifies one controlled Firm, keeps the available ordinary and Bonus hand behind a screen, and gives the covered New Year pile an exact 40 × 61 mm stacking well with its total count visible. Returned opening markers stay face up beside the folio so the Closure count remains public. Three overlapping exact-size agenda wells preserve Election order; each low-player pair places its odd-numbered Capital card on top.

The printable state and player piece sheet provides one opening marker, two Collection counters, and one score marker per Firm kit. A low-player player combines two kits on one folio.

The replaced auction kit is summarized in the component archive.
