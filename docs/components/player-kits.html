Lobbying firm kits

Player identity, annual access, and private card areas.

Index/Lobbying firm kits

At four to six players, each human controls one Firm kit. At two or three, each controls two Firm kits but keeps one hand, one public/private sheet pair, one shield, four yes reminder markers, and one score.

Decided

Firm identities

  1. One Fell Swoop Public Affairs
  2. Pairliament Partners
  3. TriumviRAT Advisory
  4. IVy League Public Affairs
  5. Vested Interests
  6. VI.P. Access Group
Required kit

Per Firm

  • Three identical generic Firm cards are printed. Use one as the annual party-access marker; the other two are spares. Return the active marker when its party closes.
  • Two Collection cubes; printed counters are not supplied.

The three generic copies identify the same Firm; use only one for that Firm’s party access. A Firm never owns a hand or point balance separately from its human player.

Player supply

Per human player

  • One private hand for ordinary and held Bonus cards, plus one covered New Year area. Only each area’s total card count is public.
  • One 100 × 90 mm public sheet, one 100 × 90 mm private sheet, one standing Firm shield with the full player helper printed inside and four yes reminder markers. These quantities do not double at 2–3 players; choose one controlled Firm’s sheet pair and shield.
  • Two Collection cubes, or four at two or three players.
  • Standard hand: 3 Organise, 4 Rally, 2 Smear (9 cards). Low-player hand: 6, 8, and 4 (18 cards).
  • One secret issue scoring card per human, held throughout the game, including at 2–3 players.
  • One final score: enacted policy gains and losses plus the final hand-rank bonus.
  • At Election 3, one final card count combining every ordinary and held Bonus card after Year 6 Cleanup. A low-player player ranks this human hand once, not each Firm separately.
Decided

Physical layout

The printable player sheets split each former 200 × 90 mm folio into two separate 100 × 90 mm pieces. The public sheet has an exact 40 × 61 mm well for the covered New Year pile and four 14 mm Collection wells; wells 3 and 4 retain their 2–3-player labels. Keep this sheet and returned Firm markers fully visible. New Year card faces remain hidden, with their total count public. Rotate the public sheet’s firm header 180° so it reads from across the table; New Year and Collection remain upright for the owner. Both sheet headers show only the firm identity, without Public or Private labels.

The private sheet has one exact 40 × 61 mm Private Priorities well and a 2 × 2 grid of four blank 18 × 18 mm spaces. The spaces have no labels, region names or printed no-vote icons. Each human receives four 16 × 16 mm yes markers, usable freely as entirely private reminders of desired regional outcomes. Empty spaces have no fixed meaning. Markers have no effect on actual votes, actions or scoring; there is no required placement or reset.

The standing Firm shield hides the private sheet and unused reminder markers. The exterior uses the Firm’s established colour and pattern, with its emblem, full name and motto repeated on the centre and both side panels, smaller on the wings. The complete player helper is printed on the pale interior. The overall flat size remains 280 × 110 mm, with folds at 80 mm and 200 mm. The 120 × 110 mm centre stays rectangular. Each 80 mm wing tapers to a 70 mm outer edge: its top drops 30 mm and its bottom rises 10 mm, with straight cuts from the fold corners. Fold the wings back at about right angles, then lean the shield back slightly until the sloping lower edges rest on the table (about 7°). Keep the top and player’s side open and place the private sheet’s top edge nearest the front. The public sheet sits beside the shield. The shield is sized for Private Priorities and reminders; the player still conceals their hand’s faces while keeping its total count public.

The six sheet pairs fit on two A4 portrait pages, three pairs per page; cut along the centre border to separate each pair. The 24 yes markers form a gapless 3 × 8 block on the shared token sheet, below Early Bird and the Year marker. The six shields use twelve A4 landscape pages, alternating exterior/interior for duplex printing with short-edge binding. Print one pair first to check alignment, then score from the exterior guides before cutting. Full assembly instructions are on the shield sheet. The shield interior replaces the separate player helper: Year sequence on the left wing, Lobby and Election in the centre, Operations and Keywords on the right wing. The helper uses 8 pt body text. The redundant one-scoring-card-per-player sentence and separate Party/Global law reminder paragraph are omitted.

Open prototype check: test shield stability and privacy from every seated opponent with the chosen cardstock. Check the backward lean, sightlines through the tapered sides and helper readability with the wings folded.

The printable state and player piece sheet provides three identical generic cards per Firm, plus Early Bird and calendar Year markers, with the 24 yes reminder markers on the same A4 landscape page. Use one card per controlled Firm; extra copies do not change the opening allowance. Supply Collection cubes separately. Scores are written on the final ledger, without score markers.
