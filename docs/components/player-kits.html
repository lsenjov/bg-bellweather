Lobbying firm kits

Player boards and bid ownership for Bellwether’s influence brokers.

Index/Lobbying firm kits

Each lobbying firm has a visually distinct identity kit. The first prototype supports two to six players. At four to six players, each human takes one kit. At two or three players, each human takes two kits and combines their bid components while retaining one player economy, operation-token reserve, agenda, and score.

Required kit

Per lobbying firm

Component Quantity Prototype specification Purpose
Player board 1 200 × 140 mm, two per A4 portrait sheet Identify the firm, park its bid components, hold a face-down agenda, and summarize the Campaign Round.
Counterbid cover 2 70 mm circular, labelled A and B Conceal one counterbid’s Clout and operation tokens during placement.
Opening-bid marker 1 50 mm circular, with the firm emblem and two large copies of its number Identify the firm responsible for a public opening bid from either side of the table while fitting the party-board opening area.
Score marker 1 25 mm circular, with the firm emblem and number Record one human player’s public point balance on the shared track. At two or three players, choose one of that player’s two firm markers and leave the other unused.

Manufacture: A printed 70 mm roundel is the cover artwork, not sufficient concealment by itself. Mount each roundel to an opaque cup, dome, or fabricated cover that hides differences in bid value, count, color, weight, and ordinary handling.

Player supply

Per human player

  • One concealed reserve shared by every lobbying firm they control.
  • At four to six players: 10 Clout, two Organise, two Rally, two Smear, one Court, and 5 public points.
  • At two or three players: 20 Clout, four Organise, four Rally, four Smear, two Court, and 10 public points.
  • One hidden scoring card, one cumulative point balance, and one active score marker. At two or three players, leave the second firm’s score marker unused.
  • One privacy screen or equivalent concealment for available Clout and uncommitted operation tokens.

Clout and operation tokens remain neutral rather than firm-colored because the Revolving Door and player gifts transfer them between players. Points are public and may also be given.
