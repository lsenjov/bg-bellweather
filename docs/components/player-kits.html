Lobbying firm kits

Player identity, annual access, and private card areas.

Index/Lobbying firm kits

At four to six players, each human controls one Firm. At two or three, each controls two Firms but keeps one hand, one set of counters, one scoring folio, and one score.

Decided

Firm identities

  1. One Fell Swoop Public Affairs
  2. Pairliament Partners
  3. TriumviRAT Advisory
  4. IVy League Public Affairs
  5. Vested Interests
  6. VI.P. Access Group
Required kit

Per Firm

  • One Firm identity board or folio.
  • One annual opening marker, returned during every Cleanup.
  • One score marker; a low-player player uses one of their two and leaves the other inactive.

The former three interchangeable identity cards are unnecessary. A Firm never owns a hand or point balance separately from its human player.

Player supply

Per human player

  • One private Operation hand and one visible New Year area.
  • Two Collection counters, doubled to four at two or three players.
  • Standard hand: 2 Organise, 4 Rally, 2 Smear, 2 Court. Low-player hand: 4, 8, 4, and 4.
  • Three ordered Election slots. Standard slots hold one card; low-player slots hold one registered pair with the first card physically locked as the Capital card.
  • One cumulative public point balance, starting at 5 or at 10 in a low-player game.
Open

Physical layout

The folio must distinguish the available hand from the unavailable New Year area without requiring a large face-up spread. Exact board geometry is open. The Ruleset 14 printable Firm boards and identity pieces remain historical print references until new artwork is prepared.
