Representation rules

  • Party color always identifies party state; low-saturation stationery colors identify Firms.
  • Firm markers repeat number, emblem, and pattern so Closure ownership never depends on color.
  • Collection counters must be visually distinct from Firm markers: they are player-owned action counters, not bids or party ownership.
  • Firm markers, Collection counters, and bonus-used markers all return or reset during annual Cleanup.
  • Closed parties, public Operation piles, New Year card counts, Early Bird, and points remain readable to the whole table.
