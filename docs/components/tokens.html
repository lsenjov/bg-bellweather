Representation rules

  • Party color always identifies party state; low-saturation stationery colors identify Firms.
  • Each Coalition summary token is a 14 mm party-color disc carrying only its emblem. Summary positions are interchangeable and Coalition Targets remain authoritative.
  • Firm markers repeat number, emblem, and pattern so Closure ownership never depends on color.
  • Score markers repeat their Firm’s number, emblem, stationery color, and pattern so a shared score track remains readable without color. Collection counters remain neutral.
  • Collection counters must be visually distinct from Firm markers: they are player-owned action counters, not bids or party ownership.
  • Firm markers return as their parties close; Collection counters return during Cleanup.
  • A Firm marker on a party means open. Face-up returned markers show the Closure count and remain unavailable until next year.
  • Open parties, active coalitions, public Operation piles, New Year card counts, Early Bird, and points remain readable to the whole table.
