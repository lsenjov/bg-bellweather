Representation rules

Print agents. Agent pairs are single-sided fold-and-glue; the separate vote reminders retain their existing duplex printing. No mirroring marker or numbered prophecy markers are supplied. Election prophecies tuck beneath policies; party and firm prophecies sit beside their targets.

  • Party color always identifies party state; low-saturation stationery colors identify Firms.
  • Firm markers repeat number, emblem, and pattern so Closure ownership never depends on color.
  • Collection cubes replace printed counters and must be visually distinct from Firm markers: they are player-owned action counters, not bids or party ownership.
  • Firm markers return as their parties close; Collection counters refresh after any Election and before New Year release.
  • A Firm marker on a party means open. Face-up returned markers show the Closure count and remain unavailable until next year.
  • Open parties, pending policies, enacted laws, public Operation piles, New Year card counts, Early Bird, and points remain readable to the whole table.
