Republic of Bellweather / pre-prototype

LadderBidding

Rival lobbying firms influence six animal political parties through concealed card stacks, reusable operations, and a political Revolving Door.

Working title. Auction core, card-based bid economy, territorial district map, unlimited Support actions, party bonuses, coalition scoring, player gifts, twelve-round clock, and three–Election Day scoring committed; timing balance remains open.

Start with the political vision and current influence rules, then follow the open questions that block the first complete prototype.
