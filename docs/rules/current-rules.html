Index/Current rules

Across twelve years, rival lobbying firms establish party access, perform political Operations, collect public card piles for later years, and close their own contacts before three Elections decide Bellweather’s future.

Document legend

Decided is part of the current playable design. Hypothesis is a candidate to test. Open has no selected answer.

Decided

Players, duration, and objective

The game supports two to six human players. It lasts twelve years. Hold an Election after Years 4, 8, and 12; after the third Election, every player tied for the highest cumulative point balance shares the win.

Shape party Support toward the three district–party objectives on each of your hidden scoring cards. Build additional Capital scoring by keeping the three parties named on one scoring card present in Bellweather Centre.

Working title: Ladder Bidding. The auction mechanic has been removed; the title remains open to change.

Decided

Components

  • One sixteen-district Bellweather map with fifty-seven Support spaces.
  • Six parties, unlimited Support for each, six Coalition Target markers, and public areas for yearly Firm markers, Operation piles, and open/closed state.
  • Six Firm identities with one yearly opening marker each.
  • Operation cards: Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court.
  • Two Collection counters per player, doubled to four at two or three players.
  • Twenty-four scoring cards, player boards or folios, one visible New Year area per player, an Early Bird marker, a Year/Election tracker, and a public score track.

Leverage cards, Bluff cards, gifts, bid markers, counterbid pieces, and the Pecking Order are not used. Their former specification is retained in the Ruleset 14 component archive.

A low-player player controls two Firms but retains one hand, one set of Collection counters, one point balance, and one turn whenever that player is active. Randomly choose the first Early Bird.

Give every player three ordered scoring slots, one for each Election. At four to six players, shuffle all twenty-four scoring cards once and deal one card to each slot. At two or three players, form the twelve registered adjacent odd–even pairs—SC-01 with SC-02 through SC-23 with SC-24—shuffle those face-down packets once, and deal one pair to each slot. The first card in each registered pair is that player’s Capital card for the Election; both cards still score their ordinary district objectives. Players may inspect all three of their own slots but may not exchange or reorder them.

Put one Support from every party in Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood. Put Coalition Targets on their neutral setup positions. Place the Year marker on Year 1 and each player’s score marker on their starting points.

Decided

Public and private information

Operation hands and unscored scoring cards are private. Hand size, New Year area size, Operation cards in party piles, Firm ownership, Collection counters, points, Support, Court Support, Coalition Targets, claimed party bonuses, and open/closed party state are public.

When you Collect or receive cards from a Closure, place them in a discard pile or New Year area in front of you. They cannot be used during the current year. The pile does not need to remain fully spread for constant recounting; players may remember or infer its contents.

Decided

The twelve-year structure

Every year has three stages, followed by an Election after Years 4, 8, and 12:

  1. Party Openings: players establish Firm access to different parties.
  2. Lobby actions: players take clockwise turns to Operate, Collect, Close, or Pass.
  3. Cleanup: close and award remaining parties, release New Year cards, return yearly pieces, reset bonuses, and assign the next Early Bird.
  4. Election, when scheduled: after Cleanup, reveal the assigned scoring slot and resolve the Election.
Decided

1. Party Openings

Beginning with the Early Bird, each opening turn places one of the active player’s unused Firm markers on a party with no Firm marker. A party can have at most one Firm marker that year. The marker establishes which player owns that party’s Closure; it is not a bid and commits no Operation cards.

  • Two players: use ABBA. Each player opens two different parties.
  • Three players: use ABCCBA. Each player opens two different parties.
  • Four to six players: go once clockwise from the Early Bird.

After the final opening, the Early Bird takes the first Lobby action. The snake order applies only to Party Openings; Lobby turns always move clockwise.

Decided

2. Lobby actions

On your turn, take exactly one action. Then play passes clockwise unless that action ends the year.

Operate

Play one Operation card from your hand on one open party, choose its complete parameters, and resolve it immediately. Leave the card face up in that party’s public pile. You may then finish the Operate action or play another Operation card at the same party, choosing it against the updated board. You may make the same choice again after the second card. A third card ends the Operate action automatically.

You do not choose later cards or targets in advance, and no other player acts between cards. Once the first card resolves, that party remains fixed for the complete Operate action and you cannot take a different Lobby action until you finish.

You may claim at most one party bonus during the complete Operate action, even when playing three cards. Each of a party’s two bonuses may be claimed at most once during the year. Collecting its pile does not reset those bonuses.

Collect

Spend one of your Collection counters and choose an open party with a non-empty Operation pile. Take the complete pile into your New Year area. The party remains open and can receive new Operations. Any player may Collect any party, including one they opened or previously collected.

Close

Choose an open party bearing one of your Firm markers. Take its complete Operation pile into your New Year area and mark the party closed for the rest of the year. No further Operation cards can be played there.

You cannot Close during your first Lobby turn of the year, even if that first turn was a Pass. Only the player who placed the opening Firm marker can Close that party. If a Closure makes strictly more than half of the opened parties closed, the year ends immediately, even in the middle of the turn order.

Pass

Do nothing. If every player passes consecutively, the year ends immediately. Close every remaining open party and award each complete Operation pile to the player whose Firm marker opened that party. Any non-Pass action breaks the consecutive-pass sequence.

Who becomes Early Bird?

The player whose action ends the year becomes the next Early Bird. This is the player who created the strict majority of closed parties or completed the all-player pass sequence.

Crown Road connects Harbormouth to Bellweather Centre, Canal Ward connects Grand Market to the Centre, and Old Quarter connects Ironwood to the Centre. Bellweather Centre has capacity 3. Every party has unlimited Support, but every placement still requires a free district spot.

Decided

Court Support and coalitions

Each party board has one Court space for every other party and one persistent Coalition Target. After Court Support changes, compare the five spaces. If one has a strict lead, point the acting party’s Coalition Target at that party. If the lead is tied, leave the target where it is.

Two parties form a coalition while their Coalition Targets point at each other. In ordinary district scoring, drawn Support belonging to either coalition partner scores for an objective naming the other. Coalition partners never count for Capital scoring.

Decided

3. Cleanup

  1. Close every party still open and give its complete pile to the player whose Firm marker opened it.
  2. Move every card in every player’s New Year area into that player’s hand. Those cards are now available.
  3. Return all Collection counters and Firm markers to their owners.
  4. Reset every party bonus to available.
  5. Give the Early Bird marker to the player whose action ended the year.

After a non-Election year, begin the next Party Openings. After Years 4, 8, and 12, resolve the Election only after this complete Cleanup. Year 12 uses the same Cleanup; there is no special rule for stranded or newly released cards.

Decided

4. Election

  1. Reveal agendas: reveal only this Election’s scoring card or registered pair. Leave earlier agendas face up and later agendas concealed.
  2. Draw every non-Centre district: randomize its Support and draw three from each capacity-6 district, two from each capacity-4 district, and one from each capacity-2 district, or all Support if fewer are present.
  3. Retain the result: keep only drawn Support in that district and return undrawn Support to supply. Use one shared draw for every objective naming that district.
  4. Base district score: each drawn Support matching the objective’s party or reciprocal coalition partner scores 1 point. Add all objectives across the revealed card or pair.
  5. Relative-seat modifier: at four to six players, add the positive referenced player’s base score and subtract the negative referenced player’s base score. At two or three players, ignore both references.
  6. Capital score: inspect Bellweather Centre without drawing or removing anything. Use the three different parties on the player’s Capital card. Score 0 for zero or one present, 1 for two present, or 3 for all three present. Multiple Support from one named party count once; coalition partners do not count.
  7. Total: add base district score, relative-seat modifier, and Capital score to the player’s cumulative points.
  8. Clear Court: return all Court Support to supply without moving Coalition Targets.

Bellweather Centre never participates in the ordinary draw, thinning, objective scoring, or coalition scoring. Capital scoring is an additional score after the relative-seat modifier; both cards in a low-player pair still contribute ordinary district scores, but only the pair’s first registered card supplies its three Capital parties.

After Elections 1 and 2, begin the next year with the Early Bird already assigned during Cleanup. After Election 3, the game ends and highest cumulative points win.
