Index/Current rules

Across six years, rival lobbying firms establish party access, perform political Operations, collect public card piles for later years, and close their own contacts before three Elections decide Bellweather’s future.

Decided

Players, duration, and objective

The game supports two to six human players. It lasts six years. Hold an Election after Years 2, 4, and 6; after the third Election’s final card rank bonus, every player tied for the highest cumulative point balance shares the win.

Years 1, 3, and 5 end at thematic midterm boundaries. A midterm adds no phase or mechanical effect: complete ordinary Cleanup and begin the next year.

Shape party Support toward the three district–party objectives on each of your hidden scoring cards. Build additional Capital scoring by keeping the three parties named on one scoring card present in Bellweather Centre. Preserve ordinary and Bonus cards for the final ranking, but weigh them against the actions they could provide.

Working title: Ladder Bidding. The auction mechanic has been removed; the title remains open to change.

Decided

Components

  • One sixteen-district Bellweather map with fifty-seven Support spaces and a three-pair Coalition summary tracker.
  • Six parties, unlimited Support for each, six Coalition Target markers, six Coalition summary tokens, and public areas for yearly Firm markers, Operation piles, and three blank Bonus-card spaces per party. The current two Bonus cards occupy any two spaces.
  • Six Firm identities with one yearly opening marker each.
  • Operation cards: Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court; plus twelve unique collectible Bonus cards.
  • Two Collection counters per player, doubled to four at two or three players.
  • Twenty-four scoring cards, player boards or folios, one covered New Year area per player with its total count visible, an Early Bird marker, a Year/Election tracker, and a public score track.

Leverage cards, Bluff cards, gifts, bid markers, counterbid pieces, and the Pecking Order are not used. Their former specification is retained in the Ruleset 14 component archive.

Decided

Public and private information

Operation and Bonus cards in hand and unscored scoring cards are private. Combined hand size, combined New Year area size, Operation cards in party piles, Bonus cards at their home parties, Firm markers on open parties or returned to players, Collection counters, points, Support, Court Support, Coalition Targets, and the Coalition summary are public.

When you Collect or receive cards from a Closure, place them in your New Year area. They cannot be used during the current year. The exact contents are private, though the total card count remains public.

Decided

The six-year structure

Every year has three stages, followed by an Election after Years 2, 4, and 6:

  1. Party Openings: players establish Firm access to different parties.
  2. Lobby actions: players take clockwise turns to Operate, Collect, Close, or Pass.
  3. Cleanup: close and award remaining parties, release New Year cards, return yearly pieces, and assign the next Early Bird.
  4. Election, when scheduled: after Cleanup, reveal the assigned scoring slot and resolve the Election.
Decided

1. Party Openings

Beginning with the Early Bird, each opening turn places one of the active player’s unused Firm markers on a party with no Firm marker. A party can have at most one Firm marker that year. The marker establishes which player owns that party’s Closure; it is not a bid and commits no Operation cards. A Firm marker on a party means that party is open. Without one, it is unavailable for Lobby actions.

  • Two players: use ABBA. Each player opens two different parties.
  • Three players: use ABCCBA. Each player opens two different parties.
  • Four to six players: go once clockwise from the Early Bird.

After the final opening, the Early Bird takes the first Lobby action. The snake order applies only to Party Openings; Lobby turns always move clockwise.

Decided

2. Lobby actions

On your turn, take exactly one action. Then play passes clockwise unless that action ends the year.

Operate

Play one ordinary Operation card or one Bonus card from your hand on one open party, choose its complete parameters, and resolve it immediately. Leave an ordinary card face up in that party’s public pile. Return a successfully played Bonus card immediately to its home party. You may then finish the Operate action or play another card at the same party, choosing it against the updated board. You may make the same choice again after the second card. A third card ends the Operate action automatically.

You do not choose later cards or targets in advance, and no other player acts between cards. Once the first card resolves, that party remains fixed for the complete Operate action and you cannot take a different Lobby action until you finish.

A Bonus card is a complete standalone action and requires no ordinary card. It consumes one of the one-to-three card slots, never enters an Operation pile, and may be held indefinitely. Any number of cards in one Operate may be Bonus cards. A Bonus card may be played on its home party or that party’s current reciprocal coalition partner; a one-way or former Target is not enough. The open party being operated is the acting party for every piece and Target named by the card.

Resolve a Bonus card atomically. If its baseline or additional effect cannot resolve completely, the play is illegal and neither the board nor card location changes. A successful card returns home immediately and may be collected again later that year, but returning to an already closed home party never triggers a retroactive award.

Collect

Spend one of your Collection counters and choose an open party with a non-empty Operation pile. Take the complete pile into your New Year area and optionally take at most one Bonus card currently at that party. The party remains open and can receive new Operations. Any player may Collect any party, including one they opened or previously collected.

Close

Choose an open party bearing one of your Firm markers. Take its complete Operation pile into your New Year area, optionally take at most one Bonus card currently there, then return the Firm marker face up to your play area. The Bonus choice remains available even when the Operation pile is empty. Without its marker, the party is closed and accepts no more Lobby actions that year. A returned marker cannot open another party until the next year.

You cannot Close during your first Lobby turn of the year, even if that first turn was a Pass. Only the player who placed the opening Firm marker can Close that party. Returned Firm markers show the public Closure count. If a Closure makes strictly more than half of the opened parties closed, the year ends immediately, even in the middle of the turn order. Close every remaining party and resolve each opener’s optional Bonus-card choice before Cleanup, as for an all-player Pass ending.

Pass

Do nothing. If every player passes consecutively, the year ends immediately. Close every remaining open party, award each complete Operation pile to the player whose Firm marker opened that party, and return those markers. Before Cleanup, each recipient independently may take at most one Bonus card from each party they just received, including a party with an empty pile. Resolve these choices in party order. Any non-Pass action breaks the consecutive-pass sequence.

Who becomes Early Bird?

The player whose action ends the year becomes the next Early Bird. This is the player who created the strict majority of closed parties or completed the all-player pass sequence.

Decided

Court Support and coalitions

Each party board has one Court space for every other party and one persistent Coalition Target. After Court Support changes, compare the five spaces. If one has a strict lead, point the acting party’s Coalition Target at that party. If the lead is tied, leave the target where it is.

Two parties form a coalition while their Coalition Targets point at each other. In ordinary district scoring, drawn Support belonging to either coalition partner scores for an objective naming the other. Coalition partners never count for Capital scoring.

After each card finishes resolving, the acting player reconciles the map’s Coalition summary with all six Targets. Put reciprocal partners together on any empty joined pair and put every other party’s summary token in the No coalition reserve. The Targets remain authoritative if the summary disagrees.

Decided

3. Cleanup

  1. Close every party still open, give its complete pile to the player whose Firm marker opened it, return that marker, and let that opener optionally take at most one Bonus card from that party. Complete every opener’s choice before continuing.
  2. Move every ordinary and Bonus card in every player’s New Year area into that player’s private hand. Those cards are now available.
  3. Return all Collection counters. All Firm markers are now back with their owners and available for the next Party Openings.
  4. Give the Early Bird marker to the player whose action ended the year.

After a non-Election year, begin the next Party Openings; the thematic midterm after Years 1, 3, and 5 changes nothing. After Years 2, 4, and 6, resolve the Election only after this complete Cleanup. Year 6 uses the same Cleanup, and cards released from New Year during that Cleanup are in hand for the final card ranking.

Decided

4. Election

  1. Reveal agendas: reveal only this Election’s scoring card or registered pair. Leave earlier agendas face up and later agendas concealed.
  2. Draw every non-Centre district: randomize its Support and draw three from each capacity-6 district, two from each capacity-4 district, and one from each capacity-2 district, or all Support if fewer are present.
  3. Retain the result: keep only drawn Support in that district and return undrawn Support to supply. Use one shared draw for every objective naming that district.
  4. Base district score: each drawn Support matching the objective’s party or reciprocal coalition partner scores 1 point. Add all objectives across the revealed card or pair.
  5. Relative-seat modifier: at four to six players, add the positive referenced player’s base score and subtract the negative referenced player’s base score. At two or three players, ignore both references.
  6. Capital score: inspect Bellweather Centre without drawing or removing anything. Use the three different parties on the player’s Capital card. Score 0 for zero or one present, 1 for two present, or 3 for all three present. Multiple Support from one named party count once; coalition partners do not count.
  7. Final card rank, Election 3 only: after Year 6 Cleanup, count every ordinary Operation card and held Bonus card in each human player’s combined hand. Bonus cards at their home parties do not count. For each player, count the other players whose final card total is less than or equal to theirs and score that many points. Equivalently, rank totals into slots from 0 through one less than the player count; tied players use the highest slot occupied by their tie.
  8. Total: add base district score, relative-seat modifier, Capital score, and any final card rank bonus to the player’s cumulative points.
  9. Clear Court: return all Court Support to supply without moving Coalition Targets.

Bellweather Centre never participates in the ordinary draw, thinning, objective scoring, or coalition scoring. Capital scoring is an additional score after the relative-seat modifier; both cards in a low-player pair still contribute ordinary district scores, but only the pair’s first registered card supplies its three Capital parties.

A low-player player ranks their one combined human hand, not two separate Firm supplies. Elections 1 and 2 have no final card rank bonus. After those Elections, begin the next year with the Early Bird already assigned during Cleanup. After Election 3 and its final ranking, the game ends and highest cumulative points win.
