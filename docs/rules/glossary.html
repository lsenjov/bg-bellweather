Glossary

Stable words reduce accidental rule changes.

Index/Glossary

The working vocabulary distinguishes the auction loop, public party state, hidden scoring agendas, and Election Day results.

Working vocabulary
Term Current use Status Avoid confusing with
Base district score The points a player earns from the three district–party objectives on one scoring card before applying its positive and negative seat references. Rules term Cumulative score or a score copied through a seat reference
Bid A physically escrowed commitment of Clout and any optional operatives to one influence contest. Rules term A payment to the targeted party
Campaign Round One complete sequence of choosing contest targets, placing opening bids and counterbids, revealing all bids, resolving every contest, and completing every Revolving Door. Rules term One contest, one opening bid, or Election Day
Clout Reusable political leverage that ranks bids and transfers intact through the Revolving Door. Themed rules term Literal money, points, or party support
Coalition The mutual state formed while two parties’ overtures point toward each other. Themed rules term The Many Wings Coalition party or a one-way overture
Contest One party or Press Perch opportunity in which lobbying houses may place bids. Rules term Round or turn
Counterbid A concealed, revisable bid placed after opening contests are locked. Rules term The public opening bid
District One named map area containing a limited number of spots for party Support. Two districts are neighboring only when they share a black border segment; meeting at a point or across Crownwater does not count. Rules term A party or contest target
Election Day The scoring procedure after a campaign period in which scoring cards are revealed and current district Support is sampled. Themed rules term A Campaign Round or an action that changes Support
Lobbying house The political influence organization controlled by one player. Themed rules term A party
Operative A reusable card committed to a bid and executed at a party or the Press Perch. Themed rules term The player or the operation family
Operation family Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court; each has one baseline effect available at every party. Rules term A party bonus
Overture A party’s one-way pointer, which Court may set to a different party. Two reciprocal overtures form a coalition. Themed rules term A coalition or a lobbying-house agreement
Party bonus One of two favored-operation effects offered by a party. Each may be claimed with one matching operative per contest, with bids receiving first refusal from highest rank to lowest; most resolve immediately, while Night Parliament’s resolve after ordinary operatives. Rules term An operation’s universal baseline effect
Party One of six animal political institutions that serves as an ordinary influence target. Themed rules term A player or lobbying house
Pecking Order The persistent media order that determines party-contest resolution. Themed rules term Bid rank, player order, electoral support, or score
Press Perch The special contest target that displays and changes the Pecking Order and resolves before every party. Themed rules term A seventh political party
Revolving Door The transfer after a contest in which each complete bid moves to the owner of the bid immediately below it and the lowest moves to the highest. Themed rules term Party recruitment or a reward paid by the board
Scoring card A hidden player card containing three district–party objectives plus positive and negative relative-seat references. Rules term An operative card, party board, or public objective
Support Public party pieces that occupy limited district spots and return to their party’s reserve when removed. Rules term Clout, score, a lobbying house, or votes already cast
Archived vocabulary

Ladder-era terms

Ladder, position, and rung described an early shared-track hypothesis. They remain in historical documents and the working title but have no current rules meaning.

Style rule

Use one term for one game concept. If a component and an action share a name, change one before writing more rules.
