Glossary

Stable words reduce accidental rule changes.

Index/Glossary

The current vocabulary distinguishes access to a party, actions taken through it, cards waiting for the next year, and Election scoring.

Working vocabulary
TermCurrent useAvoid confusing with
Capital cardThe scoring card whose three parties determine a player’s Bellweather Centre score. It is the only card at four to six players and the fixed first card of a registered pair at two or three.A separate component or a card chosen at Election time
Capital scoreAn additional Election score of 0 for zero or one named party present in Bellweather Centre, 1 for two, or 3 for all three.Ordinary district scoring or coalition matching
Bonus cardOne of twelve unique collectible cards, with two homes at each party. It provides one atomic standalone action, may be held indefinitely, and returns home immediately after a successful play.An ordinary Operation card or a recurring party-board state
CleanupThe end-of-year procedure that closes remaining parties, resolves their optional Bonus-card awards, releases New Year cards, returns Collection counters, and assigns next year’s Early Bird.An Election; scheduled Elections happen afterward
CloseA Lobby action available only to a party’s opener after that player’s first Lobby turn. The opener takes its pile and optionally one available Bonus card into New Year, then returns its Firm marker, leaving the party unavailable for the year.Collect, which leaves the party open
CoalitionThe mutual state formed while two parties’ Coalition Targets point at each other. Coalition partners match ordinary district objectives but never Capital parties.The Many Wings Coalition party
Coalition summary tokenOne of six emblem-only party tokens used to copy reciprocal Coalition Target pairs onto the physical map tracker.A Coalition Target or the authoritative game state
CollectA Lobby action that spends a Collection counter to move one open party’s complete nonempty Operation pile and optionally one available Bonus card into the acting player’s New Year area. The party remains open.Close or drawing cards from a deck
Collection counterOne of a player’s two annual Collect uses, doubled to four at two or three players. It returns during Cleanup.A bid marker or ownership marker
CourtAn Operation that places acting-party Support on another party’s Court space and may change the acting party’s Coalition Target.A coalition or direct target selection
Early BirdThe player who acts first during Party Openings and the Lobby. The player whose action or final pass ends the year becomes Early Bird next year.The last player guaranteed an action in the current year
ElectionThe scoring and whole-map thinning procedure after Cleanup in Years 2, 4, and 6.A year or Lobby action
Firm markerA player-identity marker placed to open a party and returned when it closes. Markers returned during the Lobby show the public Closure count and cannot be reused until the next year.A competitive bid or a separate Closed marker
Final card rank bonusThe Election 3 score for a human player’s ordinary and held Bonus cards after Year 6 Cleanup: one point for every other player whose final count is less than or equal to theirs. Ties therefore take the highest occupied rank.Separate Firm hands or Bonus cards at party homes
LobbyThe annual clockwise action stage in which players Operate, Collect, Close, or Pass.A physical room or the pregame lobby
MidtermThe thematic boundary after Years 1, 3, and 5. It has no additional procedure or mechanical effect beyond ordinary Cleanup.An Election or separate game phase
New Year areaA private area holding ordinary and Bonus cards gained by Collect or Closure. Its total size is public, its cards are unavailable during the current year, and they enter hand during Cleanup.An available hand or discard pile
OperateA Lobby action that resolves one ordinary Operation or held Bonus card immediately, then may resolve up to two more one at a time at the same open party.Each individual card within the action
Operation cardA reusable Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court card resolved at a party and then left in that party’s public pile.A scoring card
Operation pileThe public cards accumulated at an open party until a player Collects them or the party closes.A player’s New Year area
Party OpeningPlacement of a Firm marker on a party without one. The annual order is ABBA at two players, ABCCBA at three, and once clockwise at four to six.A bid, ownership of the party, or the Lobby turn order
PassA Lobby action that does nothing. A complete uninterrupted orbit of passes closes all open parties and ends the year.Withdrawing from the rest of the year
Scoring cardA hidden agenda with three district–party objectives and relative-seat references. Each player has one per Election at four to six players or a registered pair at two or three.An Operation card
SupportPublic party pieces that occupy limited map or Court spaces.A lobbying firm, Operation card, or score
YearOne Party Openings stage, one Lobby, and one Cleanup. An Election follows every second year.An Election cycle, which contains two years
