Glossary

Ruleset 29

Current rules · Index
Firm
A player’s supernatural identity and its agents and firm cards. Each human controls one Firm, with two opening markers at two or three players.
Opening marker
Yearly access marker on a party; its owner receives its Closure. A party with one is open to all players.
Agent
A Firm’s map piece, separate from Support capacity. Eligible agents authorise common Operations. Agents normally persist through Elections and year changes.
Common card
An Organise, Rally or Smear card that enters the acting party’s pile after play.
Firm card
A Firm’s personal movement or ability card, normally returning through New Year; includes Hecate’s placed prophecies.
Hecate reward card
One of six reusable Operations earned by prophecy success, requiring a sister and returning directly to her reward supply after play.
Support
Party pieces occupying district spaces; each survivor casts one regional policy vote.
Issue priority
A fixed ordering of Healthcare, Transport, Education, Housing, Environment and Fiscal. Party orders determine votes; private player orders determine points.
Policy
A regional proposal with one + issue, one − issue, six party votes and a Party or Global effect if enacted.
Law
A passed policy. Enacted effects are permanent. Party laws modify only the recipient’s Operations; duplicate effects do not stack for that party. Global scoring laws affect everyone and do not stack.
Party law
A law awarded to the party with the most surviving For votes in its region when passed; tied supporters use leftmost printed order. It stays with that party and applies only when that party is acting, regardless of the player.
Global law
A law affecting all players. Muted Priorities and Broad Interests are the two Global effects.
Centre
The fourth region, containing one capacity-3 district that is never thinned.
Operation
Organise, Rally or Smear. An Operate action resolves one to three cards sequentially; each card may use a different open party.
Party card
A collectible reusable card played from hand and returned home on success. A printed baseline receives applicable laws; standalone effects use their own text.
Extra
A follow-up to the original Operation. The player orders the party-card extra and law extras together.
New Year area
Private collected cards, counted publicly, unavailable until New Year, after Closures and any Election.
Election
After Years 2, 4 and 6, thin non-Centre districts and vote on four policies. Score only after the final Election.
Hand rank
Final points equal to the number of other human players holding fewer or the same number of common and party cards; exclude firm cards and Hecate reward cards.
