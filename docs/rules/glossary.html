Glossary

Stable words reduce accidental rule changes.

Index/Glossary

The working vocabulary distinguishes the auction loop, public party state, hidden scoring agendas, and Election Day results.

Working vocabulary
Term Current use Status Avoid confusing with
Base district score The points a player earns from every district–party objective on their one scoring card or compatible low-player pair before applying any positive and negative seat references. Rules term Cumulative score or a score copied through a seat reference
Bid A physically escrowed stack of Leverage cards and any optional Bluff and operation cards committed to one influence contest. Rules term A payment to the targeted party
Bluff card An inert reusable bid card that may be hidden in a bid to obscure its operation-card count. It transfers with the bid through the Revolving Door and may be given while behind a player’s screen. Rules term A zero-Leverage counterbid or a card with an operation
Campaign Round One complete sequence of creating the automatic Pecking Order auction, choosing party-contest targets, placing opening bids and counterbids, revealing all bids, resolving every contest, and completing every Revolving Door. Rules term One contest, one opening bid, or Election Day
Leverage Reusable political leverage represented by one-Leverage cards. Leverage ranks bids and may transfer through the Revolving Door or, while behind a player’s screen, through gifts. Themed rules term Literal money, points, or party support
Firm identity card One of three interchangeable cards carrying a firm’s large emblem, name, number, and pattern. Use one beside an opening bid or face up on a counterbid stack; leave it with its firm when the transferable bid cards Revolve. Component term A transferable bid card or score marker
Coalition The mutual state formed while two parties’ coalition targets point toward each other. On Election Day, drawn Support belonging to either partner matches objectives naming either party. Themed rules term The Many Wings Coalition party or a one-way coalition target
Court An operation that places one acting-party Support on another party’s Court space, then moves the acting party’s Coalition Target if one Court space has a unique lead. Themed rules term A coalition, a legal tribunal, or direct target selection
Court Support Party Support placed on prospective partners’ Court spaces. It is public, does not count as district Support, and returns to supply after Election Day without moving Coalition Targets. Rules term District Support or a Coalition Target token
Contest One automatic Pecking Order auction or one player-opened party opportunity in which lobbying firms may place bids. Rules term Round or turn
Counterbid A concealed, revisable bid placed after opening contests are locked. Rules term The public opening bid
Deal An agreement between players. The game does not enforce it; players may keep or break promises and enforce deals socially. Rules term A completed gift or Night Parliament’s After-Hours Deal bonus
District One named map area containing a limited number of spots for party Support. Two districts are neighboring only when they share a black border segment; meeting at a point or across Crownwater does not count. Rules term A party or contest target
Election Day The scoring procedure after a campaign period in which scoring cards are revealed and current district Support is sampled. Themed rules term A Campaign Round or an action that changes Support
First opener The human player holding the Early Bird marker who is A in the round’s opening order. Choose one randomly for the first round; thereafter, use the player who placed the previous round’s highest active Pecking Order bid. Opening turns follow ABBA at two players, ABCCBA at three, or one clockwise turn each at four to six. If no active Pecking Order bid remained, keep the existing first opener. The Revolving Door does not transfer this role. Rules term The first party in the Pecking Order or the recipient of the highest bid through the Revolving Door
Lobbying firm One of six political influence organizations whose identity kit marks a player’s bids. One human controls one firm at four to six players and two firms at two or three players, while retaining one player economy and score. Themed rules term A party
Operation card A neutral reusable 40 × 61 mm Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court card that may be given while behind a player’s screen or committed to a bid and executed at a party or the Pecking Order. Themed rules term A scoring card, player marker, or party Support
Operation family Organise, Rally, Smear, or Court; each has one baseline effect available at every party. Rules term A party bonus
Coalition Target A party’s persistent one-way pointer. A unique Court Support leader moves it; a tie and Election Day cleanup leave it in place. Two reciprocal Coalition Targets form a coalition. Themed rules term A coalition or a lobbying-firm agreement
Party bonus One of two favored-operation effects offered by a party. Each may be claimed with one matching operation card per contest, with bids receiving first refusal from highest rank to lowest; most resolve immediately, while Night Parliament’s resolve after every contest and Revolving Door finishes. Rules term An operation’s universal baseline effect
Party One of six animal political institutions that serves as an ordinary influence target. Themed rules term A player or lobbying firm
Pecking Order The board and persistent party sequence that determine party-contest resolution, as well as the mandatory no-opening-bid auction that changes that sequence before the parties resolve. Its operation cards execute from the lowest active bid to the highest, then its bids Revolve normally. Themed rules term Bid rank, player order, electoral support, or score
Points A player’s public point balance. Players begin with 5 points, doubled to 10 at two or three players; gifts and Election Day results change the balance, which may become negative. Rules term Leverage, Support, or a base district score
Revolving Door The transfer after a contest in which each complete bid moves to the owner of the bid immediately below it and the lowest moves to the highest. Themed rules term Party recruitment or a reward paid by the board
Scoring card A hidden player card containing three district–party objectives plus positive and negative relative-seat references. Each player holds two compatible cards at two or three players and one card at four to six. Rules term An operation card, party board, or public objective
Support Public party pieces from an unlimited supply that occupy limited district spots and leave the map when removed. Rules term Leverage, score, a lobbying firm, or votes already cast
Archived vocabulary

Ladder-era terms

Ladder, position, and rung described an early shared-track hypothesis. Press Perch was the former name for the Pecking Order board and auction. These terms remain in historical documents but have no current rules meaning.

Style rule

Use one term for one game concept. If a component and an action share a name, change one before writing more rules.
