Worked examples

ABBA applies only to Party Openings

State: Ada is Early Bird in a two-player game. Each player must open two different parties.

Resolution: Party Openings occur Ada, Bert, Bert, Ada. When the Lobby begins, turns simply continue clockwise from Ada: Ada, Bert, Ada, Bert, and so on.

Why: The snake order gives later players a fair opening choice without changing the ordinary action orbit.

Operations resolve before the next card is chosen

State: Ada begins an Operate action at Riverworks by playing Rally. Both Riverworks bonuses are ready.

Resolution: Ada chooses the Rally’s target and resolves it immediately. She may now finish or choose a second card at Riverworks against the changed board. After resolving a second card she may finish or choose a third; resolving the third ends the action automatically. No other player acts during this sequence, and Ada may use no more than one Riverworks bonus across it.

Why: The one-bonus limit belongs to the Operate action, not to each card.

Night Shift is an immediate double Rally

State: Ada plays Rally at Night Parliament and chooses to use its ready Night Shift bonus.

Resolution: Resolve the ordinary Rally, then immediately add a second Night Parliament Support in the same district if it still has room. There is no delayed marker or later resolution window.

Why: Night Shift is provisionally a double Rally for the yearly structure.

Collect takes a pile out of circulation

State: Riverworks is open with four Operation cards in its pile. Bert has a Collection counter available.

Resolution: Bert spends one counter and moves the entire pile to his face-up New Year area. Riverworks remains open and may receive more Operations later this year. Bert cannot use the collected cards until Cleanup releases them to his hand.

Why: Collection harvests the current pile without reserving or closing the party.

Only the opener may Close

State: Ada opened Honeycomb. On Bert’s turn, Honeycomb holds a valuable pile. Ada has already taken one Lobby turn this year.

Resolution: Bert cannot Close Honeycomb, although he may Collect its pile. On Ada’s next turn she may Close it, take its current pile into her New Year area, and remove her Firm marker. No one may Operate or Collect there for the rest of the year.

Why: Closure and automatic closure always award the pile to the player who placed the opening Firm marker.

A strict majority ends the Lobby mid-orbit

State: Six parties opened. Two are already closed. Ada legally closes a third.

Resolution: Three of six is not more than half, so play continues. If Bert then closes a fourth, the Lobby ends immediately. Unclosed party piles are awarded to their openers; later players do not finish the orbit.

Why: The end trigger is a strict majority of opened parties, checked after each closure.

Consecutive passes close every party

State: In a four-player game, Cato passes, then Dara, Ada, and Bert also pass without any intervening action.

Resolution: All open parties close immediately and each pile goes to that party’s opener. Bert, the player whose pass ended the year, receives the Early Bird marker during Cleanup.

Why: Any Operate, Collect, or Close breaks the consecutive-pass chain; the player who actually ends the year becomes next year’s Early Bird.

Cleanup precedes Election scoring

State: Year 4 has ended and Ada collected a Rally card during that year.

Resolution: Complete Cleanup first: award remaining piles, release New Year cards such as Ada’s Rally to hand, return Firm markers and Collection counters, reset bonuses, and move Early Bird. Then reveal Election 1 agendas and score. The released Rally cannot affect that Election because there is no Lobby action between Cleanup and scoring.

Why: Elections occur after the ordinary year boundary, leaving Cleanup uniform in all twelve years.

The first card fixes low-player Capital scoring

State: Bert’s registered Election pair is SC-05 followed by SC-06. Bellweather Centre contains two parties named on SC-05 and all three parties named on SC-06.

Resolution: Both cards score their ordinary district objectives. Only SC-05 supplies Capital parties, so Bert gains 1 Capital point for having two of its three parties present. He cannot choose SC-06 for 3 points.

Why: The first card in a registered pair is locked as its Capital card when the pair is formed.
