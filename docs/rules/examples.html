Changing parties within a turn

A common Rally uses an eligible agent in a Honeycomb district. The next card can be a common Smear using a different eligible agent for Riverworks. Each party must be open. A party card instead bypasses agent locations; the gallery still selects an awake portrait and rotates it afterwards.

The Fiddler’s finale

Play I Can Wait, Rally, then Organise: all names differ. Resolve Organise, optionally carrying the Fiddler, then optionally resolve it again from his updated location for the same party. Put the single Organise card in that party’s pile and place one favour there if it has none. Each law triggers at most once for the card.

Hecate predicts a Firm

Hecate predicts the doppelgängers will play a common Organise. A turn using only movement or party cards leaves the prediction pending. On their next turn with common cards, Rally followed by Organise fulfils it. Rally followed by ending the turn fails it. The prophecy goes to New Year either way.

Portrait toll

An awake portrait in Coast and another in Harbormouth make those districts adjacent. An opponent using that connection pays a common or party card from hand to the gallery before resolving the effect. Moving Support through it without accompanying agents still costs the toll. The network stays fixed until the card is complete.

Regional policy vote

After thinning, four surviving Support vote For and three Against. The region’s policy passes. Another region ties 2–2 and discards its policy. Count all four regions before activating the passed effects. Centre votes with all its Support.

Awarding a Party law

Mobile Clinics has Honeycomb, Night Parliament and Old Shell in that For-row order. In its region, surviving Honeycomb and Night Parliament Support each supply two For votes, while Riverworks supplies three Against votes. The policy passes 4–3. Honeycomb receives Fresh Start because it is leftmost among the tied For contributors; Riverworks is ineligible despite having the most votes of any one party. Keep Mobile Clinics beside Honeycomb’s board permanently. Every player still scores its +Healthcare / −Transport at game end.

Any player restoring an absent Honeycomb with Organise or Rally receives Fresh Start. Restoring Night Parliament does not receive Honeycomb’s effect. Global Muted Priorities would instead change every player’s scoring values, with no recipient award.

Scoring an enacted policy

Your Healthcare is worth 6 and Transport 2. An enacted +Healthcare / −Transport policy scores 4. With Muted Priorities, Healthcare becomes 4 and the same policy scores 2. Apply this to every enacted policy, then add hand rank.

Hand rank ties

Four players finish with 8, 10, 10 and 12 cards. They receive 0, 2, 2 and 3 points. Count common and party cards in hand after final New Year release; exclude all firm and Hecate reward cards.

Ordering competing extras

The acting party has been awarded Chain Migration. A player uses Dig In at that party, even if it is not Old Shell; both extras follow its Organise. If the source has one free spot, the firm chooses which extra fills it. If Chain Migration fills it first, Dig In skips. If Dig In fills it first, Chain Migration skips. Each law applies once, with no follow-up loops.

Other party-card extras remain required: if a law placement uses the last destination space needed by Waggle Route, that ordering is illegal. Choose an order in which the party-card succeeds.

Displacement and Spin

The acting party has been awarded Displacement. Its Smear displaces a rival into a free neighboring district. Spin then adds acting-party Support to the original target district’s vacated spot.
