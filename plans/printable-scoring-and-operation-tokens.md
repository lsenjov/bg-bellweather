# Printable scoring cards and operation tokens

## Goal

Turn the complete scoring deck and reusable operation supply into print-ready prototype components.

## Step 1 — Scoring-card fronts

- Create twenty-four 40 × 61 mm scoring-card fronts for 41 × 63 mm sleeves.
- Place twelve cards on each of two A4 portrait sheets with cut guides and actual-size print instructions.
- Use compact party names plus non-color identifiers, preserve the exact card inventory, and omit backs.
- Validate card count, dimensions, content, pagination, and clipping.

## Step 2 — Operation tokens

- Replace reusable operative cards with neutral 25 × 25 mm operation tokens.
- Provide a six-player supply: twelve Organise, twelve Rally, twelve Smear, and six Court tokens.
- Keep the tokens independent of lobbying-house identity because complete bids transfer through the Revolving Door.
- Update live rules and component documents to use operation-token terminology and archive the replaced operative-card specification.

## Step 3 — Integration and review

- Link both printable artifacts from the component index and update decisions, open questions, and changelog.
- Validate A4 pagination, actual dimensions, grayscale legibility, document links, and deck/token counts.
- Obtain an independent review and resolve every high- or medium-severity finding.
