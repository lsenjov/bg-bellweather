# Political animal theme

## Goal

Commit the selected theme without inventing unresolved party effects, election
scoring, constituency rules, or operative counts.

## Step 1: Record the theme in the live design

- Replace the open pitch with rival lobbying houses influencing animal political
  parties in the fictional Republic of Bellwether.
- Define the six party targets and their paired operation families.
- Give existing mechanical structures thematic names while preserving their
  rules.
- Record the selected choices in the decision log.
- Promote the remaining theme-dependent questions into the current question
  queue.

## Step 2: Align components and archive entry points

- Update the board, cards, tokens, player-aid requirements, and glossary.
- Refresh the documentation index and playtest guide where ladder-era language
  no longer describes the live design.
- Add the theme adoption to the changelog.

## Step 3: Validate and review

- Run the documentation validator.
- Inspect the diff for contradictions and accidental conversion of open
  questions into rules.
- Obtain an independent review and resolve every high- or medium-severity issue;
  report any low-severity issues before making optional fixes.
