# Firm cards and tracker fit

## Goal

Revise the confirmed physical components so every lobbying firm has three interchangeable, logo-forward identity cards and the existing 25 mm score and round markers fit inside the map trackers.

## Steps

1. Replace each firm’s two labelled Counterbid Cover cards and circular opening-bid marker with three identical 40 × 61 mm identity cards. Retain the 25 mm score marker, enlarge the card emblems, and update live component documentation and history.
2. Recompose the A3 map with a right-side 3 × 5 campaign grid whose columns represent the three election cycles, plus a bottom two-row 0–20 score track with 26 mm spaces. Reposition the district art without changing topology or Support spots.
3. Regenerate all print PDFs, inspect the revised firm sheet and map, and run document and repository validation.
4. Request an independent review and resolve every high or medium finding before handoff.
