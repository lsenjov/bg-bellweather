# Back channel and firm identity

## Goal

Keep table communication reliably visible at every width, bring the physical firms’ visual identity into the private folio and every filing, and make revealed filing contents compact without showing zero-value card families.

## Steps

1. **Back channel and folio identity — completed.** Combine the Gift Ledger and Table Talk into one full-width Back Channel panel with a stable desktop split and mobile stack. Add the board-game firm emblems and pastel identities to single- and dual-firm private folios while keeping spectator folios neutral.
2. **Firm-stamped filing summaries — completed.** Apply each physical firm’s emblem and pastel identity to every filing. Replace verbose revealed card-family text with accessible L/B/O/R/S/C summaries, omit zero-value families and empty totals, and preserve covered/partially revealed information boundaries.
3. **Documentation, verification, and review.** Strengthen the existing design note that every card family must retain a distinct initial, update focused UI coverage, run the full repository check, inspect wide and narrow layouts in the collaborative browser, and resolve all high and medium independent-review findings.

## Constraints

- A firm treatment must not imply party ownership or use saturated party-state styling.
- Dual-firm players must see both firm identities; neither firm may be selected as the player-wide default.
- Firm identity may appear on covered filings because `firmId` is already public, but concealed card families must remain concealed.
- Initials abbreviate filing summaries only; controls and private inventory keep full names.
- Zero-value card families and zero-card covered totals render no card-summary text.
