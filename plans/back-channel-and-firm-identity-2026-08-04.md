# Back channel and firm identity

## Goal

Keep table communication reliably visible at every width, bring the physical firms’ visual identity into the private folio and every filing, and make revealed filing contents compact without showing zero-value card families.

## Steps

1. **Back channel and folio identity — completed.** Combine the Gift Ledger and Table Talk into one full-width Back Channel panel with a stable desktop split and mobile stack. Add the board-game firm emblems and pastel identities to private folios while keeping spectator folios neutral.
2. **Firm-stamped filing summaries — completed.** Apply each physical firm’s emblem and pastel identity to every filing. Replace verbose revealed card-family text with accessible L/B/O/R/S/C summaries, omit zero-value families and empty totals, and preserve covered/partially revealed information boundaries.
3. **Single app identity, public tracker, documentation, verification, and review — completed.** Assign one firm per player in the digital game at every player count while retaining the doubled low-player card supply, openings, and counterbid capacity. Carry each firm’s colour and emblem into the points tracker and show Ready/Waiting there during readiness phases. Strengthen the existing design note that every card family must retain a distinct initial, update focused UI coverage, run the full repository check, inspect wide and narrow layouts in the collaborative browser, and resolve all high and medium independent-review findings.

## Constraints

- A firm treatment must not imply party ownership or use saturated party-state styling.
- The digital game assigns one firm per player. At two or three players, doubled resources and bid capacity are independent of firm count.
- The board game continues to give each low-player-count human two firms' physical components.
- Firm identity may appear on covered filings because `firmId` is already public, but concealed card families must remain concealed.
- Initials abbreviate filing summaries only; controls and private inventory keep full names.
- Zero-value card families and zero-card covered totals render no card-summary text.
