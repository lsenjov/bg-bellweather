## Print constraints

- Agents: 25.4 mm squares, pairs printed single-sided, identical standee faces; Gallery awake/asleep faces. Fifteen standees, six flat reversible portraits.
- Small tokens: thirteen 16 mm single-layer tokens (six favours, six opening-history tokens, one Hecate trap).
- Cards: 40 × 61 mm; aligned duplex backs. Shared common back, per-party/per-firm backs, separate reward back. Large top labels on Election prophecies.
- No mirroring token, numbered prophecy markers or reward-supply board. Keep current A3 map for fit testing.
