# Universal operation baselines and party bonuses

## Goal

Replace party-operation compatibility restrictions with universal operation
baselines and two independently claimable party bonuses per influence contest,
without inventing the unresolved effects.

## Step 1: Preserve and record the rule change

- Archive the superseded compatibility-only rule and component specification.
- Record the universal-baseline and ranked-bonus decisions in the decision log.
- Add the player-visible change to the changelog.

## Step 2: Align the live documentation

- Update the rules and component specifications.
- Replace compatibility language with favored-operation and bonus language.
- Revise the glossary, design guidance, open question, and playtest prompts.

## Step 3: Validate and review

- Run the documentation validator and repository checks.
- Search for contradictory live compatibility language.
- Obtain an independent review and resolve every high- or medium-severity issue.
- Report any low-severity findings before making optional changes.

## Done when

- Every operation family is legal at every party and has an unresolved baseline
  effect.
- Every party favors its existing pair of operation families and offers one
  independently claimable bonus for each.
- Bids receive first refusal on available bonuses from highest rank to lowest.
- The former compatibility-only rule remains available as design history.
