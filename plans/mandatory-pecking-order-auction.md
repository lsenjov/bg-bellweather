# Mandatory Pecking Order auction and Press Perch component

## Goal

Make manipulation of the Pecking Order a guaranteed part of every Campaign Round and provide a printable board with movable party-order tokens that makes its exceptional resolution order explicit.

## Committed rule change

- Every Campaign Round includes one Press Perch auction in addition to the player-opened party contests.
- Players cannot open the Press Perch auction and it has no opening bid.
- The ordinary counterbid allowance may be committed to the Press Perch or any open party contest.
- Every Press Perch bid is an ordinary counterbid, including empty bids and auction-local equal-Clout cancellation.
- Rank Press Perch bids by Clout normally, but execute their operation tokens from the lowest active bid to the highest. The highest-Clout bid therefore acts last.
- Every operation token at the Press Perch has the same effect regardless of family: choose and swap one adjacent pair of party tokens in the current Pecking Order.
- After all Press Perch operations resolve, perform the Revolving Door using the normal high-to-low bid ranking: each complete bid transfers to the owner immediately below it and the lowest transfers to the highest.
- Resolve the Press Perch before every party contest, then resolve party contests in the resulting Pecking Order.

## Component direction

- Produce a two-sheet A4 landscape printable:
  - one Press Perch board with six ordered party positions, a clearly marked counterbid edge, reverse operative-resolution guidance, and normal Revolving Door guidance;
  - one token sheet with six movable, saturated party-order markers matching the prototype Support colours and party emblems.
- Treat the board as a media institution rather than a seventh party: black newsroom typography, warm paper, registration red, and bulletin yellow.
- Do not print an opening-bid position or opening marker on the Press Perch.
- Direct players to place covered bids beside the board because the maximum counterbid supply cannot fit into fixed 70 mm spaces on one A4 sheet.
- Preserve the committed fixed-starting-order rule without inventing the exact initial sequence; continue to label the exact order as open.

## Documentation

1. Archive the former optional, opener-selected Press Perch contest rule and its open component format.
2. Update current rules, glossary, examples, design decision log, open questions, and changelog.
3. Add the printable component to the board and token inventories, index navigation, and setup links.
4. Update every lobbying-house round reminder to show Press Perch low-to-high execution before party contests.

## Validation

- Current rules contain no instruction allowing a player to open the Press Perch.
- The board contains no opening-bid area.
- The party-order track reads from first party contest to last party contest.
- The Press Perch operation arrow reads low Clout to high Clout.
- The Revolving Door remains described using the normal high-to-low bid ranking.
- All six party tokens match the agreed yellow, green, pink, blue, orange, and black assignments and remain identifiable by number, name, and emblem without colour.
- Both sheets print on A4 landscape without clipping or scaling.
- Document structure, local links, and archive links pass repository checks.
