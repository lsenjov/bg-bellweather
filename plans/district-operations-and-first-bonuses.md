# District operations and first party bonuses

## Goal

Record the agreed district Support system, concrete operation baselines,
directional coalition overtures, and the approved Honeycomb and Old Shell
bonuses without committing the proposed Foxglove bonuses.

## Step 1: Preserve and record the rule changes

- Archive the superseded Campaign, Expansion, Platform, and Coalition family
  specification.
- Record the district, operation, coalition, and approved bonus decisions.
- Add the player-visible changes to the changelog.

## Step 2: Align rules and components

- Add the district map, Support, overture, and reinforced-overture rules.
- Rename the four operation families to Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court.
- Define the universal baselines and supportless-party fallbacks.
- Add the Honeycomb and Old Shell bonuses while leaving the other eight open.
- Update board, card, token, player-aid, and glossary specifications.

## Step 3: Align supporting documents

- Revise open questions and next-test guidance.
- Add rules examples for spatial actions, recovery, bonuses, and coalitions.
- Update entry-point summaries and design principles where needed.

## Step 4: Validate and review

- Run the documentation validator and repository checks.
- Search live documents for superseded family names and unresolved-baseline
  claims.
- Obtain an independent review and resolve every high- or medium-severity issue.
- Report any low-severity findings before making optional changes.

## Done when

- Every party can use all four concrete operation baselines.
- A supportless party can re-enter anywhere with Organise or Rally and Smear
  anywhere.
- Reciprocal one-way overtures form coalitions.
- Honeycomb and Old Shell each have two decided bonuses.
- Foxglove and the other three parties still have explicitly open bonuses.
