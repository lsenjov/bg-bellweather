Superseded

Former terminology

The setting was the Republic of Bellwether. Reusable political currency was Clout. Court was the operation that redirected a party’s one-way Overture, and two reciprocal Overtures formed a coalition.

The application and rules engine used matching internal names, including @bellwether/* packages, clout resources, court operations, and overtures state.

Replacement

The setting is now the Republic of Bellweather. Leverage replaces Clout, Coalition replaces the Court operation, and each directional Overture is now a Coalition Target. Two reciprocal Coalition Targets still form a coalition; no mechanic changed.

The ruleset-version-4 implementation uses the new names throughout package scopes, API payloads, persisted state, environment variables, and browser session keys. No compatibility alias or saved-game migration was retained because no web-playtest saves existed.
