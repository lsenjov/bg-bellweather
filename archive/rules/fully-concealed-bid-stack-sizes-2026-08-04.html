Fully concealed bid-stack sizes

Superseded hidden-information rule retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former rule

An opening bid showed its face-up Leverage, but the number and mixture of its face-down operation and Bluff cards remained hidden. A counterbid showed only its target and firm identity card; both its total transferable-card count and its composition remained hidden until reveal.

Replacement

The total number of transferable cards in every placed bid is public. This count includes Leverage, Bluff, and operation cards but excludes the firm identity card. Covered card-family quantities and the mixture of the stack remain hidden until reveal.
