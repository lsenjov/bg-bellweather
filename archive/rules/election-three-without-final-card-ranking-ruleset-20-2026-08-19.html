Ruleset 20 behavior

Election 3 added only base district score, any relative-seat modifier, and Capital score to each player’s cumulative points. Held ordinary Operation cards, held Bonus cards, and Bonus cards at party homes scored no points. After Court Support cleared, every player tied for the highest cumulative score shared the win.
