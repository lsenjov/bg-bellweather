Former rule

Election Day drew Support only from districts named on at least one revealed scoring card. Each such district kept its shared capacity-based draw and returned every undrawn Support to supply. Every district absent from all revealed cards remained unchanged, so a full unscored district could stay full across Election Day.

Replacement

Ruleset version 12 holds an Election in every district except Bellweather Centre. Every capacity-six district keeps three randomly drawn Support, every capacity-four district keeps two, and every capacity-two district keeps one, regardless of the revealed scoring cards. Scoring cards still determine which district draws award points. Bellweather Centre remains unchanged as a powerful jumping-off point and possible blocker.
