Index/Current rules

Across six years, rival lobbying firms establish party access, perform political Operations, collect public card piles for later years, and close their own contacts before three Elections decide Bellweather’s future.

Decided

Players, duration, and objective

The game supports two to six human players. It lasts six years. Hold an Election after Years 2, 4, and 6; after the third Election’s final card rank bonus, every player tied for the highest cumulative point balance shares the win.

Years 1, 3, and 5 end at thematic midterm boundaries. A midterm adds no phase or mechanical effect: complete ordinary Cleanup and begin the next year.

Influence the four regions to pass policies that suit your secret promise priorities. Each enacted policy gains its + category value and loses its − category value at game end. Preserve cards for the final hand-ranking bonus.

Working title: Ladder Bidding. The auction mechanic has been removed; the title remains open to change.

Decided

Components

  • One sixteen-district Bellweather map with fifty-seven Support spaces.
  • Six parties, unlimited Support for each, public areas for yearly Firm markers, Operation piles, and three Bonus-card spaces per party.
  • Six Firm identities with one yearly opening marker each.
  • Operation cards: Organise, Rally, and Smear; plus eighteen unique collectible Bonus cards.
  • Two Collection counters per player, doubled to four at two or three players.
  • Thirty policy cards, twelve secret promise scoring cards, one player folio and one player helper per human player, one covered New Year area per player with its total count visible, an Early Bird marker, a Year/Election tracker, and a public score track.

Leverage cards, Bluff cards, gifts, bid markers, counterbid pieces, and the Pecking Order are not used. Their former specification is retained in the Ruleset 14 component archive.

Decided

Public and private information

Operation and Bonus cards in hand and promise scoring cards until the final Election are private. Combined hand size, combined New Year area size, Operation cards in party piles, Bonus cards at their home parties, Firm markers on open parties or returned to players, Collection counters, points, Support, public party rankings, pending policies, enacted laws and discarded policies are public.

When you Collect or receive cards from a Closure, place them in your New Year area. They cannot be used during the current year. The exact contents are private, though the total card count remains public.

Decided

The six-year structure

Every year has three stages, followed by an Election after Years 2, 4, and 6:

  1. Party Openings: players establish Firm access to different parties.
  2. Lobby actions: players take clockwise turns to Operate, Collect, Close, or Pass.
  3. Cleanup: close and award remaining parties, release New Year cards, return yearly pieces, and assign the next Early Bird.
  4. Election, when scheduled: after Cleanup, resolve all four policy votes.
Decided

1. Party Openings

Beginning with the Early Bird, each opening turn places one of the active player’s unused Firm markers on a party with no Firm marker. A party can have at most one Firm marker at a time. The marker establishes which player owns that party’s Closure; it is not a bid and commits no Operation cards. A Firm marker on a party means that party is open. Without one, it is unavailable for Lobby actions.

  • Two players: use ABBA. Each player opens two different parties.
  • Three players: use ABCCBA. Each player opens two different parties.
  • Four to six players: go once clockwise from the Early Bird.

After the final opening, the Early Bird takes the first Lobby action. The snake order applies only to Party Openings; Lobby turns always move clockwise.

Decided

2. Lobby actions

On your turn, take exactly one action. Then play passes clockwise unless that action ends the year.

Operate

Play one ordinary Operation card or one Bonus card from your hand on one open party, choose its complete parameters, and resolve it immediately. Leave an ordinary card face up in that party’s public pile. Return a successfully played Bonus card immediately to its home party. You may then finish the Operate action or play another card at the same party, choosing it against the updated board. You may make the same choice again after the second card. A third card ends the Operate action automatically.

You do not choose later cards or targets in advance, and no other player acts between cards. Once the first card resolves, that party remains fixed for the complete Operate action and you cannot take a different Lobby action until you finish.

A Bonus card is a complete standalone action and requires no ordinary card. It consumes one of the one-to-three card slots, never enters an Operation pile, and may be held indefinitely. Any number of cards in one Operate may be Bonus cards. Play a held Bonus card on any open party. The open party being operated remains the acting party for the entire card.

A Bonus that explicitly resolves an Operation uses its law-modified baseline. Unbound effects and standalone actions such as Canal Network follow their own text.

Resolve each card atomically. The firm freely orders all triggered extras together, including the Bonus extra and law follow-ups. Law extras are mandatory when possible and otherwise skipped. Required Bonus extras must succeed or the entire play is illegal. Dig In skips a full source; Midnight Leak skips when no matching rival is available in a neighboring district. Displacement leaves Smear Bonuses referring to the same rival and original target district. A successful Bonus returns home immediately, even if home is closed; no retroactive award occurs.

Collect

Spend one of your Collection counters and choose any open party. Take its complete Operation pile into your New Year area and optionally take at most one Bonus card currently at that party. The pile may be empty, and the action remains legal when no Bonus card is available or taken. The party remains open and can receive new Operations. Any player may Collect any party, including one they opened or previously collected.

Close

Choose an open party bearing one of your Firm markers. Take its complete Operation pile into your New Year area, optionally take at most one Bonus card currently there, then return the Firm marker face up to your play area. The Bonus choice remains available even when the Operation pile is empty. Without its marker, the party is closed and accepts no more Lobby actions unless a card opens it again. A returned marker is normally unavailable until the next year, but Midnight Session can place one on a closed party.

You cannot Close during your first Lobby turn of the year. Only the player who currently owns the Firm marker on a party can Close it. At the end of every Lobby turn, count the Firm markers placed during Party Openings that are currently returned. If strictly more than half are returned, the year ends. Do not check between cards in an Operate action. Close every remaining party and resolve each current Firm owner’s optional Bonus-card choice before Cleanup.

Pass

You may Pass only after every Firm marker you placed during Party Openings has returned. Do nothing, then apply the ordinary end-of-turn check and continue clockwise if the year remains active.

Who becomes Early Bird?

The player whose Lobby turn ends with a strict majority of Firm markers returned becomes the next Early Bird.

The inland map contains sixteen districts: Urban, Mixed and Outlying each have capacity 18, and Bellweather Centre separately has capacity 3. There are 23 adjacencies across shared land borders and nine marked bridges. Water severs adjacency for every effect unless a bridge connects the districts. Every party has unlimited Support, but every placement still requires a free district spot.

Policies and laws

Each party has a fixed public priority order. It votes For when a policy’s + category outranks its − category, otherwise Against. Each policy prints all six votes in coloured party tiles. Opposing priority pairs ensure three parties on each side.

Enacted laws are global and permanent. Different effects combine; duplicate effects do not stack. Each law triggers at most once per card action, only from the original Operation, never another extra. Movement permissions combine; Carpooling can move two together while Political Exchange swaps up to two rivals back to the source. Bridge Campaign requires a direct marked bridge, not a possible route.

Decided

3. Cleanup

  1. Close every party still open, give its complete pile to the owner of the Firm marker currently there, return that marker, and let that owner optionally take at most one Bonus card from that party. Complete every owner’s choice before continuing.
  2. Move every ordinary and Bonus card in every player’s New Year area into that player’s private hand. Those cards are now available.
  3. Return all Collection counters. All Firm markers are now back with their owners and available for the next Party Openings.
  4. Give the Early Bird marker to the player whose turn ended the year.

After a non-Election year, begin the next Party Openings; the thematic midterm after Years 1, 3, and 5 changes nothing. After Years 2, 4, and 6, resolve the Election only after this complete Cleanup. Year 6 uses the same Cleanup, and cards released from New Year during that Cleanup are in hand for the final card ranking.

Decided

4. Election

  1. After Cleanup, randomly retain up to half capacity in each non-Centre district: 3, 2 or 1 Support in capacity-6, -4 or -2 districts. Retain all when fewer are present. Centre retains all its Support.
  2. Each surviving Support votes on its region’s pending policy using its party’s printed position. More For than Against passes; ties, including 0–0, fail.
  3. Count all four votes before activating new laws. Keep passed cards globally; discard failed cards. Opposite policies can both pass.
  4. After Elections 1 and 2 reveal a new policy for each region. No scoring or scoring-card reveal occurs yet. Begin the next year with the assigned Early Bird.
  5. After Election 3, reveal every player’s scoring card. For each enacted policy gain its + category value and lose its − category value. Apply all scoring laws, including newly enacted laws: Muted Priorities changes printed 6 to 4; Broad Interests changes printed 1 to 3. Both gains and losses change, without reordering priorities or changing votes.
  6. Add final hand rank: count other human players holding fewer than or the same number of ordinary Operation and Bonus cards after Year 6 Cleanup. Score one point for each. Home Bonus cards, policies and scoring cards do not count. At two or three players count each human’s combined hand once.

Final totals may be negative. Highest total wins; tied players share victory. There is no Capital score or regional agenda score. Deal no policies after the final Election. At most eight laws are active during play, and twelve policies can score at game end.
