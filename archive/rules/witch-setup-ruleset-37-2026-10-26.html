Witch setup — Ruleset 37

Replaced on 2026-10-26: Ruleset 38 allows each Witch piece to start in any district independently. Current rules · Previous components and source.

Former firm rules

Place witch, crow, cat and toad together in one chosen district. The witch cannot authorise common Operations. Crow authorises Organise, cat Rally and toad Smear. Immediately before resolving its assigned Operation, that familiar may move one neighbouring district; it must then legally perform that Operation, never using a common card merely to move. The step is before, not after, the Operation. Laws need no additional familiar presence. Party cards bypass positioning and grant no free familiar step.

Three firm cards—Crow, Cat and Toad—each teleport either the witch to that familiar or that familiar to the witch, anywhere on the map. Before or after teleporting, you may move the named familiar to a neighbouring district once, regardless of which agent teleports. The teleport is required; the familiar’s step is optional. Then send the card to New Year. All her agents follow the general voluntary Organise transport rule.
