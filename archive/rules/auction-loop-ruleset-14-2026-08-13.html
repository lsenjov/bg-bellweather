Superseded

Former player economy

Players held Leverage, Bluff, and Operation cards in a concealed, closed economy. They could make one-way gifts of cards or points. Each Firm supplied three interchangeable identity cards: one identified a public opening bid and two covered concealed counterbid stacks. Low-player games doubled the economy, Firms, opening bids, and counterbid allowance.

Former Campaign Round

  1. Players placed funded party opening bids in Early Bird order, using ABBA at two players and ABCCBA at three.
  2. Players placed and revised concealed counterbids simultaneously, either under a timer or until unanimous readiness. A separate Pecking Order contest was always available.
  3. Each contest revealed as a group. Equal counterbids cancelled, remaining bids ranked by Leverage, Operation cards executed, and the complete reusable bids transferred around the ranking through the Revolving Door.
  4. The Pecking Order contest resolved first and Operation cards there swapped adjacent party tokens. Party contests then followed that persistent order.
  5. After resolution, received cards returned to concealed reserves and the next Campaign Round began. Election Days followed Rounds 4, 8, and 12.

Former party-bonus timing

A party offered each favored bonus once per contest, with bids receiving first refusal from highest rank downward. One bid could claim both bonuses. Night Shift was delayed until every party contest and Revolving Door had finished, then added one Night Support to a legal least-occupied Rally district.

Replacement

Ruleset 15 removes Leverage, Bluff, gifts, auctions, counterbids, bid transfers, the Pecking Order, and delayed effects. Each year now consists of Party Openings, clockwise Lobby actions, Cleanup, and—after Years 4, 8, and 12—an Election. Players Operate directly, Collect public party piles with counters, Close parties they opened, or Pass.

Earlier archived foundations

The auction rules grew from an optional Press Perch contest, while party-specific Operation compatibility preceded universal baselines. The first Election specification is retained as the unresolved Election Day archive.
