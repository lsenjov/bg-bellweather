Ruleset-13 scoring agendas

Superseded per-period deal and redeal procedure retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former setup

Shuffle all twenty-four scoring cards. At four to six players, deal one face down to each player. At two or three players, complete one player’s hand before moving clockwise to the next: deal their first card face down, then draw a second card. The player privately checks both cards. If the second card shares any district with the first, discard it face down and draw again until the pair names six distinct districts. If the draw pile empties during this deal, shuffle the face-down rejected cards into a new draw pile while keeping every held card out.

Former Election transition

Every player revealed their current scoring card or compatible low-player pair on Election Day. After the first and second Election Days, collect every scoring card, including rejected second draws and undealt cards, and shuffle all twenty-four together. Deal new hidden cards for the next four-round set using the same player-count procedure.

Replacement

Ruleset 14 shuffles once at setup and assigns every player three fixed, ordered Election slots. Future slots are privately visible to their owner, remain concealed from opponents, and are never reassigned or redealt.
