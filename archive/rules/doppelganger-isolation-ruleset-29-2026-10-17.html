Doppelgänger isolation — Ruleset 29

Replaced on 2026-10-17 by per-party pile mirroring. Current rules.

Former doppelgänger rules

Place two interchangeable agents in any two distinct districts. They can never occupy the same district. Four identical firm movement cards each move one agent to a neighbouring district; on a mirroring turn, the other may then also move one neighbouring district if legal. Choose the first agent freely; neither may enter the other’s current district. Neighbouring agents are allowed.

Declare a mirroring turn before the first card. Both agents must have started the turn without another player’s agent in their district, and neither may enter a district containing another player’s agent that turn. Sleeping portraits do not count as witnesses; awake portraits do. Starting with company prevents mirroring for the whole turn even if the agent moves away. A normal turn allows shared districts but only one effect per card.

On a mirroring turn each common card may operate through one or both agents. Choose effects that are legal before either resolves and jointly possible: they cannot claim the same Support or last free space. Both use the same acting party; both Smears also target the same rival party. Resolve them one at a time, including voluntary Organise transport subject to agent restrictions. Each law triggers at most once for the card; choose an eligible instance. Recovery can place two Support when jointly legal. Party cards resolve once and bypass agents.

Former Gallery exception

Sleeping portraits do not witness doppelgängers.

Former playtest question

Can blocked doppelgängers regain useful positions? Mirroring required isolation for a whole turn and scaled with board crowding. Test low and high player counts; record blocked turns, single-agent turns and use of the four movement cards.
