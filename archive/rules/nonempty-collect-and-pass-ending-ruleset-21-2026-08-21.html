Nonempty Collect and pass ending

Archived 2026-08-21 when Ruleset 22 made personal Closure mandatory before Pass.

Index/Changelog/Ruleset 21 Lobby ending

This page preserves the replaced Ruleset 21 action eligibility and year-ending rules. It is historical reference, not current play guidance.

Archived rule

Collect

Spend one Collection counter and choose an open party with a non-empty Operation pile. Take the complete pile into New Year and optionally take at most one Bonus card there. The party remains open.

Archived rule

Pass and the year ending

Pass did nothing. If every player passed consecutively, the year ended immediately, every remaining party closed to its opener, and the final passer became Early Bird. Any non-Pass action broke the sequence. A strict majority of closed parties remained the alternative year-ending trigger.

Replacement

Ruleset 22

Collect may target any open party regardless of pile or Bonus contents. Pass requires all of the acting player’s Firm markers to have returned and never ends the year. Only a strict Closure majority ends the Lobby.
