Superseded

Former campaign clock and victory

The game lasted three campaign periods of twelve Campaign Rounds each. Election Day followed rounds 12, 24, and 36. After the third Election Day, the single player with the highest cumulative score won; the rules did not resolve a tie.

Superseded

Former player setup

Every human player began with 20 Clout, four Organise, four Rally, four Smear, two Court, and zero points regardless of player count. Players at two or three received extra firm identity and bid pieces but did not receive additional economic resources.

The setup called for a fixed Pecking Order whose exact order remained unresolved. Starting Support quantities and positions and all starting overtures also remained unresolved.

Superseded

Former trade and deal rule

Players could exchange eligible Clout and operation tokens. A deal was binding when every term could be satisfied immediately, and the players had to complete every part as agreed. Future promises remained nonbinding, and completed transfers were not reversed when one was broken.

Replacement

On 2026-07-30, the game changed to twelve total Campaign Rounds with Election Days after rounds 4, 8, and 12. Four- to six-player games begin each player with 10 Clout, two Organise, two Rally, two Smear, one Court, and 5 public points; two- and three-player games double every listed resource and the point balance. Support became unlimited. Each party begins with one Support in Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood, every overture begins unpointed, and the Pecking Order is randomized.

Eligible Clout, operation tokens, and points now move only as one-way atomic gifts. Agreements are socially enforced: there is no acceptance step, reversal, or rules enforcement. Players tied for the highest final point balance share the win.
