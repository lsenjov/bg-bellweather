Witch teleports — Ruleset 31

Replaced on 2026-10-20: Ruleset 32 adds one optional neighbouring-district move for the named familiar before or after a firm card’s teleport. The assigned-Operation step is unchanged. Current rules · Previous components and source.

Former firm rules

Place witch, crow, cat and toad together in one chosen district. The witch cannot authorise common Operations. Crow authorises Organise, cat Rally and toad Smear. Immediately before resolving its assigned Operation, that familiar may move one neighbouring district; it must then legally perform that Operation, never using a common card merely to move. The step is before, not after, the Operation. Laws need no additional familiar presence. Party cards bypass positioning and grant no free familiar step.

Three firm cards—Crow, Cat and Toad—each teleport either the witch to that familiar or that familiar to the witch, anywhere on the map, then go to New Year. All her agents follow the general voluntary Organise transport rule.
