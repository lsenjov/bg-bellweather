Batched low-player opening turns

Superseded rules retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former procedure

Starting with the Early Bird holder and proceeding clockwise, each human player took one opening turn. During that single turn, a player at a two- or three-player table placed both opening bids they were required and able to fund. A player at a four-to-six-player table placed one.

Replacement

On 2026-08-05, ruleset version 9 split doubled low-player openings into a visible snake. Two players use ABBA and three use ABCCBA, where A holds the Early Bird marker and letters proceed clockwise. Every position is a separate turn that places one funded opening or passes with no Leverage, and affordability is checked again for later repeated turns. Four-to-six-player games retain one clockwise opening turn per player.
