Hecate trap — Ruleset 32

Replaced on 2026-10-20: Ruleset 33 supplies three An Unwelcome Step cards and matching markers, limits placed traps to one per region, and excludes Bellwether Centre. Other prophecy placement, trigger and reward rules are unchanged. Current rules · Previous components and source.

Former firm rules

Place three interchangeable sisters in any districts, sharing allowed. Each authorises all common Operations. Her fifteen firm cards comprise three movement cards (move one sister one neighbouring district) and twelve prophecies below. Her separate public reward supply begins with two Organise, two Rally and two Smear reward cards, none in hand.

All prophecies are public and can be placed remotely: at most one prophecy on each individual region, district, party and Firm. There is no overall cap. A prophecy can only be the third card of an Operate action, following any two non-prophecy cards. Hecate cannot fulfil a prophecy during a turn when she plays one; if she has already fulfilled one that turn she cannot play one. A prophecy stays placed across years until it resolves or she sends it to New Year, for free at any time. There is no additional tracked once-per-year restriction; a returned card can be replayed when otherwise legal.

On success choose one available Hecate reward card and take it directly into hand for use on a subsequent turn; it cannot be played on the turn it is earned. If none is available, lose that reward. Independently, optionally move one sister one neighbouring district for free. Chains are permitted without a special exception. Resolve rewards after the triggering card finishes and before the next card; where timing has no effect, take them as soon as the result is clear. Reward cards use ordinary slots and normal agent requirements, return directly to the supply after use, and do not enter party piles or New Year.

  • Five Election prophecies: place on a region in a midterm year (1, 3 or 5) only, predicting its next Election’s final For-minus-Against margin: +5 or greater, +1 to +4, 0, −4 to −1, or −5 or lower. Ties still fail the policy. Resolve after thinning and voting; success or failure sends the card to New Year, before that Election’s New Year release. Returned cards may be reused within a valid midterm placement window.
  • Three pile prophecies: predict 0–2, 3–4 or 5–6 cards when the named party’s pile is next taken through Collect, Close or automatic Closure. Count the pile, excluding the separately awarded party card. Another player taking the matching count succeeds; a different count or Hecate taking the pile fails. Either result sends the prophecy to New Year.
  • Three Firm prophecies: name Organise, Rally or Smear on another Firm. Wait for its next turn containing common-card play; fulfil immediately on the matching common card, otherwise fail when that turn ends. Earlier nonmatching common cards do not fail it mid-turn. Turns without common cards leave it pending. Firm, party and reward cards do not count even if they resolve those Operations. Success and failure send it to New Year; there is no penalty to the target.
  • One district-entry trap: another player’s agent entering the named district triggers it, including Organise transport but not Support movement alone. That player chooses one common card from hand to send to their New Year area, or pays nothing if they have none. Hecate receives the standard success reward and movement even if nothing was paid. Resolve once per entry event even if several agents enter, then send the trap to New Year.
