Former setup

At four to six players, each human began with one Court card. At two or three players, the complete per-player economy doubled, giving each human two Court cards.

Replacement

On 2026-08-05, ruleset version 10 doubled Court to two cards per standard player and four per low-player doubled supply. All other setup quantities and Court behavior remain unchanged.
