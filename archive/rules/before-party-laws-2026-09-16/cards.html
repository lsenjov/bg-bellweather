Cards

Policy component prototype · September 2026

Index/Components

The policy prototype uses 54 ordinary Operation cards, 18 Bonus cards, 12 secret scoring cards and 30 public policy cards. The browser app and current rulebook implement this policy system as Ruleset 26. The physical map revision uses Industrial Belt and smaller outer electorates; the app and current rulebook retain the preceding map.

Policy deck, party orders, law interactions · Scoring deck specification.

Operation supply

Standard hand: 3 Organise, 4 Rally, 2 Smear (9 cards). At 2–3 players, each human has one doubled hand: 6 Organise, 8 Rally, 4 Smear (18 cards). The complete six-kit supply is 18 Organise, 24 Rally and 12 Smear. Remove all ordinary Court cards without replacement.

Ordinary cards remain 40 × 61 mm, have no Firm identity, enter public party piles when played, and count toward final hand rank when held after Year 6 Cleanup.

Bonus supply

Keep three unique 40 × 61 mm Bonus cards per party. Collection or Closure requires taking exactly one home Bonus card if any are available. A held Bonus may be played on any open acting party, returns home immediately after successful play, and never enters the Operation pile. There is no automatic off-home Court.

Common Cause, Whisper Network and Joint Campaign now print Unbound because they no longer resolve Court. Institutional Memory references a pending policy; Midnight Leak spreads Smear to a neighboring district. The other thirteen effects retain their text. A named party in an effect means that party; “acting party” means the open party selected for Operate.

Ordinary Bonus legality remains atomic. Laws improve an explicitly named baseline Operation and add their effects; standalone actions do not become Operations. Interaction rulings are listed in the policy reference.

Shared table state

Four pending policy positions, one per region; one shared enacted-laws display; a draw deck and separate failed-policy discard. Each firm has one secret scoring card. Party priorities and policy votes are public. Court Support, Coalition Targets and Coalition summary pieces are retired.
