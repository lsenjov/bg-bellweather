Regional policy vote

After thinning, four surviving Support vote For and three Against. The region’s policy passes. Another region ties 2–2 and discards its policy. Count all four regions before activating the passed effects. Centre votes with all its Support.

Scoring an enacted policy

Your Healthcare is worth 6 and Transport 2. An enacted +Healthcare / −Transport policy scores 4. With Muted Priorities, Healthcare becomes 4 and the same policy scores 2. Apply this to every enacted policy, then add hand rank.

Hand rank ties

Four players finish with 8, 10, 10 and 12 cards. They receive 0, 2, 2 and 3 points. Count held Bonus cards and cards released during final Cleanup.

Ordering competing extras

Dig In and Chain Migration both follow an Organise. If the source has one free spot, the firm chooses which extra fills it. If Chain Migration fills it first, Dig In skips. If Dig In fills it first, Chain Migration skips. Each law applies once, with no follow-up loops.

Other Bonus extras remain required: if a law placement uses the last destination space needed by Waggle Route, that ordering is illegal. Choose an order in which the Bonus succeeds.

Displacement and Spin

Smear displaces a rival into a free neighboring district. Spin then adds acting-party Support to the original target district’s vacated spot.
