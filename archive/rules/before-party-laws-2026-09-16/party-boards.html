Policy prototype party workspaces

Print at actual size on three A4 landscape sheets and cut each sheet along the shared middle border. Every party workspace holds one annual Firm opening marker, one public Operation pile, three Bonus-card spaces, and its fixed public issue ranking.

A Firm marker in the opening well means the party is open. Return it to its owner when the party closes; a Bonus card may move it to another party or reuse it later. Put each party’s three collectible Bonus cards over its three blank card spaces and return played cards to a blank space at their home party. A party votes For when the policy’s + issue ranks above its − issue. Rankings never change.
