Direct Coalition redirection

Superseded operation and bonus rules retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former rule

Coalition was both an operation family and the reciprocal relationship between parties. A Coalition operation pointed the acting party’s Coalition Target directly at any other party except its current target.

Old Shell’s Binding Pact reinforced a successfully redirected target with a Shell marker. The next Coalition card that would redirect it removed the marker instead. Many Wings’ Local Chapters added Support beside the party selected by the redirect.

Replacement

Court is now the operation. It adds temporary acting-party Support to a prospective partner’s Court space. A unique Court Support leader becomes the party’s Coalition Target; a tied lead leaves the existing target in place. Court Support clears after each Election Day, but Coalition Targets persist.

Binding Pact now adds a second Old Shell Support to the selected Court space. Local Chapters adds Many Wings Support in a district containing Support belonging to Many Wings’ current Coalition Target after Court resolves. Two parties still form a coalition only while their targets are reciprocal.
