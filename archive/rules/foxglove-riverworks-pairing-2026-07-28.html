Former rule

Foxglove League favored Rally and Smear. Riverworks Party favored Organise and Smear. The assignments preserved every unique pair of Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court, but neither party had concrete bonuses.

Replacement

On 2026-07-28, Foxglove and Riverworks exchanged Rally and Organise. Foxglove now favors Organise and Smear, supporting global relocation and conversion. Riverworks now favors Rally and Smear, spreading growth and attrition across neighboring districts.
