Decided

The six-year structure

  1. Party Openings: establish access to different parties.
  2. Fiddler’s opening burst: spend last year’s favours; there are none in Year 1.
  3. Lobby: take clockwise turns from Early Bird.
  4. Year-end: resolve remaining Closures, then the scheduled Election, then refresh counters and prepare the next year.
  5. New Year: release New Year cards into hands, then begin Party Openings.
Decided

3. Year-end and New Year

  1. Close all remaining open parties in Firm-number order. Award each pile and one available party card to its marker’s owner, return the marker, and resolve all resulting effects. A player with two openings chooses their Closure order.
  2. After Years 2, 4 and 6, hold the Election below, including Election prophecies. Elections precede New Year.
  3. Return Collection counters and opening markers; assign Early Bird to the player whose turn ended the year. Complete preparation for the next year.
  4. Release all New Year cards into their owners’ hands. Agents stay in their districts throughout year changes and Elections.
  5. Begin the next Party Openings, then the Fiddler’s burst, then regular Lobby turns. After Year 6, release cards for final hand ranking without starting another playable year.
