Ruleset 17 Closure marking

The separate closed-state procedure retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former rule

To Close, the opener took the complete Operation pile and marked the party closed while leaving its Firm opening marker on the party. The retained marker continued to identify the Closure owner until Cleanup.

Closed parties accepted no more Operations or Collections. Cleanup returned all Firm opening markers and reset the separate Closed markers.

Replacement

The current rule returns the Firm opening marker immediately on every voluntary or automatic Closure. A marker on a party now means open, and face-up returned markers show the Closure count without separate state pieces.
