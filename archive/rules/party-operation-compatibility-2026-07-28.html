Party-operation compatibility

Superseded rule retained as design history.

Superseded

Former rule

Each party accepted two operation families. An operative could be used at a party only when its family matched one of that party’s operation icons, and the party defined the operative’s concrete effect. The Press Perch accepted all four families.

The six parties covered every unique pair of Campaign, Expansion, Platform, and Coalition. Every family was compatible with three parties.

Replacement

On 2026-07-28, universal baseline effects replaced compatibility restrictions. Every operation family may now be used at every party. The existing pairs identify favored operations whose party bonuses are offered once each to bids from highest rank to lowest.
