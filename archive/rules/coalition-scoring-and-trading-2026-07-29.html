Superseded

Former coalition rule

Reciprocal overtures formed a coalition, but coalition membership had no defined Election Day effect. Its consequences beyond overture state and party bonuses remained an open question.

Superseded

Former negotiation rule

Players could negotiate, lie about bids, coordinate, and break promises during counterbidding. Agreements were never binding, and players could not transfer Clout or operation tokens outside normal resolution.

Replacement

On 2026-07-29, coalition partners began sharing drawn Support for Election Day objective scoring. Players may now trade Clout and operation tokens at any time, but only while those components are physically behind a player’s screen. Anything committed to an opening bid or counterbid is ineligible, even before the bid locks. Deals whose full terms can be satisfied immediately are binding; promises depending on future actions, states, or outcomes remain nonbinding.
