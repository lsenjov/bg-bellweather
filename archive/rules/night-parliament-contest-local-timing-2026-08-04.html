Contest-local Night Parliament timing

Superseded delayed-bonus timing retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former rule

Closing Argument and After-Hours Deal were claimed during Night Parliament’s contest. After every active bid in that contest finished its ordinary operation cards, delayed baselines resolved from highest claiming bid to lowest immediately before Night Parliament’s Revolving Door.

Replacement

Claims are still made and marked during Night Parliament’s contest. Night Parliament and every later party contest now complete their Revolving Doors first. The delayed baselines then resolve from highest claiming bid to lowest against the final state, immediately before the Campaign Round ends.
