Ruleset 19 behavior

Each party board offered two printed immediate bonuses associated with favored Operation families. Both began Ready each year. When playing a matching ordinary Operation at that party, a player could claim its Ready bonus and cover that state with a distinct used marker.

Each complete one-to-three-card Operate action could claim at most one bonus. A claimed bonus remained unavailable for the rest of the year, including after its pile was Collected. Cleanup reset every used state to Ready.

Replacement

Ruleset 20 turned the twelve effects into unique collectible standalone cards with party homes, player ownership, New Year timing, reciprocal-coalition play, and immediate return after success. See the current rules.
