Optional Press Perch contest

Superseded rule retained as design history.

Index/Archived rule
Superseded

Former rule

A player could spend one of their opening-contest opportunities on the Press Perch instead of a party. That player placed a protected opening bid with at least one Clout, and the Press Perch received counterbids from the ordinary allowance.

If a Press Perch contest had been opened, it resolved before the party contests. Active bids executed from highest Clout to lowest, as at a party. Every operation token ignored its family and swapped one adjacent pair in the Pecking Order. The Revolving Door then transferred bids normally.

If no player opened the Press Perch, the round contained no Pecking Order auction.

Replacement

On 2026-07-29, this optional contest was replaced by an automatic Pecking Order auction in every Campaign Round, and the Press Perch name was retired. The Pecking Order has no opener or opening bid. Players may commit ordinary counterbids to it, equal-Clout bids cancel normally, and operation tokens execute from the lowest active bid to the highest so the highest bidder changes the order last. The Revolving Door still uses the normal high-to-low ranking.
