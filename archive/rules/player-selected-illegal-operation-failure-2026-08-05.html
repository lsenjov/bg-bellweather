Player-selected illegal operation failure

Superseded operation-resolution wording retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former rule

Players chose the family and parameters for each committed operation card. The rules said only that an operation card which was illegal when it would execute failed, could not be replaced, and still transferred with its bid. The digital game therefore also consumed a card when its owner submitted illegal parameters even though a legal choice remained.

Replacement

Ruleset version 11 distinguishes an illegal selection from an impossible committed card. When a legal resolution exists, the owner must correct an illegal choice and keeps the same pending card. Only when none of a bid’s remaining operation cards has any legal choice do those cards fail automatically and resolution advance. Delayed Night Parliament baselines use the same distinction.
