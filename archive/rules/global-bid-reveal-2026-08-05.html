Superseded

Former procedure

After counterbidding locked, every face-down card and covered counterbid was revealed simultaneously across the board. Equal counterbids in every contest were cancelled and returned before the Pecking Order or any party contest began resolving.

Replacement

Reveal one contest as a whole when it becomes the next contest to resolve. Cancel and return equal counterbids in that contest at the same time, while every future contest and its cancellation outcomes remain covered and escrowed.
