Non-mutating Election draws

Superseded Election Day procedure retained as design history.

Index/Archived rules
Superseded

Former rule

Election Day used one shared random draw for every district named by a revealed scoring card. After all objectives used that result, every drawn Support returned to its district and every undrawn Support remained there. Election Day therefore never changed the map.

Replacement

The shared draw now survives in its named district and every undrawn Support there returns to its party’s unlimited supply. A district absent from every revealed agenda remains unchanged. Bellwether Centre never appears in the scoring deck, so Election Day never thins it.
