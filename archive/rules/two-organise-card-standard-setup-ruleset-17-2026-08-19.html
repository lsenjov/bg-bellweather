Superseded

Former setup

At four to six players, each human began with two Organise, four Rally, two Smear, and two Court cards. At two or three players, the complete per-player supply doubled to four Organise, eight Rally, four Smear, and four Court cards.

Replacement

On 2026-08-19, Ruleset 18 increased Organise to three cards per standard player and six per doubled low-player supply. Rally, Smear, Court, and every other setup quantity remained unchanged.
