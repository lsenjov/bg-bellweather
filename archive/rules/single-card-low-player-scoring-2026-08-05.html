Single-card low-player scoring

Superseded rules retained as design history.

Superseded

Former procedure

Every player received one hidden scoring card for each four-round campaign period, regardless of player count. Each Election Day scored that card’s three district–party objectives. Four-to-six-player games applied its Gain and Lose seat references, while two- and three-player games ignored them.

After the first and second Election Days, every revealed card entered a discard pile and each player received one replacement from the remaining draw pile. The discards formed a new shuffled draw pile only if the remaining deck could not complete a deal.

Replacement

On 2026-08-05, two- and three-player games began dealing each player two scoring cards whose six districts are distinct within that player’s pair. Both cards score, while their Gain and Lose references remain ignored. Four-to-six-player games retain one card. All twenty-four cards are reshuffled before each campaign period.
