Fiddler starting favours — Ruleset 30

Replaced on 2026-10-18: the Fiddler now starts with zero favours. Earned favours and all other abilities are unchanged. Current rules.

Former firm rules

Place his single agent in any district first. Choose three different parties for starting Favours Owed. His firm cards are two identical Just Passing Through cards (move one neighbouring district) and two identical I Can Wait cards (do nothing); each uses a slot and goes to New Year.

One More Thing: cards played in one turn must have different names. If the third card is common or movement, resolve it once, then optionally a second time if legal against the updated board. A common card uses the same acting party both times, enters its pile only once, and receives each law at most once for the whole card; choose an eligible resolution for its extra. Resolve extras before proceeding. The agent may independently accompany each Organise, permitting two steps, or moving two Support from one source while staying until the second movement or throughout. Party cards are never doubled.

A third-slot common or party card places one favour on its acting party, at most one per party, even if the repeat is declined or impossible. Movement and I Can Wait do not place favours.

A Few Calls to Make: after Party Openings, before regular Lobby turns, spend or discard every favour from last year (the three starting favours in Year 1), in any chosen order. Each buys either a one-step agent move or a normal common Operation through the agent for that favour’s party. Operations require an open party and receive laws once per favour action; favours on closed parties can buy movement only. These standalone actions use no card, trigger neither One More Thing nor new favours, and cannot be saved. Year 6 favours have no later burst.

Former yearly sequence

Fiddler’s opening burst: spend last year’s favours (starting favours in Year 1).
