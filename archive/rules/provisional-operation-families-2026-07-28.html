Superseded

Former rule

Operatives belonged to the Campaign, Expansion, Platform, or Coalition family. Every family was legal at every party and had an undefined baseline effect. Campaign concerned public persuasion, Expansion concerned organization and membership, Platform concerned policy positions, and Coalition concerned agreements and government formation.

Each party favored one unique pair and offered one independently claimable bonus for each family during its influence contest. The bonus procedure was decided, but the four baselines and twelve bonuses were still unwritten.

Replacement

On 2026-07-28, a limited-capacity district Support map supplied the concrete effects. Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court replaced the provisional family names. Honeycomb Cooperative and Old Shell Union received the first four party bonuses.
