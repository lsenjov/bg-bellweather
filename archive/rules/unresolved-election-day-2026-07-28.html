Superseded

Objective

Parties will compete for electoral success through district Support while lobbying houses compete to influence the resulting government. The conversion of Support into election results, government-formation procedure, victory condition, and end trigger are unresolved.

Superseded

End of game

End trigger, Election Day, government formation, scoring, and tie-breakers are unresolved. Held Clout and operative cards do not score directly; victory must come from district Support, coalitions, or other board outcomes.
