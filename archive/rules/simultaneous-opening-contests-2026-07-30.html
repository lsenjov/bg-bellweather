Superseded

Former procedure

Players treated party-target selection as simultaneous and visible. Each party could host only one influence contest, but the rules did not determine what happened when multiple players attempted to open the same party.

Replacement

On 2026-07-30, opening bids became sequential. A first opener takes one opening turn, followed by the other human players clockwise. Each player completes their entire opening allocation during that turn and chooses only parties that remain available. The first-round opener is selected randomly; later rounds begin with the player who placed the previous round’s highest active Pecking Order bid. If no active Pecking Order bid remained, the first opener does not change.
