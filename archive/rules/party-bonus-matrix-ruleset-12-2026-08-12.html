Many Wings CoalitionOrganiseMurmuration: After Organise resolves, immediately resolve the Organise baseline again using the updated map state. The second resolution may move the same Support again or move a different Support.
Many Wings CoalitionCourtLocal Chapters: After Court resolves, add one Many Wings Support to a free spot in any district containing Support belonging to Many Wings’ current Coalition Target.
Night ParliamentRallyClosing Argument: After every contest and Revolving Door finishes, the claiming bid’s owner resolves one additional Rally baseline.
Night ParliamentCourtAfter-Hours Deal: After every contest and Revolving Door finishes, the claiming bid’s owner resolves one additional Court baseline.

Slip Away ignores adjacency but not district capacity. Murmuration’s second Organise checks whether Many Wings has Support after the first resolution; a recovery placement therefore makes the second resolution a movement. Spin, Public Works, Local Chapters, and all other effects that add Support require the stated free spot; the matching Support itself is always available.

Closing Argument and After-Hours Deal were claimed when their matching Night Parliament baseline resolved. Each claiming bid remained marked until, after the final contest’s Revolving Door, its owner resolved the delayed baselines from highest claiming bid to lowest. Legality was checked at delayed resolution; a claim failed automatically only when no legal choice remained.

Replacement

Ruleset 13 replaces all six operation pairs and the twelve powers with the approved theme-first matrix. Only Night Shift remains delayed.
