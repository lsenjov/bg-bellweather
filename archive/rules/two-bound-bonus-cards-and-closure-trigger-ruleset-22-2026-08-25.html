Index/Changelog/Ruleset 22 Bonus and ending rules

This page preserves the Ruleset 22 rules replaced by the eighteen-card deck. It is historical reference, not current play guidance.

Archived rule

Bonus supply and resolution

Each party had two collectible Bonus cards, one for each preferred Operation. Every Bonus card resolved its printed Operation baseline and additional effect as one atomic action. A successful play returned it to its home party.

Archived rule

Closure and the year ending

A returned Firm marker could not open another party until the next year. A Close ended the Lobby immediately when it made strictly more than half of the parties opened that year closed. The player taking that Close became next Early Bird.

Replacement

Ruleset 23

Each party now has two preferred-Operation Bonus cards and one Unbound card. Shell Firm can move an active Firm and pile without changing the number of returned markers, while Midnight Session can place one of the current player’s returned markers on a closed party. The strict-majority check therefore counts returned Firm markers placed during Party Openings and occurs at the end of every Lobby turn.
