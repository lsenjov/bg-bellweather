Superseded

Former rule

The game lasted twelve years. Each four-year cycle ended with an Election after ordinary Cleanup, so Elections followed Years 4, 8, and 12. The third Election ended the game.

Replacement

On 2026-08-19, Ruleset 19 shortened the game to six years with Elections after Years 2, 4, and 6. Years 1, 3, and 5 end at thematic midterm boundaries with no additional procedure or mechanical effect. Cleanup and the three-Election scoring structure remain unchanged.
