Superseded

Former specification

Each party displayed two compatibility icons. The icons told players which operative families could legally execute at that party:

  • Honeycomb Cooperative: Campaign and Expansion.
  • Old Shell Union: Platform and Coalition.
  • Foxglove League: Campaign and Platform.
  • Riverworks Party: Expansion and Platform.
  • Many Wings Coalition: Expansion and Coalition.
  • Night Parliament: Campaign and Coalition.

Replacement

The pairings remain, but the icons now identify favored operations and their party bonuses rather than restricting where operatives may execute.
