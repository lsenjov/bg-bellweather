Superseded

Former specification

The A3 district map reduced its district diagram to 76% scale to make room for a right-side 3 × 5 campaign grid and a bottom two-row score track from 0 through 20. Its approximately 26 mm spaces held the shared 22 mm round marker and 25 mm firm score markers.

Replacement

The campaign and score trackers now share a separate A4 landscape sheet. Its 27 mm spaces preserve the 3 × 5 campaign sequence while expanding scores to 0–40 in a 7 × 6 snake. The A3 district diagram returns to 88% scale without changing its topology, labels, capacities, or Support spots.
