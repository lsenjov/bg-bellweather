Index/Board

The physical table has a fixed Support map, a shared enacted-laws display and Year tracker, and six party areas. There is no shared auction or persistent party-order track.

Decided

Party areas

Each party area must show its identity, three blank card-sized Bonus spaces, a fixed public issue ranking, annual Firm opening position, and public Operation pile. The party’s three Bonus cards occupy all three spaces. A Firm marker on a party identifies who receives that party’s pile when it closes; it is not a bid. Its presence also means the party is open.

  • The pile must hold and expose the families of played Operation cards.
  • A Bonus card’s presence in any blank space visibly shows that it is available at its home party.
  • Returning a Firm marker closes its party. No separate Closed state or marker is required.
  • Quiet Hours needs no delayed marker; its chosen empty district is resolved immediately after Rally.

The printable party workspaces place an exact card-sized Firm opening and public Operation pile beside three blank, card-sized Bonus-card spaces; its fixed issue ranking determines its vote on every policy.

Decided

District map

The physical continuous-land map has fifteen districts, 51 Support spaces and 28 adjacencies, all across shared borders. Urban holds 18 Support (9 votes), Industrial Belt 14 (7 votes), and Outlying 16 (8 votes); Bellweather Centre keeps its 3 Support and votes without thinning. Shared borders define adjacency for every effect, including Smear. There are no rivers, lakes or bridges. Point contacts do not count.

Industrial Belt has a capacity-six industrial core (Northgate) beside a capacity-four suburb (Canal Ward), with capacity-two Crown Road and Meadow on the Urban and Outlying approaches. Northgate also borders Crown Road on the continuous-land map. Orchard is retired. Outlying has one capacity-four town (Eastfield) and six capacity-two villages, including Coast.

The outer regions have 48 spaces and retain at most 24 Support. A full outer map removes 24 at an Election; partially occupied districts remove fewer. The tighter space budget deliberately rewards movement before late-cycle crowding. Urban setup, Centre capacity and Centre retention are unchanged. Decided

Policy display

Place one face-up policy beside each of the four region labels. Use a separate shared display for enacted laws, with a draw deck and failed-policy discard. Enacted cards need no region-of-origin marker. Keep all law text visible; up to eight laws affect ongoing play.

Decided

Year register

The landscape A3 map has a six-space round/year tracker along its bottom edge. Each approximately 32.5 × 32.5 mm space fits the existing 22 mm Year marker. Advance numerically from 1 through 6; Elections are marked after Years 2, 4 and 6.

Midterms are thematic boundaries with no additional procedure. After Years 2, 4, and 6, complete Cleanup and then resolve the corresponding Election. The separate A4 public ledger now contains only the final score ledger, with space for negative totals.

Superseded

Retired Pecking Order

The mandatory Pecking Order auction, six movable party-order tokens, adjacent swaps, and special low-to-high Operation resolution are retired. Their former role is summarized in the component archive; the current Early Bird, Year and Firm pieces are on the shared state sheet.

Decided

Print layout

Print the default map SVG or A3 map PDF at 100% on landscape A3 (420 × 297 mm). The map is continuous land, including Bellweather Centre. Support circles are approximately 6.4 mm across; final physical Support-token size remains an open fit question. Keep party boards beside the map. The replaced rules and components remain archived.
