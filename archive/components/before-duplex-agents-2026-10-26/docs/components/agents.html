Agents · fold and glue

Print one A4 portrait sheet single-sided at 100%. Each adjacent pair folds to one 25.4 × 25.4 mm piece. Cut each pair as a rectangle, score the central dashed line, fold and glue blank backs together. Fifteen pieces use standee bases with the clip area at the bottom; six portraits lie flat and flip between a figure awake and an empty frame asleep.
