Common Operation cards

The shared supply contains eighteen Organise, twenty-four Rally and twelve Smear cards. All common Operations share one back. Apply agent permissions, party access and laws.

54 cards. Print A4 portrait at 100%, duplex, flip on the long edge. Each front page is followed by its horizontally mirrored back page. Cut the 40 × 61 mm grid after printing.

Historical source layout; the replaced PDF is not retained.
