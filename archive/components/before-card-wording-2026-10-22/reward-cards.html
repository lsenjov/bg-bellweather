Hecate reward cards

Six separate reward cards: two of each Operation. Start face up in Hecate’s reward supply, not in hand. Distinct backs separate these cards from common and firm cards.

6 cards. Print A4 portrait at 100%, duplex, flip on the long edge. Each front page is followed by its horizontally mirrored back page. Cut the 40 × 61 mm grid after printing.

Historical source layout; the replaced PDF is not retained.
