Ruleset 23 Bonus cards

Print these two front-only A4 portrait sheets at actual size. Each of the eighteen unique 40 × 61 mm cards begins over one of its home party’s three blank card spaces, may enter a player’s private hand through Collect or Closure, and returns home immediately after successful play.

Historical HTML source; current print exports use Ruleset 24.
