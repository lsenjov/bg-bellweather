Former specification

The six boards showed these operation pairs and panels: Honeycomb Organise/Rally with Leave a Cell Behind and Swarm; Old Shell Smear/Court with Stonewall and Binding Pact; Foxglove Organise/Smear with Slip Away and Spin; Riverworks Rally/Smear with Public Works and Undermine; Many Wings Organise/Court with Murmuration and Local Chapters; and Night Parliament Rally/Court with Closing Argument and After-Hours Deal.

Night Parliament had two delayed-claim reminders and both panels instructed players to mark the claiming bid until its extra baseline resolved.

Replacement

The ruleset-13 boards use the theme-first operation pairs and power names. Night Parliament retains one delayed marker for Night Shift; Midnight Leak resolves immediately.
