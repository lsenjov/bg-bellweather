Superseded

Former specification

Six Firm kits occupied three A4 landscape sheets, two kits per page. Each kit used a large identity panel, one 40 × 61 mm annual opening marker, two 20 mm Collection counters, and one 25 mm score marker.

The matching player folio originally printed four 12 mm Collection wells, so the supplied counters could not occupy their intended storage positions.

Replacement

The identity panels were removed because the opening and score pieces already carry each Firm’s number, emblem, and stationery treatment. All six opening markers, twelve standardized 14 mm Collection counters, six score markers, and the shared public state now fit on one A4 landscape sheet. Player-folio wells were enlarged to the same 14 mm size.
