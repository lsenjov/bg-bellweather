Superseded

Former specification

Each 287 × 100 mm party workspace used a 52 mm identity column, two 58 mm columns each centering one blank 40 × 61 mm Bonus-card space, and a final flexible column holding the exact 40 × 61 mm Firm opening and Operation pile wells. The Court and Coalition Target tracker spanned the three non-identity columns beneath them.

Replacement

The current party workspaces retain the same outer dimensions and every existing component. Three exact 40 mm columns now hold blank Bonus-card spaces, while the final flexible column remains wide enough for the two existing card wells. The current two Bonus cards occupy any two spaces and the third remains empty.
