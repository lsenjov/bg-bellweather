Numbered Pecking Order tokens

Superseded party-token identity treatment retained as design history.

Index/Archived component
Superseded

Former specification

Each movable 44 mm party token repeated its party’s fixed number alongside its colour, emblem, and short name. The number appeared in a small circle near the token’s upper-left edge.

Replacement

Party numbers were removed from the movable tokens because they could be mistaken for current Pecking Order ranks. Numbers now identify only the six fixed board positions, while colour, emblem, and short name identify each party token.
