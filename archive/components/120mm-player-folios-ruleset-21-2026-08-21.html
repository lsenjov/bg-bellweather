Archived component

Previous format

Each 200 × 120 mm folio carried one Firm identity, three overlapping 40 × 61 mm Election agenda wells, one exact New Year well, four Collection-counter wells, final-card-count copy, and a four-action Lobby reference. Two folios fit on each A4 portrait sheet, so the six-player supply used three pages.

The agenda wells appeared in Election 1, 2, 3 order from back to front, leaving Election 3 visually uppermost before play. The identity panel included an emblem, small Firm index, name, motto, oversized number, and final-card reminder.

Replacement

Ruleset 22

The folio is 200 × 90 mm, three per A4 page, and retains only emblem, name, motto, agenda, New Year, and Collection areas. A separate one-third-A4 player helper carries the rules. Election 3 sits at the bottom of the agenda stack, then Election 2, with the next Election 1 agenda on top.
