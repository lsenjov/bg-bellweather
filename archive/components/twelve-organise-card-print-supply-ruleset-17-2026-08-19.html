Former specification

The Ruleset 17 six-player supply contained twelve Organise, twenty-four Rally, twelve Smear, and twelve Court cards. Its sixty cards occupied four 4 × 4 A4 sheets with four blank positions.

Replacement

Ruleset 18 supplies eighteen Organise cards and retains the other family quantities, producing sixty-six cards across five A4 sheets. The same supply supports six standard hands or three doubled low-player hands.
