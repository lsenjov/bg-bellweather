Superseded

Former specification

The Press Perch needed to list all six parties in a persistent Pecking Order, show the direction of party-contest resolution, remain visibly distinct from the parties, and provide an optional contest target. Its dimensions, order markers, bid area, and printable format remained open.

The earlier board inventory reserved space for a Press Perch somewhere beside the district map and three party-board sheets but did not provide printable artwork or dedicated party-order tokens.

Replacement

The current component is called the Pecking Order. It uses one A4 landscape board and a second A4 landscape sheet containing six movable party-order tokens. The board has no opening-bid position because its auction exists automatically and accepts only ordinary counterbids.
