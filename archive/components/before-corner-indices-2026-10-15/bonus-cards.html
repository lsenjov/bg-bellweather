Party cards

Eighteen unique party cards, with one shared back per home party. Party cards bypass normal agent-location requirements; the Gallery uses any awake portrait and rotates it after resolution.

18 cards. Print A4 portrait at 100%, duplex, flip on the long edge. Each front page is followed by its horizontally mirrored back page. Cut the 40 × 61 mm grid after printing.
