Firm cards

The complete 31-card personal supply. Prophecies are placed publicly; all other firm cards normally go to New Year. See the Hecate reference for placement restrictions and the standard success reward.

31 cards. Print A4 portrait at 100%, duplex, flip on the long edge. Each front page is followed by its horizontally mirrored back page. Cut the 40 × 61 mm grid after printing.
