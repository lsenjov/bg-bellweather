Lobbying firm kits

Player identity, annual access, and private card areas.

Index/Lobbying firm kits

At four to six players, each human controls one Firm kit. At two or three, each controls two Firm kits but keeps one hand, one public/private sheet pair, one shield, four yes reminder markers, and one score.

Decided

Firm identities

  1. One Fell Swoop Public Affairs
  2. Pairliament Partners
  3. TriumviRAT Advisory
  4. IVy League Public Affairs
  5. Vested Interests
  6. VI.P. Access Group
Required kit

Per Firm

  • Three identical generic Firm cards are printed. Use one as the annual party-access marker; the other two are spares. Return the active marker when its party closes.
  • Two Collection cubes; printed counters are not supplied.

The three generic copies identify the same Firm; use only one for that Firm’s party access. A Firm never owns a hand or point balance separately from its human player.

Player supply

Per human player

  • One private hand for ordinary and held Bonus cards, plus one covered New Year area. Only each area’s total card count is public.
  • One 100 × 90 mm public sheet, one 100 × 90 mm private sheet, one standing Firm shield and four yes reminder markers. These quantities do not double at 2–3 players; choose one controlled Firm’s sheet pair and shield.
  • Two Collection cubes, or four at two or three players.
  • Standard hand: 3 Organise, 4 Rally, 2 Smear (9 cards). Low-player hand: 6, 8, and 4 (18 cards).
  • One secret issue scoring card per human, held throughout the game, including at 2–3 players.
  • One final score: enacted policy gains and losses plus the final hand-rank bonus.
  • At Election 3, one final card count combining every ordinary and held Bonus card after Year 6 Cleanup. A low-player player ranks this human hand once, not each Firm separately.
Decided

Physical layout

The printable player sheets split each former 200 × 90 mm folio into two separate 100 × 90 mm pieces. The public sheet has an exact 40 × 61 mm well for the covered New Year pile and four 14 mm Collection wells; wells 3 and 4 retain their 2–3-player labels. Keep this sheet and returned Firm markers fully visible. New Year card faces remain hidden, with their total count public.

The private sheet has one exact 40 × 61 mm Private Priorities well and a 2 × 2 grid of four blank 18 × 18 mm spaces. The spaces have no labels, region names or printed no-vote icons. Each human receives four 16 × 16 mm yes markers, usable freely as entirely private reminders of desired regional outcomes. Empty spaces have no fixed meaning. Markers have no effect on actual votes, actions or scoring; there is no required placement or reset.

The standing Firm shield hides the private sheet and unused reminder markers. Its illustrated office frontage carries the Firm’s name, emblem and motto; the reverse is pale stationery with a small emblem. One opaque 280 × 110 mm card folds at 80 mm and 200 mm to make a 120 mm front and two 80 mm wings, standing 110 mm tall. Keep the top and player’s side open and place the private sheet’s top edge nearest the front. The public sheet sits beside the shield. The shield is sized for Private Priorities and reminders; the player still conceals their hand’s faces while keeping its total count public.

The six sheet pairs fit on two A4 portrait pages, three pairs per page; cut along the centre border to separate each pair. The 24 yes markers use one A4 portrait page. The six shields use twelve A4 landscape pages, alternating exterior/interior for duplex printing with short-edge binding. Print one pair first to check alignment, then score from the exterior guides before cutting. Full assembly instructions are on the shield sheet. The player helper carries the rules reminders.

Open prototype check: test shield stability and privacy from every seated opponent with the chosen cardstock. The 110 mm height and 80 mm wings may need adjustment after a physical test.

The printable state and player piece sheet provides three identical generic cards per Firm, plus Early Bird and calendar Year markers. Use one card per controlled Firm; extra copies do not change the opening allowance. Supply Collection cubes separately. Scores are written on the final ledger, without score markers.
