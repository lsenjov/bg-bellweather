Lobbying-house terminology

Superseded player-organization naming retained as design history.

Index/Archived component
Superseded

Former specification

Players controlled lobbying houses. Boards and identity pieces used “House 01” through “House 06” as generic kit labels, and each counterbid-cover face repeated its 70 mm manufacturing diameter beside the house identifier.

Replacement

Player organizations are now lobbying firms, shortened to firms on components. Their names, numbers, and emblems remain unchanged. Counterbid-cover faces identify their firm but no longer print the manufacturing diameter, which remains in the component instructions.
