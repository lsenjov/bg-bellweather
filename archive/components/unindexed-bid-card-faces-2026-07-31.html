Superseded

Former specification

Transferable card fronts relied on the full family name, central glyph, tint, and optional pattern. They had no playing-card indices, so the family name was not visible from both corners or when cards were tightly fanned.

Replacement

Leverage, Bluff, Organise, Rally, Smear, and Coalition use the unique initials L, B, O, R, S, and C. Each front prints its initial upright in the upper-left and rotated 180 degrees in the lower-right. Full names, effects, central glyphs, colour families, shared backs, dimensions, and quantities remain unchanged.
