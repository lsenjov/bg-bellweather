Forty-two-token operation supply

Superseded component format retained as design history.

Index/Archived component
Superseded

Former specification

Each player received two Organise, two Rally, two Smear, and one Court token. A six-player sheet therefore contained forty-two tokens: twelve of each ordinary family and six Court.

The four families used saturated brown, green, red, and blue fields with light text, plus distinct names, glyphs, and patterns.

Replacement

The supply doubled to four Organise, four Rally, four Smear, and two Court per player. Eighty-four neutral tokens fill all but four positions of the A4 zero-gutter grid. Pastel stationery colours now align them with the lobbying-house palette.
