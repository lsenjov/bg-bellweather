Superseded

Former specification

Each standard player began with two Organise, two Rally, two Smear, and one Court card. Players at two or three received twice that supply. The six-player print file therefore contained twelve cards of each ordinary operation and six Court cards.

Replacement

Each standard player now begins with four Rally cards while Organise, Smear, and Court remain at two, two, and one. The doubled low-player supply contains eight Rally cards. The six-player print file supplies twenty-four Rally cards so board growth can outpace direct removal.
