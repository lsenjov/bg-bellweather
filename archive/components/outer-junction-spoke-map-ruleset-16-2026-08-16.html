Former topology

The map contained sixteen districts, fifty-seven Support spaces, and eighteen connections. Twelve districts formed the unchanged outer ring. Each capacity-two spoke joined Bellweather Centre to a capacity-two outer junction:

  • Crown Road connected Northreach to Bellweather Centre.
  • Canal Ward connected Reedwater to Bellweather Centre.
  • Old Quarter connected Westgate to Bellweather Centre.

Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood were capacity-six districts with only their two outer-ring neighbors. Northreach, Reedwater, and Westgate each had their two ring neighbors plus one spoke.

Replacement

Ruleset 17 preserves the outer ring, district names, capacities, connection count, and Support-space count, but connects the three spokes directly to Harbormouth, Grand Market, and Ironwood. The capacity-two outer junctions lose their spoke connections.
