Superseded

Former specification

Each party board had a neutral setup position and five target-coloured positions used only by its Coalition Target token. The Coalition card redirected that token immediately. Old Shell also showed a Shell marker position for Binding Pact.

The operation card was titled Coalition and used C corner indices, a redirect glyph, and direct-redirection rules text.

Replacement

The five prospective-partner positions are now Court spaces that hold normal party Support. The existing source-coloured Coalition Target token marks the unique Court Support leader and remains where it is during a tie and after Election Day cleanup. Old Shell no longer needs a Shell marker.

The operation card is titled Court, retains the unique C index and card quantity, and instructs the player to place Support on a Court space.
