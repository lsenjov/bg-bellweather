Eighty-four-token operation supply

Superseded component specification retained as design history.

Index/Archived component
Superseded

Former specification

Every player received four Organise, four Rally, four Smear, and two Court tokens at every player count. A six-player sheet therefore contained eighty-four tokens: twenty-four of each ordinary family and twelve Court.

The 25 mm tokens used pastel stationery colours and filled all but four positions of an eight-by-eleven zero-gutter A4 grid.

Replacement

At four to six players, each player now begins with two Organise, two Rally, two Smear, and one Court. At two or three players, that supply doubles. The largest required shared supply is therefore forty-two tokens: twelve of each ordinary family and six Court.
