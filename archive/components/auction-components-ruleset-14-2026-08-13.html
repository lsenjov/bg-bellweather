Ruleset 14 auction components

Superseded physical specifications retained as component history.

Index/Archived components
Superseded

Transferable bid cards

The six-player supply contained sixty Leverage, twenty-four Bluff, twelve Organise, twenty-four Rally, twelve Smear, and twelve Court cards. All 144 fronts used 40 × 61 mm cards in opaque sleeves. Leverage and Bluff existed only to construct and obscure auction stacks.

Firm identity and bid pieces

Each Firm kit contained three interchangeable 40 × 61 mm identity cards. One identified an opening bid; two covered counterbid stacks. Earlier versions used a 50 mm opening marker and two 70 mm counterbid covers. Firm boards reminded players to conceal uncommitted bid cards behind a screen.

Pecking Order

A printable board held six 44 mm party tokens in a persistent media order, plus the Early Bird marker and Coalition Target tokens. Operation cards committed to its automatic auction swapped adjacent party tokens rather than resolving their usual baselines.

Party and campaign boards

Each party board contained an opening-bid area and two bonus Ready circles. Night Parliament also required a delayed-bonus marker. The campaign sheet labelled twelve Campaign Rounds and three Election Days.

Replacement

Ruleset 15 retains only Operation cards and scoring cards. Each Firm now needs one yearly opening marker, each player needs two Collection counters, party areas hold public Operation piles and an open/closed state, and each player needs a visible New Year area. Low-player games double Firms, Operation cards, and Collection counters.

Earlier archived foundations

The physical auction system followed an open Press Perch component proposal and the earlier party-specific Operation icon specification.
