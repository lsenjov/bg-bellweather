Former specification

The first printable operation-token sheet separated every 25 mm square with a 3 mm gutter. Six rows of seven tokens occupied a 193 × 165 mm grid centered on A4.

Replacement

The 3 mm gutters were removed. Adjacent tokens now share cut lines while the six-player supply and every token’s 25 mm finished size remain unchanged.
