Superseded

Former specification

The physical prototype used Clout coins or cubes, forty-two neutral 25 mm operation tokens, and twelve 70 mm roundels mounted on opaque cups or domes. Firm boards devoted panels to issued pieces and the six-step Campaign Round. Party boards used uncoloured overture labels and opening areas that instructed players to place a container. The Pecking Order occupied two A4 landscape sheets: one full board with three procedure panels and one token sheet. The district map printed a capacity label inside every district and had no round or score tracker.

Replacement

Playtest feedback moved the transferable bid economy to sleeved 40 × 61 mm cards. Standard players now receive ten one-Clout cards, four inert Bluff cards, and seven operation cards. Firm-specific Counterbid Cover cards top face-down counterbid stacks. Opening Clout remains face up while opening Bluff and operation cards remain face down.

Party overtures now use one source-party token on target-coloured positions. Firm boards use an exact-size scoring-card well and compact party-power reminders. The Pecking Order combines its board, six party tokens, Early Bird marker, and round marker on one sheet. The map omits redundant capacity labels and carries the round and 0–20 score tracks.
