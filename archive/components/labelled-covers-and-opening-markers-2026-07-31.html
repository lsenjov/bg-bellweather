Superseded

Former specification

Each lobbying firm received two 40 × 61 mm Counterbid Cover cards explicitly labelled “Counterbid Cover” and A or B, plus one separate 50 mm circular opening-bid marker. The large opening marker repeated the firm number at opposite edges, while the smaller Cover-card emblems shared space with their functional labels.

Replacement

Each firm now receives three interchangeable 40 × 61 mm identity cards. Any card may identify the firm’s opening bid or cover one of its two counterbid stacks. The cards omit functional and A/B labels so their enlarged emblems remain readable from across the table. The 25 mm score marker remains a separate component.
