Former specification

The A4 public tracker used three columns of five spaces: Years 1–4 and Election 1, Years 5–8 and Election 2, then Years 9–12 and Election 3.

Replacement

Ruleset 19 retains three columns but reduces each to two year spaces and one Election space: Y1/Y2/E1, Y3/Y4/E2, and Y5/Y6/E3. The printed guidance identifies each odd-year boundary as a thematic midterm and preserves Cleanup before every Election.
