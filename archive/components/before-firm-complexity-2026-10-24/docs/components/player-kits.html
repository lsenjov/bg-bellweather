Emblems and colour

First Chair: a fiddle resting against an office chair. Second Opinion: two identical speech bubbles. Third Party: three hands surrounding a coin. Four Familiar Faces: four panes with hat, feather, cat’s eye and toad’s foot. Five Strings: a control bar with five hanging strings. Six Figure: a vertical stroke through a 6, inside a portrait frame.

Use muted, distinct firm palettes, adjusted to these identities, with firm number and emblem as redundant identifiers. Saturated party colours remain reserved for party identity and Support. Exact prototype colours live with the shared print identity definitions.

Player area

Each human has one public/private sheet pair, one privacy shield, a single-sided A5 landscape firm reference and a double-sided A5 portrait shared reference (print two per A4 landscape sheet, duplex with short-edge binding, then cut apart). Shields carry identity only; rules are separate so future revisions do not require shield reprints.

The 100 × 90 mm public sheet retains a covered New Year area and four Collection wells, with wells 3–4 for low player counts. The 100 × 90 mm private sheet retains the sideways 61 × 40 mm priority-card well and four blank 36 × 20 mm reminder spaces. Give each human four reversible ✓/✕ vote markers and supply your own generic counters for private reminders. No extra reward-supply board, mirroring token or prophecy-number markers are required.

Assembly

Agent pairs fold and glue into 25.4 × 25.4 mm squares. Firms 1–5 need fifteen standee bases in a full game; leave the bottom clip area clear. Gallery portraits lie flat and flip: a figure means awake, and an empty picture frame means asleep. The asleep face has a blank interior, with no figure, firm number or state label; the awake face is unchanged. Small tokens and opening markers print single-layer. See each printable sheet for cut, fold and orientation guides.

Keep the existing tapered shield geometry: 280 × 110 mm overall, folds at 80 and 200 mm, 120 × 110 mm centre and 80 mm wings with 70 mm outer edges. The reverse stays unprinted. Print references and shields at actual size.
