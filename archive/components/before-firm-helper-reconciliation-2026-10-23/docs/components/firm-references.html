Firm references

Six single-sided A5 landscape references. Print one per firm at actual size; keep beside the shield.

1

First Chair Consulting

The Fiddler · “We set the tone.”

Setup

Place the Fiddler in any district first. Put three favours on different parties. Two Just Passing Through and two I Can Wait cards start in hand.

The Grand Tour

Make the first Party Opening. Your primary opening must use a different party each year; mark it with an open-door token. Your second opening at 2–3 players is unrestricted.

One More Thing

Cards played in a turn must have different names. If your third card is common or movement, optionally resolve it twice. Check legality again for the repeat; use the same party. Laws trigger once per card.

Favours Owed

A third-slot common or party card places one favour on its party, maximum one per party. After openings each year, spend or discard all favours in any order: move one step, or perform one law-modified common Operation for that party through your agent. Closed-party favours buy movement only. No crescendo or new favours from these actions. Year 6 favours have no later payout.

Personal kit does not double at low player counts · Firm cards → New Year unless placed · Agents persist across years
2

Second Opinion Advisory

The Doppelgängers · “We couldn’t agree more.”

Setup & movement

Place two interchangeable agents in distinct districts. Four Parallel Paths cards each move one agent to a neighbouring district, then optionally the other to a neighbouring district. Choose either first and move sequentially; neither may enter the other’s current district. Neighbouring agents are allowed. Other players’ agents never restrict movement or mirroring.

Mirror the pile

Only your first common card on each acting party each turn can mirror. Before resolving it, note the top common card already in that party’s pile. Choose one agent to resolve the played Operation, then optionally use the other for the noted Operation with the same acting party. Check legality and choose parameters when each resolves; each Smear may choose its own legal rival.

Resolve sequentially

Each agent performs at most one of the two Operations. Both require normal positioning and obey the acting party’s laws. Include voluntary Organise transport; keep agents in distinct districts. Laws trigger at most once for the whole played card; choose an eligible resolution. The played card uses one slot and enters the pile once after resolution, covering the copied card, which stays in the pile.

One opportunity per party

Empty piles and declined or illegal copies still spend the opportunity. Later common cards on that party use one agent normally. Opportunities reset at your next turn; three parties can each provide a mirror. Copy only the Operation: no extra card or slot, no card play, no further mirror and no prophecy card-play credit. Party and firm cards neither spend opportunities nor mirror. Party cards resolve once and bypass agents. No declaration or isolation applies.

Personal kit does not double at low player counts · Firm cards → New Year unless placed · Agents persist across years

Third Party Interests

Hecate · “We knew you’d agree.”

Setup & supply

Place three sisters anywhere, sharing allowed. Start with three movement cards and fourteen prophecies in hand. Six reward cards—two of each Operation—begin in a separate face-up supply. All sisters authorise every common Operation.

Place prophecies

Only as your third card, following two non-prophecy cards. No fulfilment on a turn when you place one; you cannot place one if you already fulfilled one that turn. Remote and public; maximum one on each region, district, party or firm. Withdraw to New Year for free at any time; no automatic expiry.

Success

Choose an available reward card into hand for a subsequent turn; lose the card reward if supply is empty. Independently, optionally move a sister one step. Successful Election prophecies repeat this entire reward twice, including both optional moves. Resolve after the triggering card. Reward Operations require agents and open parties; return to supply after use.

Fourteen prophecies

Five Election margins: place only in Years 1/3/5. Three pile sizes: next collection or Closure; your own collection fails. Three firm predictions: matching common card anywhere in its next common-card turn. Three district-entry traps: at most one placed trap per region, excluding Bellwether Centre. Election prophecies have a separate limit. Another player’s agent enters; owner sends a common card to New Year if able. See cards for full conditions.

Personal kit does not double at low player counts · Firm cards → New Year unless placed · Agents persist across years

Four Familiar Faces

The Witch · “We know someone.”

Setup

Place witch, crow, cat and toad together in one district. Start with Crow, Cat and Toad teleport cards.

Specialised familiars

Crow authorises Organise; cat Rally; toad Smear. The witch cannot authorise common Operations. Immediately before its assigned Operation, the familiar may move one neighbouring district. It must then perform a legal Operation: no movement-only common cards.

Three teleport cards

Each named card teleports either that familiar to the witch or the witch to that familiar, anywhere on the map. Before or after teleporting, you may move the named familiar to a neighbouring district once, whichever agent teleports. The teleport is required. Then send the card to New Year.

Support & party cards

Any of your agents may voluntarily accompany Organise from its source. Party cards bypass positioning and grant no free familiar step. Law extras require no additional familiar presence.

Five Strings Attached

The Puppeteer · “Every move has a reason.”

Setup

Choose the puppeteer’s start, then move four times one neighbouring district, leaving a marionette in each departure district. Revisits and sharing are allowed. Three movement cards start in hand.

Only marionettes operate

Each of the four marionettes authorises every common Operation. The puppeteer itself cannot. Neither leader nor marionettes accompanies Organise.

Mandatory cycle

After the whole common card resolves, including laws: retrieve the used marionette, move the puppeteer exactly one neighbouring district, and leave that marionette in the departure district. Once per common card.

Personal movement

Retrieve any marionette, move the puppeteer one neighbouring district, then leave it behind. No Operation or Support required. Party cards do not trigger the cycle. Sharing is unrestricted.

Six Figure Consulting

The Haunted Gallery · “We can put you in the picture.”

Setup

Place six portraits in six distinct districts. Choose three awake. Always three awake and three asleep. Two Overnight Shipping cards start in hand.

Perform, then rotate

An awake portrait authorises common Operations normally. Party cards may use any awake portrait, regardless of effect location. After the entire common or party card resolves, the used portrait sleeps and one that was asleep before the card wakes.

A network of openings

Awake portrait districts are adjacent for all effects. Your firm uses this freely. Another player pays you one common or party card from hand before using the connection. One toll per played card; separate effects pay separately. No payment means no access. Geographic neighbours remain free.

Overnight Shipping

Move any one portrait anywhere, preserving awake/asleep state. Portraits never accompany Organise. Later sharing is allowed.
