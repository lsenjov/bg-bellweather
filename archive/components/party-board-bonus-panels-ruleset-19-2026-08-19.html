Superseded

Ruleset 19 component

Every party workspace printed two full-text bonus panels. Each panel included a coverable Ready circle, and the shared-state sheet supplied two matching bonus-used markers per party, twelve total. Placing a marker over Ready showed that the effect was unavailable until Cleanup.

Replacement

Ruleset 20 gives each party two exact 40 × 61 mm homes for unique collectible Bonus cards. Card presence now communicates availability, so the Ready circles and bonus-used markers are unnecessary. See the current party workspaces and Bonus-card sheet.
