Superseded

Former specification

Reusable cards represented Organise, Rally, Smear, and Court. Players concealed uncommitted cards, committed them physically to bids, revealed them together, executed their operation families, and transferred them intact through the Revolving Door.

The family icon was intended to remain visible in a fanned hand. Exact per-player card quantities were unresolved.

Replacement

Neutral 25 mm square operation tokens replaced the cards. Each player now contributes two Organise, two Rally, two Smear, and one Court token to the closed economy. The rules behavior is unchanged.
