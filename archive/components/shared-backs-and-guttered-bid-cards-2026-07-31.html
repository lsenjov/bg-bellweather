Superseded printable layout retained as design history.

Superseded

Former specification

The 138 transferable bid cards printed across eighteen A4 portrait pages: nine 4 × 4 front sheets alternated with nine mirrored sheets carrying a shared navy back. A 2 mm gutter separated adjacent 40 × 61 mm cards.

Replacement

The printable supply contains nine front-only sheets. Their 4 × 4 grids have no gutters, so adjacent cards share cut lines. Each face remains 40 × 61 mm and requires an opaque 41 × 63 mm sleeve for concealed play.
