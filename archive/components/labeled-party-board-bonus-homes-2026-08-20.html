Superseded

Former specification

Each party workspace had two 40 × 61 mm Bonus-card homes. Every home printed “Bonus card home,” its card’s title, and “Return card here after play.” In board order, Honeycomb printed Waggle Route then Common Cause; Old Shell printed Dig In then Stonewall; Foxglove printed Spin then Whisper Network; Riverworks printed Canal Network then Public Works; Many Wings printed Scatter the Flock then Joint Campaign; and Night Parliament printed Quiet Hours then Midnight Leak.

Replacement

Each party workspace retains two card-sized, party-coloured spaces without visible wording. Party identity, operation family, title, and rules remain on each Bonus card, allowing cards to change without requiring a new party-board print.
