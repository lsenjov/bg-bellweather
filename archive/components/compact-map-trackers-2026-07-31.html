Compact map trackers

Superseded campaign and score layouts retained as design history.

Index/Archived components
Superseded

Former specification

The map placed all twelve Campaign Rounds and three Election Days in one narrow horizontal strip. A second single-row strip held scores 0 through 20. Those compact spaces showed sequence but could not contain the shared round marker or the firms’ 25 mm score markers.

Replacement

The campaign tracker now occupies a right-side 3 × 5 grid, with one complete four-round-and-election cycle in each column. The score tracker uses a two-row 0–20 snake with approximately 26 mm square spaces. The district diagram is scaled and repositioned around those trackers without changing its Support spots or connections.
