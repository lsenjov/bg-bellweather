Superseded

Former specification

One A4 landscape sheet placed a 0–40 score snake beside a three-column campaign register. Each campaign column held two Year spaces and a separate Election space, and every square measured 27 mm to fit the existing public markers.

Replacement

The current A3 map carries six 27 mm Year spaces in its lower-right corner. Odd years sit above a Midterms divider and even years above an Elections divider. The A4 public ledger retains the unchanged centred 0–40 score snake.
