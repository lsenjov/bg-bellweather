Superseded

Former board specification

Each lobbying firm used one 200 × 140 mm board, with two boards on each A4 portrait sheet. A single exact-size 40 × 61 mm agenda well held one face-down scoring card at four to six players or one stacked compatible pair at two or three players.

The well was labelled “Sealed agenda” and “Scoring card(s),” with the quantity reminder “1 normally / 2 at 2–3p.” The identity panel instructed players to keep their Leverage, Bluff, uncommitted operation cards, and agenda behind one player screen.

The remaining board area contained the four operation baselines and shorthand for both powers of all six parties.

Replacement

Ruleset 14 uses a compact 200 × 120 mm board with three ordered, overlapping 40 × 61 mm agenda wells. Each Election position holds one card or a stacked compatible low-player pair.
