Policy prototype state and player pieces

Print all four A4 landscape pages at actual size, duplex with short-edge binding, on opaque card. This produces two physical sheets. Pages 1–2 hold the Firm cards and public pieces with a blank back. Pages 3–4 hold 24 reversible ✓/✕ vote reminders and 24 reversible 1–2/3+ range reminders.

Print a plain-paper test first to check front/back alignment. Keep page 2 even though it is blank, so the marker fronts and backs stay paired. Back-side block positions are swapped for registration; the text and symbols read normally. Cut the marker blocks along the front grid lines. For a one-sided printer, print page 1 once and mount pages 3 and 4 back-to-back with their top edges aligned.

Give each human four markers of each type: four pairs in total, including at 2–3 players. Each marker is 16 × 16 mm. Use and flip either type freely in the four blank pair spaces on the private sheet; they are entirely private reminders with no effect on votes, actions or scoring. No face or number range has a prescribed game meaning.

Use one Firm card per human at four to six players, or one from each of two different Firms at two or three players. Extra copies are spares, not additional party access. Use two Collection cubes per human, or four at two or three players. The 24 mm Early Bird and 22 mm Year marker retain their existing roles; record scores on the final ledger.
